# Plan 128: League edit page revamp

Implements `docs/specs/128-league-edit-revamp.md` (approved 2026-10-31). UI only: no backend, no migration. Branch `feat/097-098-import-framework-and-schedule-import` (uncommitted work continues). One `frontend-builder`, sequential; `nvm use 22.12.0`; vitest folders alone, one at a time.

## Context
The edit page (`ui/src/pages/manage/LeagueFormPage.tsx`) is the oldest-looking league screen: a Season control on every tab, a loose "Use first and last match" label beside a big Save button, loose gaps on Details, Branding and social buried in Details, a tall Playing conditions form with misaligned switches, and Teams and Schedule tabs that now duplicate the league page. The owner approved the spec, the removal of the Teams and Schedule tabs, and the Season pill placement.

## What exists (from the code read)
- `leagueEdit/leagueEditTabConfig.ts`: `LEAGUE_EDIT_TABS` (details, teams, schedule, conditions "Playing conditions", contacts), `resolveLeagueEditTab`, `editTabForView` (schedule, teams, conditions views map to same-named edit tabs; used by the league page's edit links).
- `LeagueFormPage.tsx`: `SEASON_TABS = ['teams','schedule','conditions']` drives the header `HeaderSeasonSelect`; Details shows `LeagueAgeReferenceLine`, then `LeagueSeasonDatesBlock` carrying its own Season select (`seasons`, `onSeasonChange` props), then `LeagueForm`; the other season tabs render `LeagueSeasonDatesBlock` above their content (conditions, teams, schedule). Footer actions (Cancel, Save, Duplicate, status toggle) only on Details. Teams/Schedule render `LeagueEditTeamsTab` (uses `onAddTeam`, `linkOpen`, `LinkExistingRecordDialog`, `linkMutation`, affiliation queries) and `LeagueEditScheduleTab`.
- `components/LeagueForm/LeagueForm.tsx`: ONE `<form id=LEAGUE_FORM_ID>` with sections Basic info (3-column grid, age cut-off group), Contact, Branding and social (`MediaUpload` logo + `SocialLinksFields`); one state and one payload.
- `leagueEdit/LeagueSeasonDatesBlock.tsx`: Start/End date inputs, ghost "Use first and last match" (with `EventAvailableOutlinedIcon`), "Save season dates", optional Season select, warning banner and range note; form `aria-label="Season dates"`.
- `leagueEdit/LeagueEditConditionsTab.tsx` and `components/PlayingConditionsForm/PlayingConditionsForm.tsx`: `ContentControlsLine` (scope + PDF upload + Share), `FormSectionHeading` sections Innings/Points/Bonus points/Notes, `fieldGridSx` 3 columns with `gap: 2`, each `CompactSwitch` in its own full-width `gridColumn: '1 / -1'` box, form `gap: 2`.
- Shared helpers to reuse: `FormSectionHeading`, `CompactSwitch`, `Input`, `compactButtonSx`, `HeaderSeasonSelect`, `viewTabs` styles via `LeagueEditTabs`, `useBackLink`/`useAfterSave` (plan 127, already in `LeagueFormPage`).

## Slices
1. **Tabs.** `leagueEditTabConfig.ts`: tabs become details, branding ("Branding"), conditions ("Playing Conditions"), contacts; `resolveLeagueEditTab` maps a stale `teams` or `schedule` value to details (unknown stays details); `editTabForView` returns null for schedule and teams (Edit League Details opens Details), conditions stays. `LeagueEditTabs` unchanged otherwise. Check the league page links that use `editTabForView` and the `?tab=` links built in `LeagueViewLayout`, `LeagueRowMenu` and `LeagueConditionsView`.
2. **Season pill and dates.** `LeagueFormPage`: `SEASON_TABS = ['details','conditions']`, so the pill shows beside the title on Details and Playing Conditions only; remove the season-scoped `LeagueSeasonDatesBlock` copy above Teams/Schedule/Conditions (Conditions no longer shows the dates block; its dates live on Details) and drop the Season select from the block (remove `seasons`/`onSeasonChange` props and the page's passing of them). Season dates row at the top of the Details card: label "Season dates for {season}", Start, End, white secondary "Use First and Last Match" and filled "Save Season Dates", both `size="compact"` and identical height (drop the ghost grey variant, icon and the loose helper line; the helper becomes the tooltip text). Keep the warning banner and range note.
3. **Remove Teams and Schedule from the edit page.** Delete `LeagueEditTeamsTab.tsx`, `LeagueEditScheduleTab.tsx`, `LeagueTeamTable`/`AffiliatedTeamRows` only if nothing else imports them (grep first; keep anything still used elsewhere), and with them their tests and stories. In `LeagueFormPage` remove `linkOpen`/`LinkExistingRecordDialog`, `linkMutation`, affiliation and team queries and `matchesQuery` use that only served those tabs (keep `matchesQuery` if the season dates block needs it for "Use First and Last Match"). Update `LeagueEditTabs.test`, `leagueEditTabConfig.test`, `LeagueFormPage.test`.
4. **LeagueForm: Details compact + Branding tab.** Add a `visibleSection?: 'details' | 'branding'` prop (default both, so Add League and existing callers are unchanged). The form stays ONE mounted `<form>` with ONE state and payload; the section not visible is hidden with `display: none` so edits on either tab survive switching and one Save posts everything. Details: Basic info then Contact in the card, fields in the 3-column grid at md (2 at sm, 1 on phone), `Input` at 36px with `gap: 1.25`, the age cut-off group becomes month and day in the same grid (no separate subtitle row), the age-reference sentence (`LeagueAgeReferenceLine`) sits directly under the age fields, the long helper moves to a tooltip or one caption line, `FormSectionHeading` kept but tightened (spacing and 24px icon; adjust `FormSectionHeading` only if needed and only through an optional prop so other forms are unchanged). Branding: logo `MediaUpload` and `SocialLinksFields` in the same card. In `LeagueFormPage`, the footer (Cancel, Save Changes, Duplicate, status toggle) shows on Details and Branding; both submit `LEAGUE_FORM_ID`.
5. **Playing Conditions compact.** `PlayingConditionsForm`: form `gap: 1.5`, `fieldGridSx` `gap: 1.25`; Innings: the three numeric fields in one row, then all switches (Limit balls per over with its Maximum balls per over field beside it, Free hit after a no ball, Allow substitutions) in a two-column grid with each `CompactSwitch` at the same 36px row height, fielding restrictions notes as a 2-row field; Points: one row of up to four (win, tie, no result, loss; forfeit win wraps); Bonus points: the Enable switch with its two fields on the same row; Notes: 3 rows. `LeagueEditConditionsTab`: the scope line, PDF upload and Share on a single `ContentControlsLine` row; "Playing conditions for 2026/2027" kept. Section dividers are the existing hairline, not large gaps. No change to fields, validation, payload or `PLAYING_CONDITIONS_FORM_ID`.
6. **Contacts.** Density pass only: hairline rows, 36px controls, one filled Add Contact on the tab toolbar if not already; nothing more (spec: beyond that is Later).
7. **Docs.** `docs/standards/page-anatomy.md` (edit-page row, section 9 review log, new dated decisions: edit page holds settings only, Season pill only on season-dependent tabs); `design-system.md` League edit section; `docs/roadmap.md` unchanged unless a new deferred item appears. Mark spec 128 status built-pending-owner-check.
