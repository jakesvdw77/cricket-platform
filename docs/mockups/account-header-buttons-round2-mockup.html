Person screen header, alternatives

The row of four outlined buttons is heavy: four equal boxes, all shouting, with Sign out as loud as Account. Five calmer ways below, each with the same four things (Account, Back to club, Switch profile, Sign out). Nothing is built.

1 · Your name, one menu (my pick)

One avatar chip with your name. Everything personal is behind it, grouped, with Sign out last and quiet. The standard pattern people already know, and the pending count sits on the chip. Back to club stays visible beside it for managers because it is a navigation, not an account action.

4 · Account and Sign out only

Two controls. Account opens the page of personal things (Invitations, Link requests, Link more players, Switch profile, Preferences). Back to club moves into the footer where it already sits ("Back to club" is there today). Sign out stays as a quiet link.
