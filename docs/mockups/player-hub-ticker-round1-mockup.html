Scoreboard ticker

A rolling line along the bottom of the Player Hub console, like the strip under a TV scoreboard. It is picked from many kinds of news, in a shuffled order, so every visit to the Hub feels different. Four ways it could move are below, all live. Hover the strip to pause it. Press Shuffle to re-roll the order. All text here is made up.

A · Classic rolling ticker

Continuous slow scroll right to left, with a type tag on every item. The most familiar and the calmest; nothing flips or flashes. Pauses on hover or touch.

B · Headline roll (one at a time)

One headline at a time, rolling up like a scoreboard flap every 6 seconds, with a counter and dots. Bigger and easier to read, good on a phone; slower to get through many items.

C · Tag plate + sliding text

A fixed plate at the left that flips to the kind of news (RESULT, LIVE, NEXT, MESSAGE, SPONSOR), with the text sliding in beside it. A sponsor gets a cream plate for its name or logo. The clearest about what it is looking at, and the best home for a sponsor.

Result

D · LED board

Amber dot-matrix on black, as on a ground's own scoreboard. The most striking and the most "game feel", but it is the loudest, and a lot of amber text is harder to read for long.

On a phone (variant B shrunk)

On a narrow screen only one short line shows at a time (B), and the tag becomes a small chip. The ticker sits above the footer bar, never over content.

What could go in it

From the app today

  • Results: "Villagers beat Rovers by 24 runs" (published results with the Show in Player Hub flag on).
  • Live now: a match in progress, with the score once the LiveScore source gives it.
  • Upcoming: "Sat 14:00 vs Rovers, Over 40, Main Oval", with a countdown inside 24 hours.
  • Poll deadlines: "Availability for Sat closes tonight".

Later and free to add

  • Messages from the club (when Updates is live).
  • Sponsors, paid by the club, with a plate and a link.
  • Milestones: a fifty, a five-for, a hat-trick, a first wicket.
  • League: "Division 1: Villagers top on 22 points".
  • Birthdays (first name only, with consent).
  • Weather for the next match; On this day from the club's history; Fun facts.

Rules to decide

  • Weighted shuffle, never the same kind twice in a row; sponsor at most 1 in 5 items.
  • Live always wins: a live match is pinned in the mix while it is on.
  • Privacy: only what the viewer's own club and held players may already see; juniors by full name as on results; no birthday without a club switch.
  • Quiet by choice: reduced motion shows static rotation; a switch in Settings; hidden on small phones if wanted.
  • Items link to the page they came from (tap a result, open it).
