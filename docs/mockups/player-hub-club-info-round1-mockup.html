Player Hub: Club info

Made-up data (Riverside Cricket Club, invented people); nothing is built. For spec 148. The page uses the Hub look the owner already knows: the pill row (About, Contacts, Links), with the logo and banner shown on About itself,, a facts card like the league page, framed icon buttons for call, email, copy and links, and friendly empty states. A pill is hidden when the club switched that block off or has nothing for it. The directory lists adults only, only those the club and the person agreed to list, and never offers Share, Print, CSV or vCard.

1 · About (the logos live here)

The banner as a wide band across the top, the logo large beside the club name (each with a small Download button only when the club allows it), then the Club details facts card (address with an Open In Maps button, phone, email, website) and the framed social icons. Values are cream; gold is only the selected pill.

Notes

Privacy: contact details appear only when the club switch and the person's own flag allow it; the club-level phone and email are separate from the directory. No export: there is no Share, Print, Download, CSV, vCard or Copy All control anywhere in the directory, and it hides itself when printed. Copy: one value at a time, with a short "Number copied" line. Copy rules: no "you" or "your", Title Case labels.
