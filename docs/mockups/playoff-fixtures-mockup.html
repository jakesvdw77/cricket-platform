Playoff and knockout fixtures (spec 155 draft, mockup)

Four pieces: the new Add Playoff Games item in the schedule's Manage menu; the Add Playoff Games dialog (pairings table, presets, then a confirm summary); how the games show in the league schedule (stage chip, italic TBC side, a Stage filter); and the Stage field on the match form with its suggestion list. A side that is not known yet is a TBC placeholder; once the pool ends it is resolved by hand through the normal Edit Match. The log counts pool matches only.
