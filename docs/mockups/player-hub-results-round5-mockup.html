Player Hub results: round 5

Drawn again from the owner's notes. The result leads (the latest match as a banner), not the player's figures. The leagues played that season, and then the teams in the chosen league, are filters above it. The player's own contribution is a slim strip at the foot of every card. Cards fill the width in a responsive grid (one column on a phone, two or three on a laptop), with no carousel, no arrows, no scrollbar. On the desktop the menu collapses to an icon rail after 10 seconds, and the grid reflows to use the space.

Desktop: wait 10 seconds

The menu collapses to the rail with a countdown line along its foot. Hover or click the rail or the round handle to bring it back; the timer starts again when the pointer leaves. Change the Season or the chips to see the grid and the filters respond.

The 10 seconds is shortened to a visible countdown line in this mockup; in the product it becomes a preference (on or off) later.
