Player Hub results: round 6

The filters no longer sit on the page all the time. One Filters button in the header (a gold dot when something is filtered) opens a bottom sheet on a phone and a side panel on a wide screen. Chosen filters show as small removable tags under the header, and only when something is filtered. A player with one league and one team gets no button at all, just a quiet line naming them. The recent form card, latest banner and card grid are unchanged from round 5 (one card fills the row).

Wide screen: side panel

Press Filters: the panel docks on the right and the grid narrows to make room (it does not cover the cards). Pick a league, then a team; remove a tag with its ✕; Reset clears both. The menu is shown collapsed to its rail, as it is after 10 seconds.

Phone: bottom sheet

The sheet's button shows how many matches the current choice gives ("Show 3 matches") and closes the sheet. On a wide screen the choices apply as you tap, and the panel has a Done button.
