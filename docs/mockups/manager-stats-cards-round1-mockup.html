What changes, in one list

Replaces: the "View As Table" buttons (the flip is the table), the five fixed Overview charts (they become cards), and the always-open chart wall (cards group under headings and can be collapsed).
Keeps: the counters strip, the filters (League, Season, Competition, as one compact row), the form row, and every table (on the back of its card). Leaderboards stay tables, because a manager needs to sort and scan.
Adds: manager-only cards (squad depth, used v available, data health with a fix-it link), who got him out, selection and availability history.
One system: the card contract and registry from spec 147, shared by the Hub and the manager app; each side chooses its cards.

Questions: 1) Is chart-front, table-back the right trade, or should managers have a toggle that shows tables everywhere? 2) Should the cards be collapsible per group? 3) Which manager-only cards matter most for selection? 4) Same flip animation as the Hub, or a quieter cross-fade on the manager side?
