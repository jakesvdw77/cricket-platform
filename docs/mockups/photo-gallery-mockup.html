What is deliberately left out

  • No approval step, flagging or comments yet. Photos show at once; a manager deletes what is not up to standard.
  • No editing of a photo's tags after upload (delete and upload again).
  • Photos are for signed-in club members only, never public.

Questions for you

  • Should a signed-in account that has no held player yet be able to upload? (Default: no.)
  • Can an uploader delete their own photo? (Default: yes.)
  • Must a photo have at least one tag? (Default: no, untagged photos show under All.)
  • Should the carousel follow the filters, or always show the club's latest 12? (Default: always the latest 12.)
