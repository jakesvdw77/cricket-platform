My read

The top bar belongs to the whole app (the club and you), so a league logo there would change as you move between leagues and compete with your avatar. I would keep the logo beside the title (variant 1, or variant 2 if your logos vary), and show a league logo in the top bar only if you want a persistent "which league am I in" cue; if so it needs the white chip (variant 4).
