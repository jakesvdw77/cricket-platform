Suggestions

  • Three lines saved on a phone, five on a desktop. League and weather share the first line, "versus" is gone, and the status tag moves up beside the team it belongs to (phone) or the venue (desktop).
  • Derby: the tag goes on the row of the team the player is picked for, so a derby reads correctly ("Selected" beside Vets A). A small "Derby" is added to the league line. This needs one new field on the server: which side is the player's (today the match only says whether it is theirs). Until then, the tag stays on the venue line and a derby shows no side.
  • Wording on a phone (under about 480 px): Waiting For Team Announcement becomes Team Pending. Selected, Not Selected and Selected (12th) are short enough already. Full wording on a desktop.
  • Logos: 40 px on a phone, 44 px on a desktop (down from 56 px, which is what makes the card tall). If you want the larger logos kept, the desktop layout still saves three lines.
  • Question: is dropping the word "versus" fine? The small "vs" make the meaning clear, but it is a visible change.
