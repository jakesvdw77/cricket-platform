Hub Availability: sub-menus

Made-up data; nothing is built. The Availability page gets the same pill row as Stats and Matches: Open Polls (the default, where the player answers), Closed Polls (polls that closed recently) and a third pill that shows the player's answers over time, answered or not. Plus a status filter. Answers are Available, Unavailable and Not Sure; "Not answered" is the fourth state.

3 · The third pill: My Responses (name options below)

A record of the player's availability across polls: a short summary (answered, available, unavailable, not answered) and a table of every poll from the season with the answer and the outcome. Where Closed Polls is "what just closed", this is "how reliable has this player been". Open and closed polls both appear, filterable by status.

Names for the third pill

My Responses (my pick, plain and clear) · Answer History · Availability Record · Track Record (friendlier, a little ambiguous) · Replies (shortest). The page's pill row would then read: Open Polls, Closed Polls, My Responses. With the player's first name in the heading when a parent has several players: "Hannes's Responses".

Status filter: All, Available, Unavailable, Not Sure, Not Answered (and the filter also applies to Closed Polls). Kept in the URL with the pill, and remembered per player. Counts on the pills: Open (still to answer), Closed (recent), My Responses (this season).
