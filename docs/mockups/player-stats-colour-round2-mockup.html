Player Stats: intelligent colour

Colour that means something. Where a number is good or bad for the player, the bar says so, with a calm green-to-red scale instead of one grey. Where there is no good or bad (leagues, seasons, venues), bars stay neutral. Gold stays for the headline number and the selection. Made-up figures; nothing is built.

The rule

Gold: the headline number and the selection. Colour with meaning (green to red, calm): score bands, how a batter gets out, team record when the player scores 30+, discipline (wides and no-balls), anywhere better or worse is obvious. Milestones: far away grey, getting there amber, close (80% or more) green, reached gold. Neutral grey/sand: leagues, teams, seasons, venues, matches, anything that is just a list. Comparisons of two categories that have no good or bad (Fours v Sixes, Home v Away): the two-tone green/blue from before. A bowler's wicket types are all good: greens and blue. A batter's dismissal types: not out green, ways out warm.
