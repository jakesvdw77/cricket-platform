Player Hub Stats, round 2: more than a table

Every card below flips (tap it): the front is the headline, the back is the story behind it. All made-up figures. Each card carries a small tag saying whether we HAVE the data, only have it PARTLY, or it is NOT CAPTURED yet, taken from what the scorecard records today (batting: runs, balls, 4s, 6s, how out, bowler, fielder; bowling: balls, maidens, runs, wickets, dots, wides, no-balls; fielding: catches, stumpings, run-outs). There is no ball-by-ball, fall-of-wickets, partnership or per-over data, so nothing here pretends to be a worm chart or a wagon wheel.

What we cannot show yet

NOT CAPTURED Worm chart, run rate by phase (powerplay, middle, death), partnerships, fall of wickets, scoring zones and wagon wheels, balls dot percentage for batters, boundaries conceded by bowlers, wicketkeeper dismissals, dropped catches. Each needs ball-by-ball capture or extra scorecard fields; spec 103 lists them. A future ball-by-ball scorer would unlock most of them at once.

A shape for the pages

My stats: a header strip (the season card), then a grid of flip cards grouped Batting, Bowling, Fielding and "Sam as a teammate" (availability, selection). Tiles still drill to the innings table. Team stats: the same grid for the team. Leaderboards: stay tables, with a flip card at the top for "Where Sam stands".
