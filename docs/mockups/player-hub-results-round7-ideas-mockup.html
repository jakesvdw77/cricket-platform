Six directions for adding to the Results area, each sketched on the data the app already has. They are independent: pick any, drop any, or tell me what is missing. Nothing is built. All names and figures are made up.

What it is: more on the Hub Result page: a run progress chart, player of the match, partnerships, fall of wickets. Data: scorecard and over-by-over already captured for imported and captured results; player of the match needs a manager to pick one (or we compute a suggestion). Effort: medium.

5Head to head

What it is: on a result (and on an upcoming match later), the history between the two teams. Data: derivable from results the club already holds. Effort: small to medium. Counts only results visible to players, so it agrees with the Results list.

What it is: a share card for a result a parent is proud of, to send on to family. Data: none new. Note: spec 145 lists sharing as a non-goal and the manager app already has Share Result (spec 114). The Hub version would need its own rules for juniors' names. Effort: medium.

My lean: 1 and 4 first (small, built on what exists, they make the page feel like a scoreboard), then 3, which is the Stats section you already wanted. 2 is the most striking but needs a "player of the match" decision. 5 and 6 are nice extras.
