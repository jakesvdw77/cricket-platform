Spec 150: Roles, permissions and access (Access page)

Round 2 mockup: a grant is now person + role + place. The role says what the person can do; the place says where. Shown for a club-wide Manager and for a Juniors Coordinator, who sees less and can grant less. Custom roles are not in this round; the five built-in roles are. Emails are invented; the first card uses the owner as the example.

  • Role: Manager (all), Coordinator, Team Manager, Scorer, Viewer. A role is a fixed bundle of permissions (see the Roles tab, section 6).
  • Place: the whole club, or one section and everything beneath it. Club-wide permissions (leagues, club setup, imports, player accounts) only count on a whole-club grant.
  • You can only give what you hold: a role is offered only if you hold every permission it carries at that place; sections above yours never appear.
  • Safety: nobody changes or removes their own access; the last club-wide Manager cannot be removed or changed.
