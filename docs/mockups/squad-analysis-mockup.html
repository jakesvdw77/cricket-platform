How each figure is worked out

Main bowler: has a bowling type and is not ticked part-time. Part-time bowler: ticked part-time. All-rounder: a main bowler who bats Opener, Top Order or Middle Order. Batter: anyone who is not a main bowler. Pace: Fast, Fast-medium, Medium-fast. Spin: Off break, Leg break, Orthodox, Wrist spin, Googly. Gaps shown only when true: no wicketkeeper, only one wicketkeeper, no opener set, fewer than 5 bowling options, no spin, no left-hand batter, fewer than 11 players, players without a batting position.
