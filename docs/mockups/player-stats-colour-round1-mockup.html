The rules in each look

Gold: the headline number, the selected or active thing, the focus ring. Nothing else.
Neutral sand: any bar that is one series (leagues, teams, matches, runs by season, venues).
Legend colours: only where categories are compared: Fours v Sixes, Home v Away, Opener v Middle v Tail, dismissal types. Green and blue first, sand and soft red when there are more. Always labelled, so colour never carries meaning alone.
Muted: section labels, the Flip hint, captions.
