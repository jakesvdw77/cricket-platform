Share a league

Mockup for spec 122. Desktop (1280, shown scaled) and phone (375) side by side. Colours and fonts come from brandTokens.ts and theme.ts; buttons are the compact 32 px size. Players are never called children anywhere in the dialog or its output.

1. Step 1, What to share

Multi-select rows (checkbox pattern). Season pill shows only when the dialog opens without a season. Here the league card opened it, so Details and Schedule are preselected. Playing conditions is greyed with its reason. With nothing ticked, Next is disabled and the footer says why (second phone frame).

2. Step 2, Output format

Teams and Schedule are ticked. Calendar needs every ticked content to support it, so it is greyed with the reason. Facebook is hidden because this club switched it off. Back keeps the ticks.

3. WhatsApp preview with contact privacy

League details is ticked and names a contact. Phone and email are off every time the dialog opens and are never remembered. Role and name only by default.

4. Share on the league card and the list row

Card: labelled outlined Share in the footer buttons row (no icon-only variant). List row: Share in the row actions. Card view of the list uses the card. Both preselect League details and Schedule, with the list's selected season.

5. Club profile, Share formats

One switch per format, per content type. Administrators only: everyone else sees it read only. The last enabled format for a content type cannot be switched off.
