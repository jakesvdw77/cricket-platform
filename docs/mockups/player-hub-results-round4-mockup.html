Player Hub results: round 4, my recommendation

You asked what I would do, without leaning on either of your ideas. This is it, built from the Hub's normal parts (the dark console rows, a header, tabs, a dropdown, a bottom sheet) and the cards you approved. Everything is clickable.

My answer in four decisions

1. Results is a main menu item (a leaf, not a group). It is the thing that sells the app, so it should be one tap from the menu, not Schedule > Results. It opens straight onto the page; it has no sub-menu. 2. Stats stays a group (My stats, Team stats, Leaderboards) and uses the same page frame. 3. The scope is shown only when it matters (progressive disclosure): the Season dropdown is always in the header; Team tabs appear only for a player who has more than one team; a League filter button appears with the Team tabs and shows a dot when active. A player with one team and one league sees nothing but "2026 ▾". 4. The menu stays at seven: Account (sign out, switch player, find players) moves to the header avatar so Results can have its own slot.

Why this over the alternatives

Against Results with sub-menus per team: most players have one team, so a team sub-menu would be a pointless extra tap for them; and a drill-in sub-menu hides the page you came for. Tabs appear only for the players who need them and are one tap. Against the header scope chip alone: the Team switch is the most common change for a two-team child, and a chip hides it behind a sheet; tabs show it. Season is the next most common, so it stays one tap in the header. League is rare, so it lives in the filter sheet. What it costs: the Hub menu test and spec 121 (exactly seven main items, Account as a menu item) must change, Account moves to the avatar, and Schedule > Results goes away (Schedule keeps Matches, Leagues, Team sheets).
