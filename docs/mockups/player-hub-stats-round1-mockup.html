Player Hub Stats, round 1

The three Stats pages in the Hub, in the same dark scoreboard style as Results, reusing its pieces: header band with Season and Filters, tiles, form strips, tables. All names and figures are made up. Juniors are shown by full name inside the Hub (owner, 2026-11-07), never in exports or public pages. Nothing is built.

1 · My stats (the selected player)

Opens on the player's headline figures for the chosen scope. Tabs for Batting, Bowling, Fielding and Awards & record, the same four as the manager Player page. Every tile is tappable and drills into the innings behind it (section 2). Copy names the player, never "you".

What is on each page, in one list

My stats: Batting, Bowling, Fielding, Awards & record tabs; tiles with drill into innings; form strip; scope Season, League, Team; "based on N of M results" note.
Team stats: record, form, totals, run rates, top batters and bowlers; team picker for multi-team players.
Leaderboards: Batting, Bowling, Fielding, Awards; Sort by; own row highlighted; Season, League, Team filters.
Menu: Stats stays a group of three (My stats, Team stats, Leaderboards); no new main items.
