Team Sheet, Match Poster style (spec 153 draft, mockup)

A second Style beside the plain sheet, in colour. Same data and switches. The whole squad is listed alphabetically and the Playing tick box stays, so who is playing can be seen at a glance. The W/K column marks the wicketkeeper when Pre-Mark is on, and Captain and Sign are write-on lines at the bottom (Captain pre-filled when Pre-Mark is on). Dashed boxes are the logo slots: each logo is scaled to fit its box, so no logo can push the layout.
  • Header: the affiliation logo (the club) left, the league logo right, the league name and year large in the centre with the tournament dates beneath.
  • Crests: our team logo and the opposition's crest (a league team's logo; a name only when the opponent has no logo).
  • Table: Playing tick box, shirt number, "First SURNAME", W/K column, optional Players: Bat Pos | Bowl (batting position and bowling order, as in the example) and Comment columns (shown here).
  • Blank match: the second page: the same poster with the opposition crest empty and the opposition, date, time and venue left as write-on lines, for play-offs where they are not known until the day. The league header, the host, the sponsors and the whole squad still print.
  • Effects: green side banner with a gold edge, a fading footer band with the club tagline, sponsor logos and the tournament host (a host name and logo set on the League).
