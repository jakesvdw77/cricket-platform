## Decisions of 2026-10-18 (owner)

1. **All suggested extras are kept**, notably **recent form**: the last 5 batting innings and the last 5 bowling spells as small form strips on the Player page Stats card and on the breakdown page.
2. **Leaderboards are in Part 1** and are very important (end-of-year awards): a Leaderboards page with Batting, Bowling, Fielding and Awards boards, filtered by Season, League, Team and Section (descendants included), and an **Awards view** (the "awards night" sheet) that can be printed as a PDF and copied as text.
3. **Data gaps stay deferred.** The owner does not want results capture changed now. Every missing or partially captured statistic is shown as **Not captured yet**, in one panel. A future, named **scorecard capture** spec (batter dot balls, boundaries conceded per bowler, fall of wickets and partnerships, wicketkeeper and captain markers) is listed under Deferred; this spec changes nothing in results capture.
4. **Definitions confirmed:** retired out is a dismissal but **not** a duck. **Hauls** are three exclusive bands because games are short: **3-wicket** (exactly 3), **4-wicket** (exactly 4), **5-wicket** (5 or more), shown as three separate counters that add up cleanly. This replaces the earlier "at least" wording for four- and five-wicket hauls.
5. **Minimums apply to the Awards view only, by default.** The **Awards view** applies a minimum games, innings or overs by default (adjustable there, defaults stated). The **Leaderboards**, used to see the **current standing**, **show everyone**: no qualification by default, with an **optional** minimum control (default: none) so a manager can preview who would qualify. Rate boards in the standings show the figure **plus the innings or overs behind it** in the row, so a small sample is never misleading.
6. **Manager-only in this part; players see leaderboards later, with the player view.** Everything here is designed for the **club manager**. The API and the calculator are kept reusable so the player view needs no rework; a player-facing board will need its own access rule and `114`'s `shareName` and junior-lock treatment.

## Problem & Goals

Managers now capture and publish full scorecards (`101`) and can import them (`104`), but nothing reads those rows back. The Player page still shows a Stats card with a "More coming soon" note and a dash for runs and wickets (`060`), and its only real numbers, "Games this season" and "Games overall", count team **selections** (`MatchSidePlayer`), not matches actually played. A manager who captures every result gets no payoff for it, and at the end of a season there is no way to see who scored the most runs or took the most wickets, let alone to hand out awards.

This spec is **Part 1 of statistics: player statistics** (batting, bowling and fielding) computed on read from **published** results, plus **leaderboards and an end-of-season awards sheet** built on the same numbers. **Team statistics are Part 2** (named below, with a data-availability note, not designed). The product owner expects some data to be missing from our results and asked for suggestions of further statistics and of where the data does not exist; both are here, and anything that needs new capture is deferred.

Goals:
- **A. Every statistic the owner listed**, with its exact name and description, either computed or flagged **Not captured yet** (the Data availability tables say which, and why).
- **B. One definition of every number**, in one pure class (`PlayerStatsCalculator`, `service.support`), so the Player page, every leaderboard, the awards sheet and the tests can never disagree: a leaderboard figure always equals the same player's figure on the Player page for the same filters.
- **C. Honest by construction:** only published results, only our registered players, no guests, no opposition, each figure carrying how much of the data it is based on, so a manager is never misled by a scorecard that was only half captured.
- **D. Filters:** the Player page offers career, Season, League, Team and Competition (league versus friendly); the leaderboards offer Season (default: the current season), League, Team and Section (with its descendant sections), in any combination.
- **E. The Player page Stats card goes live** (headline figures and form strips) with a button to a full breakdown page with Batting, Bowling and Fielding tabs.
- **F. Leaderboards** for the current standing: Batting, Bowling, Fielding and Awards boards showing **everyone** by default, with ranks (equal ranks for ties), stated tie-breaks, the sample size in every rate row, an optional minimum to preview who would qualify, and backend-driven paging (top 10, Show more).
- **G. An Awards view:** one screen showing the leader in each headline category for the chosen filters, **with a default minimum** (adjustable), on screen (PDF and copy-as-text deferred, decision 10).
- **H. Room for later:** the read model leaves a source/scope dimension free for the roadmap's "Player-recorded games" (separate store, never blended silently), for Part 2, and for a **player-facing view** (the API and calculator are reusable; see Architecture).

## Part 2: Team statistics (named only, not designed)

The owner's team list: Matches Played, Wins, Losses, Ties, No Results, Win Percentage (wins / decisive results x 100), Runs Scored, Wickets Lost, Team Batting Average, Average Runs per Innings, Highest and Lowest Team Total, Highest Successful Chase, Largest Victory by Runs and by Wickets, Team Run Rate, Team Net Run Rate (competition defined), Total Wickets Taken, Team Bowling Average, Team Economy Rate, Partnerships (runs by wicket), Extras Conceded, Average Extras per match.

**Data-availability note.** Team statistics need no scorecard: nearly all are computable from `match_result` and `match_innings` alone (`outcome_type`, `winning_side`, `margin_type`, `margin_value`, `scheduled_balls`, `balls_per_over`; innings `runs`, `wickets`, `balls`, `all_out`, `extras`, the extras breakdown), which exist for **every published result, summary level included**. So Part 2 will cover far more matches than player statistics do. Known caveats for Part 2's own spec: (1) **Partnerships (runs by wicket) are MISSING**: fall of wickets and partnerships are not captured (`101`, Non-goals), and stay so until the future scorecard capture spec; (2) **Net Run Rate** is competition defined (all-out innings are usually counted as the full quota of overs, DLS matches use revised targets), so it needs a per-league rule and is not derivable as a single formula; (3) extras and the breakdown are nullable on summary-level results, so Extras Conceded and Average Extras are partial there; (4) the per-team result rule of `100` (a derby gives one team a win and the other a loss; counters count team results) is the model to reuse; (5) Lowest Team Total needs a rule for shortened or unfinished innings (use `all_out` or the full scheduled quota). Part 2 reuses the same filters (including Section), the same published-only gate, the same "one calculator class" rule and the same leaderboard machinery if team tables are wanted.

## User Stories

- As a club manager, I can open a player and see headline figures (matches, runs, batting average, strike rate, wickets, economy) and their recent form on the Stats card, so that I get value from the results I capture without leaving the Player page.
- As a club manager, I can open a full breakdown of a player's batting, bowling and fielding, so that I can answer questions about a player's season or career.
- As a club manager, I can filter a player's breakdown by Season, League, Team and Competition (league or friendly), so that I can compare seasons or look at one team's games.
- As a club manager, I can see the **leaderboards** by season, league, team and section, alone or combined, so that I can see the current standing of every player and hand out end-of-year awards fairly.
- As a club manager, I see every player on the standings, with the innings or overs behind each average, strike rate or economy in the row, so that a figure from one innings is never mistaken for a season's form.
- As a club manager, I can switch on a **minimum innings or overs** on a standings board to preview who would qualify, and I see the same minimum, with its default, on the awards sheet, so that the awards go to players with a fair sample.
- As a club manager, I can see the awards board for a season (most runs, most wickets, best bowling and the rest) on **one screen**, so that the awards night sheet takes a minute, not an evening.
- As a club manager, I see equal ranks for ties and a stated tie-break for every board, so that an award is never decided by an invisible rule.
- As a club manager, I see how many published results the figures are based on, how many had a batting card and a bowling card, and a note where a figure is partial, so that I am never misled by half-captured scorecards.
- As a club manager, I see which statistics are not captured yet and why, so that I know what the club's capture does not yet support, instead of a zero that looks real.
- As a club manager, I see a player (or a leaderboard) with no published results shown as an empty state that explains what will make figures appear, not as a page of zeros.
- As a club manager, I can see how a player has been dismissed and how many of each dismissal a bowler has taken, so that I can spot patterns in a player's game.
- As a club manager, I see a player's player of the match awards and their team's results while they played, so that the page tells a story, not only totals.
- As a club manager, I know that unpublishing a result or linking a guest to a player changes every figure, board and the awards sheet immediately and correctly, with nothing to refresh by hand.
- As the product owner, I can see exactly which of my requested statistics the data supports today and what each missing one needs, so that I can decide what to capture next.

## Data Model Changes

**One small migration (`052`, the per-club award category choice, decision 18) and nothing else.** Statistics and boards are computed on read from the existing tables (the Architecture section explains why and what would change that). The existing indexes cover the queries (see Rollout Notes). Every table and column below was read from migrations `043` to `048` (plus `017`, `018`, `020`, `021`) and the entities `ResultPlayer`, `BattingEntry`, `BowlingEntry`, `DismissalType`, `Match` and `League`; items marked **not verified** were not read.

### What counts (rules fixed by `101`, restated, not changed)

A **counted appearance** is a `result_player` row `rp` where **all** of these hold:

| Rule | Column / source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The result is **published** | `match_result.status = 'PUBLISHED'` | Drafts never count (`100`, `ResultVisibility`). Unpublish removes a result from every figure and board at once |
| The match was **played** (decision 17) | `match_result.outcome_type IN ('WON','TIE','DRAW')`, or `outcome_type IN ('NO_RESULT','ABANDONED')` with at least one ball bowled (a `REGULAR` innings with `balls > 0`, or a `bowling_entry.balls > 0`) | A `FORFEIT`, or a no-result or abandoned match with no ball bowled, is never an appearance. The same rule applies to every batting, bowling, fielding and award row of that match |
| The row is **ours** | `result_player.opposition = false` | Opposition rows are names only, never statistics (`101`) |
| The row is **linked to a registered profile** | `result_player.player_profile_id IS NOT NULL` | A guest (`NULL`) counts for nobody until linked (`101`, "Guests and borrowed players") |
| The row is **not a substitute** | `result_player.substitute = false` | `101`'s appearance rule. A 12th man who bats, bowls or fields is promoted to `substitute = false` when his first row is saved, so he counts. A substitute fielder's catch is therefore not credited (open question 8) |
| The row is **not an unconfirmed import guess** | `result_player.import_match IS NULL` | Defensive only: `104` blocks publishing while any row carries it, so a published result has none |
| The result belongs to **this club** | `match_result.club_id = :clubId` | Tenancy |
| Entries are on **regular** innings | `match_innings.kind = 'REGULAR'` | `101` rejects super-over entries; the filter is defensive and keeps `105`'s decision explicit |

- **Matches (a match played)** is the count of **distinct** `match_result_id` over counted appearances. A player on both sides of a derby (two `result_player` rows, `PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES`) counts **one** match; all of their rows from both sides still count in full for their innings, bowling and fielding (`101`, Derby rule 6).
- A **batting, bowling or fielding row counts** when its `result_player_id` is a counted appearance. Only rows reached through a counted appearance are read, never the entry tables on their own.
- **Stored effective values are used as they are.** Bowler `wickets` and fielder `catches`, `stumpings`, `run_outs` are read from `bowling_entry` and `fielding_entry` whether their source is `DERIVED` or `MANUAL`; nothing recomputes a credit from the dismissals (`101`: "stay the stored effective values (what `103` and every check read)").
- **Edits after publishing.** A published result cannot be edited; it must be unpublished first, and then it no longer counts. The one edit allowed on a published result is **linking a guest to a player**, which changes only `player_profile_id`; because statistics are computed on read, a link changes every figure and board on the next read with no refresh step (a main reason for compute-on-read).
- **Deactivated players** keep their statistics (disable, never delete) and appear on boards for the seasons they played (an awards night often honours someone who left mid-year); the page is reachable from the Player page as today.
- **A player who moved teams in a season** is one player: unfiltered by team, all their games count together; with a Team filter only the games they played **for that team** count (the row's side picks the team, see Filters).

### Which person (aggregation key)

`result_player.player_profile_id` points at this club's `PlayerProfile`, and `UNIQUE (person_id, club_id)` means **one profile per person per club**. Within a club, grouping by `player_profile_id` and grouping by `person_id` give the same answer, so **the aggregation key is the profile (`playerId` in the URL, the same id the other `/players/{playerId}` routes use; confirm at plan time)**. The queries join `player_profile` and carry `person_id` so a later cross-club roll-up changes one group-by, not the calculator. A person with profiles at two clubs sees each club's own figures only; combined career figures wait for the roadmap's cross-club link, and a duplicate `Person` inside one club is fixed by the roadmap's in-club merge (results move to the kept profile). This is proposed, not confirmed (open question 2). Specs `015` and `018` were **not read**; the key rests on `001`, `PlayerProfile` and `101`.

### Card captured, partial figures and summary results

A published result may be **summary level** (no `result_player` at all), or have a team list but no batting or bowling card, or a card with optional columns left blank. The calculator therefore works from **what was captured**, with these rules (each is a unit-test case):

- **Card captured.** For a counted appearance, the player's **batting card is captured** when the innings in which the player's side batted has at least one `batting_entry`; the **bowling card is captured** when the innings in which the other side batted has at least one `bowling_entry`. Batting statistics (Innings and everything built on it) use only innings with a captured batting card; bowling statistics only innings with a captured bowling card. Without this rule, a team list with no batting card would make every player look like they did not bat. A player on a captured card with **no row** is treated as did not bat (or did not bowl), which is how the scorecard itself reads.
- **Matches** counts the appearance regardless of cards (a team list alone proves a match played, `101`).
- **Optional columns.** `balls`, `fours`, `sixes`, bowling `wides`, `no_balls` and `dots` are `NULL` when not captured. A statistic built on them uses only the innings where **every column it needs** is non-null, and the response reports `coverage` (innings used out of innings counted). A batting row with `balls = 0` and `runs > 0` is treated as `balls` not recorded (a batter cannot score off no balls faced; scorers sometimes enter 0 for unknown).
- **Summary-level results** have no player rows and cannot count for any player. The page says so (see "UI Requirements", the basis line).

## Precise definitions

All figures are computed once, in `PlayerStatsCalculator`, from additive per-player counters (see Architecture). Rounding is `RoundingMode.HALF_UP` applied **once at the end**, never between steps. A statistic with no valid denominator is `null` in the API and a dash in the UI, **never infinity, never 0, never NaN**.

### Batting

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Innings** | A counted batting row whose `how_out` is anything except `DID_NOT_BAT`, on an innings with a captured batting card. A row with `how_out` null (a draft-only state; publish refuses it) is ignored |
| **Dismissal** | An innings whose `how_out` has `DismissalType.isWicket() = true`: `BOWLED`, `CAUGHT`, `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED`, `LBW`, `STUMPED`, `RUN_OUT`, `HIT_WICKET`, `HANDLED_BALL`, `OBSTRUCTING_FIELD`, `HIT_BALL_TWICE`, `TIMED_OUT` and **`RETIRED_OUT`** (a tactical retirement is a wicket in `101`, so it is a dismissal; **decided 2026-10-18**) |
| **Not out** | An innings that is not a dismissal: `NOT_OUT` and `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` (retired hurt is not a dismissal, `101`). Always `Innings = Dismissals + Not outs` |
| **Runs** | Σ `runs` over innings |
| **Highest Score** | The largest `runs`; shown "87*" when that innings was not out. On equal scores the not-out innings is the higher (so 50 and 50* shows 50*) |
| **Batting Average** | Runs / Dismissals, 2 decimals. No dismissals: dash |
| **Balls Faced** | Σ `balls` over innings with `balls` recorded (and not the `balls = 0, runs > 0` case) |
| **Strike Rate** | Σ runs / Σ balls x 100, 2 decimals, **over the same innings that recorded balls** (runs from innings without balls never inflate it). Σ balls = 0: dash |
| **Centuries** | Innings with `runs >= 100` (exclusive count: not the 50s) |
| **Half-Centuries** | Innings with `runs` from 50 to 99 (exclusive: a century is not also a half-century) |
| **30+ / 50+ / 100+ Scores** | Cumulative and **inclusive**: innings with `runs >= 30`, `>= 50`, `>= 100`. So 50+ includes every century, and 100+ equals Centuries. Shown as listed by the owner; the overlap is by design |
| **Fours / Sixes** | Σ over innings that recorded them |
| **Ducks** | Dismissals with `runs = 0`, **excluding `RETIRED_OUT`** (decided 2026-10-18: retired out is a dismissal but not a duck) |
| **Golden Ducks** | Ducks where `balls = 1`. A duck whose `balls` is null is a duck but is not counted as golden (not provable). `TIMED_OUT` (0 balls) is a duck but never golden |
| **Boundary Percentage** | (4 x fours + 6 x sixes) / runs x 100, 1 decimal, over innings with both `fours` and `sixes` recorded. Σ runs = 0: dash |
| **Balls per Boundary** | Σ balls / Σ (fours + sixes), 1 decimal, over innings with `balls`, `fours` and `sixes` recorded. No boundaries: dash |
| **Dismissal Mode** | One count per dismissal type (caught, bowled, LBW, run out, stumped, hit wicket, caught and bowled, other: handled the ball, obstructing the field, hit the ball twice, timed out, retired out), plus a Not out count. Read from `how_out` as stored |
| **Dot Ball Percentage** | Not defined: not captured (see the table) |

### Bowling

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Innings Bowled** | A counted `bowling_entry` with `balls > 0` or `runs > 0` on an innings with a captured bowling card. A listed bowler with nothing bowled is not an innings |
| **Balls Bowled** | Σ `balls` (legal deliveries) |
| **Overs** | `oversFormat.ballsToOvers(balls, ballsPerOver)` when every counted bowling innings has the same `match_result.balls_per_over`. When they differ the API returns `overs = null` and `mixedOverLengths = true`; the UI shows Balls Bowled with "Mixed over lengths" (a sum of "overs" across 5-ball and 6-ball matches would be meaningless). The conversion is done in the UI by `oversFormat` only; the API carries balls |
| **Runs Conceded** | Σ `runs` (wides and no-balls charged to the bowler are in it; byes, leg byes and penalty runs are not) |
| **Wickets** | Σ `bowling_entry.wickets` as stored (never recomputed) |
| **Bowling Average** | Runs Conceded / Wickets, 2 decimals. No wickets: dash |
| **Economy Rate** | Runs Conceded / Σ (balls / ballsPerOver of that match), i.e. runs per over where an over is **that match's** number of balls, 2 decimals. For all-six-ball data this is runs x 6 / balls. No balls: dash. Intermediate overs use at least 10 decimal places; rounding happens once. The same Σ (balls / ballsPerOver) is the **overs** used by the bowling minimum and shown beside bowling rates |
| **Strike Rate** | Balls Bowled / Wickets, 1 decimal (balls per wicket, independent of over length). No wickets: dash |
| **Best Bowling** | The innings with the most wickets; ties broken by **fewest runs**, then **most balls bowled**, then (on the Player page only, to choose which innings to show) the most recent match. Shown "4/17". No bowling innings: dash |
| **Three / Four / Five-Wicket Hauls** | **Three exclusive bands (decided 2026-10-18):** an innings with **exactly 3** wickets, **exactly 4**, **5 or more**. A 6-wicket innings is one five-wicket haul and neither of the others, so the three counters never overlap and add up to the number of innings with 3 or more wickets. The owner's earlier "at least" wording is replaced by these bands. "Most wickets in an innings" is the Best bowling figure (wickets, then runs) |
| **Maiden Overs** | Σ `maidens` |
| **Wides / No-Balls** | Σ `wides`, Σ `no_balls` over innings that recorded them |
| **Dot Balls** | Σ `dots` over innings that recorded them. **Dot Ball Percentage (bowled)**, a suggestion: Σ dots / Σ balls of the same innings x 100, 1 decimal |
| **Dismissal Mode (bowler)** | Counts of batters out `BOWLED`, `CAUGHT`, `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED`, `LBW`, `STUMPED`, `HIT_WICKET` where the dismissal's `bowler_result_player_id` is this player's row, plus `Not recorded = max(0, Wickets - Σ modes)` so the table always reconciles to Wickets |

### Fielding

Catches, Stumpings, Run-outs: Σ of the stored `fielding_entry` values over counted appearances. **Dismissals** = their sum. **Dismissals per match** = Dismissals / Matches, 2 decimals (dash when no matches). A row exists only for a fielder with at least one credit, so Matches (not rows) is the denominator.

### Awards and results

**Player of the match** = count of the official award; **Team player of the match** = count of the club team's own award. **Record while playing** = for each counted match (not a derby): won if `outcome_type = WON` and `winning_side` is the player's `side`, lost if `WON` and the other side, tied for `TIE`, drawn for `DRAW`. A match between two of the club's own teams is counted under **Internal matches** and gives no win or loss to the player.

### Form and positions

**Recent form (decided 2026-10-18):** the five most recent **batting innings** (by `match.match_date`, newest first: runs, a star if not out, balls if known) and the five most recent **bowling spells** (wickets/runs, overs), shown as small strips. **Innings by batting position**: per `batting_entry.position` the innings, runs and average (a position with no dismissals shows a dash).

All filters narrow the **appearances** (and so every figure) and are applied in the query, never in the browser.

|---|---|---|---|
| Career (Player page default) | none | whole club history | "All time, within this club" |
| Season | `seasonId` | `match.season_id` (required on a match, `029`) | `FilterBar` Season, with "All seasons". **Leaderboards default to the current season** |
| League | `leagueId` | `match.league_id` (optional) | Existing League filter |

## Architecture proposal (a decision for the owner)

**Proposal: compute on read, no materialised table, one set of definitions.** Flagged as a proposal (open question 10).

| | Compute on read (proposed) | Materialised table, refreshed on publish and unpublish |
|---|---|---|
| Data volume | One player has a few hundred scorecard rows over a career; a season for a whole club is thousands; all-time for a large club tens of thousands to low hundreds of thousands. Every query starts from `result_player` and the entry tables through existing indexes, and aggregates to **one row per player** (a roster, hundreds) | Faster reads, but the volume does not call for it |
| Edited after publish | A link of a guest to a player, an unpublish, a delete of a result, a player merge all change the answer; computed on read, they are correct by construction | Each of these must trigger a refresh. A missed trigger shows a wrong number on an awards sheet, the exact "stats go stale" risk `100`'s Statistics decision avoided |
| Definitions | One place: `PlayerStatsCalculator` over additive counters | Definitions would also have to live in the refresh SQL |
| Standards | One service skeleton, `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`, DTO out; no job, no new table, no migration | New table, entity, refresher, backfill, migration and tests |
| When to switch | Only when a measured run on a seeded 200,000-row club (all-time, no filters, the heaviest request) exceeds a budget (propose 500 ms p95 for one board, 1.5 s for the awards view). **Not in Part 1 unless that measurement shows a concrete problem.** Then add a disposable cache table rebuilt per club on publish, unpublish, link and merge (as `100` allowed) | Same, but paid up front |

**Shape of the read** (so the definitions stay in one place and the Player page and every board agree by construction):
1. **One aggregate query family, `PlayerStatsAggregateRepository`** (a new repository, touched only by the stats services; ArchUnit rule to add). Native SQL (conditional `FILTER` aggregates) with the fixed predicates of "What counts" and the filters, **grouped by `player_profile_id`**, returning **additive counters** per player, never derived figures: appearances (`matches`, results with a batting card, results with a bowling card), batting (`innings`, `notOuts`, `dismissals`, `runs`, `ballsFaced` and the runs and innings that fed it, `fours`, `sixes` and the boundary runs and innings that fed them, `ducks`, `goldenDucks`, `thirtyPlus`, `fiftyPlus`, `hundredPlus`, `centuries`, `halfCenturies`, and a **highest-score key** `runs * 2 + (not out ? 1 : 0)` so a plain `max` already prefers the not-out innings), bowling (`inningsBowled`, `balls`, `runsConceded`, `wickets`, `maidens`, `wides`, `noBalls`, `dots` and their innings counts, `overs` as Σ balls / ballsPerOver of each match, the count of distinct balls-per-over values, `threeWicketHauls`, `fourWicketHauls`, `fiveWicketHauls`, and a **best-bowling key** `wickets * 10^10 + (99999 - runs) * 10^5 + balls`), fielding (`catches`, `stumpings`, `runOuts`) and awards (`playerOfTheMatch`, `teamPlayerOfTheMatch`). The single-player endpoint runs the **same query restricted to one profile**.
2. **`PlayerStatsCalculator`** (pure, `service.support`, no Spring, no repository) turns one player's counters into every derived figure (averages, rates, percentages, the haul bands as given, the decoded highest score and best bowling, the economy, the coverage). **Nothing else computes a figure**, in SQL or in the client. SQL only sums and counts stored columns; the only SQL logic beyond that is the encoded `max` keys.
3. **`LeaderboardRanker`** (pure, `service.support`) takes the calculator's output for every player in the filtered scope, applies the board's ordering and the optional minimum (see Leaderboards), assigns ranks and slices the requested page. A **parity test** asserts that for every player in a seeded club, each board's figure equals the Player page figure for the same filters.
4. **Fact-row queries for one player only** (not boards): recent form, innings by batting position, bowler dismissal modes. They are a few small indexed queries by `player_profile_id`.
5. **Pagination.** `backend.md` asks for `LIMIT`/`OFFSET` on any list that can grow unbounded. The aggregate result is **one row per player of the club (bounded by the roster; a defensive cap of 5,000 rows, above which the request is a 400 and is logged)**, and a rank with ties, a minimum computed from the scope's busiest player, and one set of definitions all need the whole ordered set, so the **server** ranks and slices the page (`page`/`size`, default 10, maximum 100) and sends only the page. The browser never receives or slices more than the page. This is a **deliberate, bounded deviation from the letter of the standard** (the same kind of exception as the Players roster, "an unpaginated, bounded list", `frontend.md`), flagged for the owner and the standards reviewer (open question 16). Fallback if rejected: SQL `RANK()` and `LIMIT`/`OFFSET` per board, with the ordering expressions duplicated in SQL and guarded by the parity test.
6. **The publish gate.** `backend.md` says only the result services touch `MatchResultRepository`, and `ResultVisibility` is a `Specification<MatchResult>`, which a join query cannot use. The aggregate queries therefore contain the fixed predicate `status = PUBLISHED` (a named constant shared with `ResultVisibility`, not a re-typed string) and a **test that a draft and an unpublished result never appear**. `backend.md`'s sentence is amended in the same PR to name the stats repositories as the second permitted reader and add their ArchUnit rule.
7. **Indexes and proof.** No new index is expected (see Rollout Notes). The plan runs `EXPLAIN` on a seeded dataset (a 200,000-row club, all-time with no filters, one season, one section with descendants) and records the plan and timings in the PR. The awards view runs **one** aggregate query and ranks every headline category from it in memory, so it costs one board, not ten.
8. **Reusable for the player view (decided 2026-10-18).** The calculator, the ranker and the aggregate repository take a **scope** (club, filters, an optional restriction to a set of teams) and know nothing about who is asking. Access (`canAccessClub`, section narrowing) lives only in the controller and the service's entry methods. A later player-facing route is therefore a new controller and an access rule (who may see which teams, and the `114` `shareName` and junior-lock treatment of names) over the same service, with no change to a definition. Nothing manager-only is baked into the DTOs: names are returned as first and last name and formatted by the client.

## Leaderboards

### Boards

Fixed in code (`LeaderboardBoard` enum, one catalogue served by the API). **The standings show everyone with a figure by default** (decided 2026-10-18); no board has a mandatory qualification. Rank is **competition ranking**: players whose figure and every tie-break criterion are equal share a rank, and the next rank skips (1, 2, 2, 4). Equal-ranked players are listed by display name, then id, so paging is stable. "Min (optional)" marks the rate boards on which the optional minimum control (below) can be used.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Batting | Most runs (`MOST_RUNS`) | runs | at least 1 innings | runs desc; fewer innings; higher highest score |
| Batting | Highest score (`HIGHEST_SCORE`) | highest score, star if not out | an innings | runs desc; not out before out; fewer balls faced (when recorded) |
| Batting | Batting average (`BATTING_AVERAGE`), **Min (optional)** | average | at least 1 innings | players **with** an average first: average desc; runs desc; more innings. Players with **no average** (never dismissed) are listed **last, unranked** (see below) |

| Batting | Most sixes (`MOST_SIXES`) | sixes | at least 1 innings | sixes desc; fewer innings; runs desc |

| Bowling | Economy rate (`ECONOMY_RATE`), **Min (optional)** | economy (lower is better) | at least 1 ball bowled | economy asc; wickets desc; more overs |

| Bowling | Most dot balls (`MOST_DOT_BALLS`), **where captured** | dots | at least 1 innings that recorded dots | dots desc; higher dot ball percentage; more overs |
| Fielding | Most catches (`MOST_CATCHES`), most stumpings (`MOST_STUMPINGS`), most run outs (`MOST_RUN_OUTS`), most dismissals (`MOST_FIELDING_DISMISSALS`) | count | at least 1 match | count desc; fewer matches; (for dismissals) more catches |
| Awards | Most player of the match (`MOST_POTM`) | official awards | at least 1 match | awards desc; more team player of the match awards; fewer matches |
| Awards | Most team player of the match (`MOST_TEAM_POTM`) | club team awards | at least 1 match | awards desc; more official awards; fewer matches |

Notes: **Best bowling merges** the owner's "Best bowling" and "Most wickets in an innings" (one definition, one board); each player appears once, with their best innings. "Fewer innings" and "fewer matches" reward efficiency and are stated on the board's help text. Counting boards list only players with at least 1 of the thing (a zero is not a standing). Boards that depend on optional columns (strike rate, fours, sixes, bowling dots) say "based on N of M innings that recorded this" and exclude innings that did not; if **no** player in scope recorded the figure, the board shows an empty state ("Dot balls are not captured for these results") instead of a list.

### Rate boards in the standings: small samples and zero denominators (decided 2026-10-18)

- **Show everyone, with the sample in the row.** A rate board lists every player who has a figure, and **each row carries the sample behind it** so a small sample is never misleading: batting average and strike rate rows show "**3 innings**" (strike rate: innings that recorded balls, and the balls faced), bowling rows show "**4.2 overs**" (the same Σ balls / ballsPerOver as the economy, through `oversFormat` when over lengths are uniform, otherwise balls) and the wickets. A row whose sample is under the **suggested minimum** (below) carries a muted **Small sample** chip, a hint only: the row is still ranked and shown.
- **Zero denominators sort last, unranked.** A player with no defined figure on a rate board (a batter never dismissed has no average; a bowler with no wickets has no bowling average or strike rate) is **listed after every ranked player, with a dash and no rank number**, ordered by volume (runs, or overs, descending) then name. They are never ranked 1 or compared with a figure that exists, and never shown as infinity or 0. (Economy has a figure for anyone who bowled a ball, so it has no such group; strike rate on the batting side excludes players with no balls recorded entirely, since there is nothing to show.)
- **Optional minimum (preview).** Each rate board has an optional **Minimum innings** (batting boards) or **Minimum overs** (bowling boards) field, **default: none** ("No minimum"). Setting it removes players below it from the ranked list (they are counted: "4 players are below the minimum") so a manager can preview who would qualify; it is **per visit** and not persisted. It never changes a figure.
- **What a minimum counts:** innings (for strike rate, innings that recorded balls); overs as Σ (balls / ballsPerOver of each match), so a 5-ball and a 6-ball match each count as the overs they were. Counting boards have no minimum.

### Minimum qualification on the Awards view (default on, adjustable)

The **Awards view** applies a minimum **by default** to the rate categories it contains (Batting average, Economy rate), because an award must go to a player with a fair sample. The controls **Minimum innings** and **Minimum overs** are on the Awards view, with the default stated ("Default 3 for these filters. Players with fewer innings cannot win a rate award.") and **Reset to default**. Batting average also needs at least one dismissal, and a bowling category needs at least one wicket where its figure is wickets-based.

- **Filter-aware default.** Let **M** be the largest number of matches played by any one player in the filtered scope (so a six-match team season and a ten-year career get different defaults). Defaults (placeholders to try on real data, held in one class, `LeaderboardQualification`, unit tested):
  - **Minimum innings** = `clamp(ceil(M / 2), 1, 5)` (about half of the busiest player's games, never below 1 or above 5);
  - **Minimum overs** = `clamp(M, 2, 10)` (about an over per match of the busiest player, never below 2 or above 10).
- The same two defaults are the **suggested minimums** that drive the muted **Small sample** chip on the standings (above), so the standings and the awards agree on what "small" means, without the standings hiding anyone.
- The chosen awards minimums are **per visit**, not persisted, so they follow the filters back to the defaults. A player hidden by a minimum is still on every board and on the Player page; nothing is altered.

### API Contract (leaderboards)

Base `/api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}`; `@PreAuthorize("@access.canAccessClub(authentication, #clubId)")`; `LeaderboardController`, `LeaderboardService` interface plus `LeaderboardServiceImpl` (`@Transactional(readOnly = true)`); DTOs only. Manager routes under `/manage`; a future player route is a separate controller (Architecture, point 8).

| Endpoint | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /leaderboards` | club manager | The board catalogue: groups, each board's `code`, `label`, `figureLabel`, `direction`, `minimumKind` (`NONE`, `INNINGS`, `OVERS`), help text with the tie-breaks. One source for the page, so the client never hard-codes a definition |
| `GET /leaderboards/{board}?seasonId=&leagueId=&teamId=&sectionId=&minimum=&page=&size=` | club manager | One board (the standing). All filters optional (no `seasonId` is all time; the page sends the current season by default) and combinable. **`minimum` is optional and absent means no minimum**; it applies only to boards whose `minimumKind` is not `NONE`. `page` starts at 0, `size` defaults to 10, maximum 100. Unknown `board` is 404; unknown or other-club `seasonId`, `leagueId`, `teamId` or `sectionId` is 404; a negative or non-numeric `minimum` is 400; a `sectionId` outside a section-scoped manager's accessible sections is 403 |
| `GET /leaderboards/awards?seasonId=&leagueId=&teamId=&sectionId=&minimumInnings=&minimumOvers=` | club manager | The awards view: the **leaders** (every player at rank 1, so a tie gives joint winners) of each headline category, from one aggregate query. **Absent minimums mean the filter-aware defaults** (the opposite of the board route), echoed in the response |

**Board response (`LeaderboardPageDto`):**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `board` | the catalogue entry |
| `scope` | `{ kind: "CLUB_VERIFIED", seasonId, leagueId, teamId, sectionId }` |
| `minimum` | `{ applies, value (null = none), suggestedDefault, unit }`: `suggestedDefault` is the filter-aware default, sent so the UI can show "Default 3" beside the optional field and mark small samples |
| `basis` | `resultsCounted`, `playersListed`, `playersRanked`, `belowMinimum`, `coverage` (innings used out of innings counted, for boards on optional columns) |
| `page` | `{ number, size, totalElements, hasMore }` |
| `rows[]` | `rank` (null for the unranked group), `playerId`, `firstName`, `lastName` (the UI formats through `PlayerName` and the existing name rules), `active`, `figure` (a number, or `{ wickets, runs }` for best bowling, or `{ runs, notOut }` for highest score, or null for the unranked group), `matches`, `innings` (batting innings on batting boards, bowling innings on bowling boards, null on fielding and awards), **`sample`** `{ innings, ballsFaced }` on batting rate boards and `{ balls, overs (null when over lengths are mixed), wickets }` on bowling rate boards, `smallSample` (true when `sample` is under `suggestedDefault`), and for the single-innings boards (`HIGHEST_SCORE`, `BEST_BOWLING`) a `detail` `{ matchId, matchDate, opponent }` resolved by one follow-up query for the page's rows |

**Awards response (`AwardsViewDto`):** `scope`, `basis`, `minimums { innings, overs, defaultInnings, defaultOvers }` (the values used), and `categories[]`, each `{ board, leaders[] }` where a leader is the same row shape (so figures equal the boards'). Headline categories (fixed in code, open question 14): **Most runs, Highest score, Batting average, Most wickets, Best bowling, Economy rate, Most catches, Most dismissals (fielding), Most player of the match**. The minimum applies to **Batting average and Economy rate** (the rate categories). A category with no qualifying player is omitted from the sheet with a note ("No bowler reached the minimum overs"), not shown empty.

**Section narrowing.** A section-scoped manager (`035`) reaches the leaderboards through `canAccessClub` but is **narrowed to the teams of their accessible sections** (intersected with any Section filter): a board compares players, and a manager must not rank, or see the figures of, teams outside their remit. A club admin sees the whole club. (The Player page endpoint is club-wide; see open question 5.)

## API Contract

Base `/api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}`; `@PreAuthorize("@access.canAccessClub(authentication, #clubId)")` like the other player routes (any club manager). `PlayerStatsController`; `PlayerStatsService` interface plus `PlayerStatsServiceImpl` with `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`; DTOs only (`backend.md`). A wrong-club or unknown player is 404 (`NotFoundException`). The leaderboard routes are in the section above.

| Endpoint | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /players/{playerId}/stats?seasonId=&leagueId=&teamId=&competition=` | club manager (`canAccessClub`) | One player's statistics from published results, with the filters of "Filters and scope". Every parameter optional; no parameters is career. An unknown `seasonId`, `leagueId` or `teamId` for this club is 404; `competition` other than `LEAGUE` / `FRIENDLY` is 400 (`ValidationException`). A player with no counted appearance returns 200 with zero counters and `basis.matchesCounted = 0` (not 404), so the empty state is a normal read |
| `GET /leaderboards`, `GET /leaderboards/{board}`, `GET /leaderboards/awards` | club manager | See "API Contract (leaderboards)" |

**Response (`PlayerStatsDto`):**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `playerId`, `scope` | `{ kind: "CLUB_VERIFIED", seasonId, leagueId, teamId, competition }` echoing the filters |
| `basis` | `matchesCounted`, `resultsWithBattingCard`, `resultsWithBowlingCard` (decision 9: no caution for results without a team list) |
| `batting` | raw counters (`matches`, `innings`, `notOuts`, `dismissals`, `runs`, `ballsFaced`, `fours`, `sixes`, `ducks`, `goldenDucks`, `thirtyPlus`, `fiftyPlus`, `hundredPlus`, `centuries`, `halfCenturies`, `highestScore { runs, notOut }`, `dismissalModes { type: count }`, `positions[]`) and derived (`average`, `strikeRate`, `boundaryPercentage`, `ballsPerBoundary`), nullable decimals already rounded, plus `coverage { inningsWithBalls, inningsWithBoundaries, inningsCounted }` |
| `bowling` | `inningsBowled`, `ballsBowled`, `ballsPerOver` list and `mixedOverLengths`, `runsConceded`, `wickets`, `maidens`, `wides`, `noBalls`, `dots`, `threeWicketHauls`, `fourWicketHauls`, `fiveWicketHauls`, `best { wickets, runs }`, `dismissalModes { type: count, notRecorded }`, derived `average`, `economy`, `strikeRate`, `dotBallPercentage`, and `coverage { inningsWithWides, inningsWithNoBalls, inningsWithDots, inningsCounted }` |
| `fielding` | `catches`, `stumpings`, `runOuts`, `dismissals`, `dismissalsPerMatch` |
| `awards`, `record` | `playerOfTheMatch`, `teamPlayerOfTheMatch`; `won`, `lost`, `tied`, `drawn`, `internal` |
| `form` | `batting[]` (last five innings) and `bowling[]` (last five spells), newest first, each with the match id, date and figures |
| `notCaptured` | the fixed catalogue of statistics the data does not support, each `{ code, label, reason }` (batting dot ball percentage, boundaries conceded, wicketkeeper dismissals, partnerships and fall of wickets, catches dropped, direct hits); one list in the calculator so removing an item when capture lands is a one-line change |

The frontend never recomputes any figure (`frontend.md`: "No business rules in the client"); it formats (`oversFormat` for overs, a dash for `null`, `PlayerName` for names). `openapi.yaml` is regenerated by hand per the repo's practice, additions only. New exceptions: none beyond the existing 404, 400 and 403.

**Who may read it.** Any manager who passes `canAccessClub`. **Players cannot read their own statistics or the boards in this part** (decided 2026-10-18: players see leaderboards later, with the player view, which needs its own access rule and `114`'s treatment of names). **Draft visibility:** statistics and boards read published results only, by the fixed predicate and the test above; a draft is never read, counted or even detected.

## Privacy and data quality

- **Privacy.** This part is manager-only: no public, shared or player-facing statistics, boards or pages, no player login. Manager screens already show full names (`101`). Many players are children and appear on boards. **Any later public, shared or player-facing board or sheet must use `114`'s First names as initials rule (`utils/share/shareName.ts`) and its junior lock, and needs its own access rule**; the awards PDF and text, the only output that leaves the screen in this part, honour that rule as described in "Awards view". Whether to rank children at all is open question 15.
- **Draft results are excluded**, unpublished results disappear at once from every figure and board.
- **Imported results with guessed names are excluded until confirmed**, because publishing is blocked while any row carries `IMPORTED_NAME_UNCONFIRMED` (`104`); the `import_match IS NULL` predicate is only a belt-and-braces guard.
- **Guests and opposition names never count** until a guest is linked (a link on a published result moves the figures and boards on the next read).
- **Summary-level results** (no scorecard) give no player rows, so figures and boards are only as complete as the scorecards captured. The basis line says so on every view. This matters for fairness on an awards sheet: a team whose results were only captured as summaries cannot have a leader, and the sheet's basis states how many results it is built from.
- **Partial scorecards** (balls, fours, sixes, wides, no-balls, dots left blank; a bowler's dismissals not attributed) are reported through `coverage` and "Not recorded", never silently treated as zero; boards that depend on them say how many innings they used.
- **Small samples** in the standings are shown, not hidden, with the innings or overs beside the figure; the awards view applies a minimum by default.
- **Overs of different lengths** are handled by `balls_per_over` per match (economy, the minimum overs) and the mixed-length rule (overs).
- **Super-overs** are excluded (no entries exist; the filter is defensive).
- **Two "games" numbers.** The Player page key figures count selections; the Stats card and the boards count matches played from published results. They answer different questions and can differ (open question 9).

## Acceptance Criteria

- A manager can open a player and see headline figures (Matches, Runs, Batting average, Strike rate, Wickets, Economy) and the last 5 innings and last 5 bowling spells on the Stats card, with dashes where a figure has no valid denominator, and a real **View full breakdown** button.
- The full breakdown has Batting, Bowling and Fielding tabs and shows every statistic marked Available or Derivable in the Data availability tables, with the exact names and descriptions the owner gave, and **three separate haul counters** (exactly 3, exactly 4, 5 or more).
- Every statistic marked MISSING or only partly captured (batting dot ball percentage, boundaries conceded, wicketkeeper dismissals, partnerships and fall of wickets) is **not** shown as a number; it is listed under **Not captured yet**, in one panel, driven by one catalogue in the calculator. **Results capture is unchanged.**
- Only **published** results of **this club** with outcome won, tie or draw count; a draft, an unpublished result, a forfeit, an abandoned and a no-result match never contribute (integration tested).
- Only counted appearances count: our registered, non-substitute rows; guests, opposition rows and unconfirmed import guesses never do. A guest linked after publishing counts from the next read. A player on both sides of a derby counts one match.
- All figures come from `PlayerStatsCalculator`; the client only formats. Every definition in "Precise definitions" has a unit test, including: average is a dash with no dismissals; 50+ includes centuries; Centuries and Half-centuries are exclusive; the haul bands do not overlap; a golden duck needs `balls = 1`; `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` is not a dismissal and `RETIRED_OUT` is a dismissal but not a duck; economy uses each match's own balls per over; mixed over lengths show Balls Bowled instead of Overs; stored effective wickets and credits are never recomputed.
- Optional figures are computed only over innings that recorded them, and a coverage caption says how many; a missing figure is never treated as 0. An innings with no captured card never makes a player look as if they did not bat or bowl.
- **A Leaderboards page** exists under the Schedule menu group after Results (`/manage/leaderboards`), with Batting, Bowling, Fielding and Awards boards exactly as listed in "Boards", each with its stated tie-breaks.
- Every leaderboard filters by **Season (default: the current season), League, Team and Section** (a Section includes its descendant sections, the same rule as `ResultPlayerEligibility`) and any combination; the four filters persist per club; all are backend parameters; a manager restricted to some sections sees only those sections' teams.
- **A leaderboard figure always equals the same player's Player page figure for the same filters** (parity tested), because both come from the same counters and `PlayerStatsCalculator`.
- **The standings show everyone with a figure; there is no minimum by default.** Every rate row shows the innings or overs behind the figure, a small sample carries a muted chip and is still ranked and listed, and **players with no defined figure (never dismissed, no wickets) are listed last, unranked, with a dash**. An **optional** "Minimum innings" or "Minimum overs" field (default: none) lets a manager preview who would qualify; it is per visit.
- **The Awards view applies a minimum by default** to its rate categories (Batting average, Economy rate): "Minimum innings" and "Minimum overs" controls are pre-filled with the filter-aware defaults (`clamp(ceil(M / 2), 1, 5)` innings, `clamp(M, 2, 10)` overs), are adjustable with Reset to default, and are stated on the PDF and the text; one innings cannot win a rate award at the defaults.
- **Ranks use equal ranks for ties** (1, 2, 2, 4); a tie at rank 1 shows every joint leader; the order within equal ranks is by name, so paging is stable.
- **Paging is backend-driven:** the top 10 by default, **Show more** fetches the next page, the browser never slices a larger list; the size is capped at 100.
- A player who moved teams in a season is one player on a Season board; a Team filter counts only their games for that team.
- **The Awards view** shows the leader(s) of each headline category on one screen for the chosen filters, (PDF and text output deferred, decision 10), with names through the existing on-screen name rules; a category nobody qualifies for is omitted with a note.
- **Manager-only:** the endpoints are guarded by `canAccessClub`; another club's player, season, league, team or section is 404; responses are DTOs; services are `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`; no entity crosses the controller boundary; no player-facing or public route exists, and the calculator, ranker and aggregate repository carry no caller or access information, so a later player view reuses them without changing a definition.
- One migration (`052`, per-club award categories) and no new table; no new dependency; the OpenAPI changes are additive; one new menu item and its brand icon.
- `backend.md`, `frontend.md`, `docs/roadmap.md` and `docs/architecture.md` are updated in the build PR (see Rollout Notes).
- Team statistics, public or player-facing boards, cross-club statistics and own-record scopes are **not** present.
