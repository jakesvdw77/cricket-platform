## Problem & Goals

`103` answers "how many runs, wickets and catches has this player made?" with totals, averages and form strips. It cannot answer the question a manager asks next: "which matches were those, who were we playing, and what did he do in each?" To see a player's history today the manager opens each result one by one from the Results page and looks for the player in the scorecard. A club that captures every result gets totals but no way to walk back through a player's games, or to check a surprising total (a low average, a missing five-wicket haul) against the matches behind it.

This spec adds a fifth tab, **Matches**, to the player statistics page (`/manage/players/:playerId/stats?tab=matches`, after "Awards & record"): one row per match the player played, newest first, with the player's own figures for that match, built on the same counting rules, filters and access as `103`. It is a read of data that already exists; nothing is captured or stored differently.

Goals:
- **A. A player's whole history, one row per counted match**, newest first: date, opponent, the team the player played for, competition or Friendly, the result from the player's side, the player's own batting, bowling and fielding figures for that match, and a player-of-the-match marker.
- **B. Exactly `103`'s counting rules, stated once.** The rows are the same appearances `103` counts (published results only, our registered non-substitute player, WON/TIE/DRAW, or NO_RESULT/ABANDONED with a ball bowled, never a forfeit, never opposition, guest or import-guess rows, `REGULAR` innings only, a deactivated match with a published result still counts). The counting rules still live in one place, the `APPEARANCES` SQL of `PlayerStatsSql`; this spec adds no second copy.
- **C. Parity with `103`, and a tile strip.** The sum of the rows' figures equals the same player's `PlayerStatsDto` totals for the same filters, and the number of rows equals `basis.matchesCounted`. Above the list a strip of Matches, Runs, Wickets and Catches (from `PlayerStats`) shows the headline totals; there is no Total row. A parity test enforces it (the exception is the optional-column coverage rule `103` already defines, restated under "What a row contains").
- **D. The page's existing filters, no new filter UI.** Season, League, Section, Team and Competition apply unchanged; they already offer only values the player has data for (`103` decision 22), persist Season and League per club, and honour the leaderboard link parameters.
- **E. Backend-driven paging** with a Show more button (`useInfiniteQuery`, never client slicing), and a "Showing N of M" line.
- **F. Honest about missing scorecards.** A match with a team list but no scorecard shows its result and dashes with a "No scorecard captured" note, never zeros.
- **G. Open the match.** Tapping a row opens that match's result page, with a way back to the same filtered list.
- **H. Reusable for the later player view.** The service and DTO carry no access information (same rule as `103`, Architecture point 8): a later player-facing route is a new controller and access rule over the same service. Names go out as first and last name only; this endpoint returns no other person's name at all (see Privacy).

## Non-goals

- **A player-facing view of the match list.** Manager-only now, like `103` (decision 13). The service, row builder and DTO are kept free of manager-only assumptions so the player view reuses them; a player-facing list needs its own access rule and `114`'s `shareName` and junior-lock treatment, decided with the player view and login flow.
- **Player-recorded own games** (roadmap: "Player-recorded games (own records)"; for example school games the player captures). This list is verified club results only, the same `CLUB_VERIFIED` scope as `103`. Own games would be a separate store and a second, labelled scope, never blended silently into this list.
- **Export** (CSV or PDF of the list). Deferred; see open question 10.
- **Per-ball or over-by-over detail**, fall of wickets, partnerships, who took a wicket or a catch. A row shows the player's own figures only (and how he was out as a type, not the bowler's or fielder's name). The scorecard on the result page is the detail.
- **Match filters beyond `103`'s** (opponent, result, venue, date range, "only matches where he scored 50+", free-text search). Season, League, Section, Team and Competition only. Adding a filter would change `103`'s filter model and the options endpoint, so it is a separate decision.
- **Sorting by a figure** (by runs, by wickets) in version 1. Date, newest first, with an optional reverse (open question 5).
- **Editing anything.** Read-only: no link, unlink or correction from this tab. A guest linked to a player on a published result (the one allowed edit, `101`) changes this list on the next read, with nothing to refresh by hand.
- **Summary-level results.** A published result with no `result_player` rows cannot name a player and so never appears in any player's list; `103` decision 9 dropped the "results without a team list" caution, and this spec does not bring it back.
- **Margin text and score lines on a row** ("won by 5 wickets", "187/6 v 142/9"). The row carries the result from the player's side only; the full result line is one tap away on the result page.
- **A new table, migration, index, cache or materialised view.** Compute on read (`103`, Architecture proposal).
- **Any change to results capture or to a definition in `PlayerStatsCalculator`.**
- **Club time zones.** Dates use the new `utils/formatInstantDate` helper (an ISO instant written as a date in the browser's zone, "14 Mar 2026"); the known gap that the system assumes South Africa time (roadmap) is unchanged.

## User Stories

- As a club manager, I can open a player's **Matches** tab and see every match they played, newest first, so that I can go back through their whole history.
- As a club manager, I can see for each match the date, the opponent, the team the player played for, the competition or Friendly, and whether the player's side won, lost, tied or drew, so that I can place the game without opening it.
- As a club manager, I can see the player's own runs, balls and how they were out, their overs, maidens, runs, wickets and dot balls, and their catches, stumpings and run-outs for each match, so that I can see where a total came from.
- As a club manager, I can see which matches the player was player of the match or their team's player of the match, so that the awards are visible in the history.
- As a club manager, I can narrow the list by Season, League, Section, Team and Competition with the filters already on the page, and the list, the headline tiles and the other tabs stay consistent with each other.
- As a club manager, I see a strip of headline tiles (Matches, Runs, Wickets, Catches) above the list, from the same statistics as the other tabs, and the sum of the rows equals them, so that the list and the totals can never disagree.
- As a club manager, I see "No scorecard captured" on a match that only has a team list, instead of zeros, so that I never mistake a missing scorecard for a player who did nothing.
- As a club manager, I see 20 matches at a time with "Showing 20 of 63" and a Show more button, so that a long career loads quickly.
- As a club manager, I can tap a match to open its result and scorecard, and come back to the same list with the same filters.
- As a section-scoped manager, I see only matches for my own sections' teams, the same as on the other tabs.

## Data Model Changes

**None. No table, column, migration or index.** Every row is computed on read from `match_result`, `match`, `result_player`, `batting_entry`, `bowling_entry`, `fielding_entry`, `match_award`, `match_innings`, `team`, `league` and the opposition-side fields of `match`, through the counted appearances of `103`.

Reuse, not redefinition: "What counts" (the table in `103`), "Card captured, partial figures and summary results", "Precise definitions" and "Filters and scope" apply to this list unchanged. Where this spec needs a rule of its own, it is under "What a row contains" below.

### What a row contains (the one pure place)

A new pure class, **`PlayerMatchRowBuilder`** (`service.support`, no Spring, no repository, no entity, no caller or access information), is the only place that decides what a row contains. It takes the raw facts of one counted match (below) and returns the row's figures, statuses and result. Nothing else in SQL, the service, the mapper or the client decides a row's content (the client formats). This is the same discipline as `103`'s `PlayerStatsCalculator`: SQL selects stored columns, the pure class decides.

**One row per match.** A row is one distinct `match_result_id` over the player's counted appearances (the matches count of `103`). A player on both sides of a derby has two `result_player` rows and gets **one** row (`101` Derby rule 6, `103` "Matches"); both sides' figures are combined into it.

**Result from the player's side** (`PlayerMatchResult`), derived exactly as `103`'s "Record while playing":

| Value | When |
|---|---|
| `WON` | `outcome_type = WON` and `winning_side` is the player's side; not a derby |
| `LOST` | `outcome_type = WON` and `winning_side` is the other side; not a derby |
| `TIED` | `outcome_type = TIE`; not a derby |
| `DRAWN` | `outcome_type = DRAW`; not a derby |
| `NO_RESULT` | `outcome_type = NO_RESULT` (counted because a ball was bowled) |
| `ABANDONED` | `outcome_type = ABANDONED` (counted because a ball was bowled) |
| `INTERNAL` | a derby (both sides are teams of this club), whatever the outcome; gives no win or loss to anyone |

`FORFEIT` never produces a row (`103` decision 17). `NO_RESULT` and `ABANDONED` rows are matches played whose figures count (decision 17) and add to none of won, lost, tied or drawn, exactly as in `103`.

**Figures per row.** Each block has a **status** so that "not captured", "did not play a part" and a real zero can never look alike:

| Block | Status values | Rule (all from `103`) |
|---|---|---|
| Batting | `NO_CARD`, `DID_NOT_BAT`, `BATTED` | `NO_CARD` when no `REGULAR` innings in which the player's side batted has a `batting_entry` (card not captured). `BATTED` when the player has at least one counted batting row with `how_out` set and not `DID_NOT_BAT`. Otherwise `DID_NOT_BAT` (on a captured card with no batting row, which is how the scorecard reads) |
| Bowling | `NO_CARD`, `DID_NOT_BOWL`, `BOWLED` | `NO_CARD` when no innings in which the other side batted has a `bowling_entry`. `BOWLED` when at least one of the player's stored bowling rows has any non-zero figure (`balls`, `runs`, `wickets` or `maidens` above 0; decision 5). Otherwise `DID_NOT_BOWL` (all stored figures 0, or no row on a captured card). `spells[]` lists only the rows with `balls > 0` or `runs > 0` (the Innings Bowled rule), so a `BOWLED` row may have an empty `spells[]` while the sums carry the wicket |
| Fielding | `NO_CARD`, `RECORDED` | `NO_CARD` only when neither a batting nor a bowling card was captured and no fielding row exists (a team list with no scorecard). `RECORDED` also whenever any fielding row exists, so a stored credit is never dropped. Otherwise `RECORDED` with the stored `catches`, `stumpings`, `run_outs` summed, and **0 only here**, because a `fielding_entry` row exists only for a fielder with a credit and the scorecard was captured (`103`) |

A match in which the player appears on a team list with no scorecard at all has `NO_CARD` on all three blocks and shows the result and dashes (open question 2). A derby row's card flags are true when the card is captured on **either** side the player appears on.

- **Batting figures** (status `BATTED`): `innings[]`, one entry per counted batting row (almost always one; two or more in a multi-innings match or in a derby where the player batted for both sides), each `{ runs, balls, notOut, howOut, fours, sixes }`; plus the summed `runs`, `ballsFaced`, `fours`, `sixes`, `innings` count. **Optional columns follow `103`'s coverage rule:** `balls` is `null` when not recorded or in the `balls = 0, runs > 0` case (the `103` "valid balls" rule), and `ballsFaced` sums only valid balls; `fours` and `sixes` are `null` when not recorded and their sums add only recorded values. A dash in the UI, never 0. `howOut` is the stored `DismissalType` code (the client prints the wording through `utils/dismissal.ts`); `notOut` is true for `NOT_OUT` and `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` (retired hurt is not a dismissal). **No bowler or fielder name** is returned.
- **Bowling figures** (status `BOWLED`): `spells[]`, one per counted bowling row (only rows with `balls > 0` or `runs > 0`; may be empty), each `{ balls, maidens, runs, wickets, dots, wides, noBalls }` (`dots`, `wides`, `noBalls` nullable); plus the summed `balls`, `maidens`, `runs`, `wickets`, `dots` (the sums cover every stored bowling row, spell or not) and the match's `ballsPerOver`. `wickets` is the stored effective value, whether `DERIVED` or `MANUAL`, never recomputed (`101`, `103`). Overs are shown by the client through `oversFormat` from balls and `ballsPerOver`; the API carries balls. `dots` sums only recorded values and is `null` when none was recorded.
- **Fielding figures:** `catches`, `stumpings`, `runOuts`, and the total dismissals is computed by the client's formatting as their sum (a plain sum for display, as `103` does for the total).
- **Awards:** `playerOfTheMatch` and `teamPlayerOfTheMatch` as counts (0, 1, or 2 for a derby team award) of `match_award` rows whose `result_player_id` is one of the player's counted appearances. A free-text winner or an opposition winner counts for nobody (`103`).

**Parity rule.** For the same filters:
- number of rows = `basis.matchesCounted` = the page's `totalElements`;
- Σ rows with status `BATTED` of `innings` count = `batting.innings`; Σ `runs` = `batting.runs`; Σ `ballsFaced` = `batting.ballsFaced`; Σ `fours`, `sixes` = `batting.fours`, `batting.sixes` (all three already follow the same coverage rule as the totals);
- Σ rows with a captured batting card = `basis.resultsWithBattingCard`; Σ rows with a captured bowling card = `basis.resultsWithBowlingCard`;
- Σ `balls`, `runs`, `wickets`, `maidens`, `dots`, `wides`, `noBalls` of bowling = the same `103` bowling counters; Σ spells = `bowling.inningsBowled`;
- Σ `catches`, `stumpings`, `runOuts` = the `103` fielding counters;
- Σ `playerOfTheMatch`, `teamPlayerOfTheMatch` = `awards`;
- the count of rows by `result` = `record.won`, `lost`, `tied`, `drawn`, `internal` (rows with `NO_RESULT` or `ABANDONED` count in none, as in `103`).

Figures that are not additive (average, strike rate, highest score, best bowling, hauls) are not summed and are not shown on the rows; the tile strip (below) shows only Matches, Runs, Wickets and Catches, taken from `PlayerStats`, not from the rows.

**Derby with a Team filter.** With a Team filter in a derby only the row on that team's side counts (`103`, Filters), so the row then holds that side's figures only and the result stays `INTERNAL`. With no Team filter the row combines both sides. Both are `103`'s behaviour; the parity test covers both.

## API Contract

Base `/api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}`; `@PreAuthorize("@access.canAccessClub(authentication, #clubId)")` (any club manager), exactly as the other player stats routes.

| Endpoint | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /players/{playerId}/stats/matches?seasonId=&leagueId=&teamId=&sectionId=&competition=&direction=&page=&size=` | club manager (`canAccessClub`), section-narrowed in the service | One page of the player's counted matches, newest first (or oldest first with `direction=asc`), with the player's figures per match |

**Filter semantics and errors: identical to `GET /players/{playerId}/stats`** (`PlayerStatsController.get`): every filter optional and combinable; an unknown or other-club player, season, league, team or section is 404 (`NotFoundException`); `competition` other than `LEAGUE` / `FRIENDLY` is 400 (`ValidationException`); a `sectionId` or `teamId` outside a section-scoped manager's reach is 403 (`ClubMatchScope`); a section-scoped manager is otherwise narrowed to the teams of their accessible sections (intersected with any Section filter, descendants included) by the same `StatsScopeResolver.resolve(...)`, called, not copied. When the caller's reach leaves nothing, the answer is an empty page (200), not an error. **New rules of this endpoint:** `page` starts at 0 (default 0), `size` defaults to **20** and is at most 100 (a `size` below 1 or above 100, a negative `page` or a `direction` other than `asc` / `desc` is 400, `ValidationException`); a player with no counted appearance returns 200 with an empty page and `totalElements = 0` (not 404), so the empty state is a normal read. Default order is `match_date` descending, then `match.id` descending as a stable tie-break (so paging never repeats or skips a row); `direction=asc` reverses both.

**Controller and service shape (`backend.md`).** The method is added to `PlayerStatsController` (same base path, same guard). A new interface `PlayerMatchListService` with `PlayerMatchListServiceImpl` (`@Transactional(readOnly = true)`, so the lazy-field rule is met from the start), kept separate from `PlayerStatsServiceImpl` so that class does not grow. DTOs only; the impl calls `StatsScopeResolver`, the stats repository and `PlayerMatchRowBuilder`; no figure is decided in the service. A player name appears in no log line or exception message (`backend.md`, Statistics).

**Response `PlayerMatchListDto`:**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `playerId`, `scope` | `{ kind: "CLUB_VERIFIED", seasonId, leagueId, teamId, sectionId, competition }` echoing the filters, the same `PlayerStatsScope` shape as `103` |
| `page` | `{ number, size, totalElements, hasMore }`, the page object `103`'s leaderboards use. `totalElements` is the number of counted matches under the filters, so it equals `basis.matchesCounted` of the stats read |
| `rows[]` | `PlayerMatchRowDto`, below |

**`PlayerMatchRowDto`:**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `matchId`, `resultId` | the match (the id the result page route uses) and its `match_result` |
| `matchDate` | the match's date and time as an ISO instant; the client formats the date |
| `ourTeams[]` | the club team(s) the player played for in this match: `{ side, teamId, teamName }`. One entry normally; two in a derby when the player is on both sides |
| `opponentName` | the opposition side's name; `null` for a derby (both sides are ours, shown as an internal match). Resolved from the match's opposing team or its free-text opposing team name, as the Results page does; the plan confirms the exact fields, including a linked league team name |
| `derby` | true when both sides are teams of this club |
| `leagueId`, `leagueName`, `competition` | the match's league, or `null`, `null` and `FRIENDLY` for a friendly; `LEAGUE` otherwise |
| `result` | `WON`, `LOST`, `TIED`, `DRAWN`, `NO_RESULT`, `ABANDONED`, `INTERNAL` (from the player's side, above) |
| `ballsPerOver` | the match's balls per over (for `oversFormat`) |
| `hasBattingCard`, `hasBowlingCard` | the card-captured flags of `103`, as defined above |
| `batting` | `{ status, innings[], inningsCount, runs, ballsFaced, fours, sixes }`; figures present only when `status = BATTED` |
| `bowling` | `{ status, spells[], balls, maidens, runs, wickets, dots }`; figures present only when `status = BOWLED` |
| `fielding` | `{ status, catches, stumpings, runOuts }` |
| `awards` | `{ playerOfTheMatch, teamPlayerOfTheMatch }` (counts) |

`openapi.yaml` is regenerated by hand per the repo's practice, additions only. No new exception type; the existing 404, 400 and 403 matrix applies.

**The tile strip needs no new endpoint.** The page already reads `GET /players/{playerId}/stats` with the same filters for its headline tiles and basis line; the strip (Matches, Runs, Wickets, Catches; decision 4) is fed from that response, so its figures equal the Batting, Bowling and Fielding tabs by construction, and stay correct when only the first page of rows is loaded.

**Who may read it.** Any manager who passes `canAccessClub`; players cannot read it in this part (`103` decision 13). Draft visibility: the query carries the fixed published predicate through the shared `APPEARANCES` fragment (`ResultVisibility.PUBLISHED_STATUS`); a draft or unpublished result never appears, and a test proves it.

### SQL approach

Extend `PlayerStatsSql` (the shared fragment `103` built so the counting rules exist once) and add the row queries to **`PlayerStatsFactsRepository`**, which is already the permitted second reader of the result tables and already touched only by the statistics services (ArchUnit, `LayeringRulesTest`), so no new ArchUnit rule is needed. (If the plan prefers a separate `PlayerMatchRowsRepository`, it must be added to that same rule and to `backend.md`'s sentence; either is acceptable.) All queries start from `PlayerStatsSql.APPEARANCES` and take its eight parameters unchanged (`clubId`, `playerId`, `seasonId`, `leagueId`, `teamId`, `competition`, `sectionRestricted`, `sectionTeamIds`), plus `limit`, `offset` and `ascending`.

The statements per page are fixed and none runs per row: count, page, batting, bowling, fielding and awards (six), plus the player lookup and the scope lookups of `StatsScopeResolver` (no N+1; a query-count test in the style of `PlayerStatsQueryCountIntegrationTest` pins it):

1. **Count:** `select count(distinct mrid) from ap`, the `totalElements`.
2. **Page of matches:** one row per `mrid` from `ap` (grouped; `bool_or(side = 'HOME')`-style side flags, `derby`, outcome, winning side, `bpo`, the match date, the league name, our team name(s) per side and the opponent name or names), `order by match_date desc, match_id desc limit :limit offset :offset` (direction flipped for `asc`). Also the two card-captured booleans, using the same `exists` expressions as `103`'s `app` CTE (`has_bat`, `has_bowl`), extracted into shared constants in `PlayerStatsSql` so the aggregate and the rows read one definition.
3. **Entries of the page (steps 3 to 6 as built: batting, bowling, fielding, awards):** the player's counted batting rows, bowling rows and fielding sums and award counts for exactly the `mrid`s of step 2 (`ap` restricted by `mrid in (:ids)`), one query per entry kind, each joined through `ap.rpid` (a row is reached only through a counted appearance, never the entry tables on their own) on `REGULAR` innings only. The "valid balls" expression (`be.balls is not null and not (be.balls = 0 and coalesce(be.runs, 0) > 0)`) that `103`'s `bi` CTE and `recentInnings` both repeat becomes one shared constant used by all three, so the coverage rule is defined once; this is a refactor with no behaviour change, proven by the existing `PlayerStatsSqlLiteralsTest` and the parity test.

`PlayerMatchRowBuilder` then turns each match's header and entries into the row. SQL only selects stored columns and the shared boolean predicates; sums, statuses and the result mapping are the builder's. The builder's sums and `103`'s SQL `sum()` are two independent implementations of the same totals, which is exactly what makes the parity test meaningful.

**Indexes.** None added. The query is for **one player**, so it starts at `ix_result_player_profile` (partial, `player_profile_id IS NOT NULL`), joins `ix_match_result_club_status`, the unique `match_result(match_id)` and `ix_match_date`/`ix_match_season`/`ix_match_league`, and reaches entries through `ix_batting_entry_result_player`, `ix_bowling_entry_result_player`, `ix_fielding_entry_result_player` and `ix_match_award_result` (all present, `103` Rollout Notes). A career is hundreds of appearances, not the 200,000 rows of a leaderboard. The plan records an `EXPLAIN` of the heaviest realistic case (one player with 500 appearances, no filters) in the PR; budget 150 ms p95 for a page. A new index only if that shows a problem (the candidate would be `match_award(result_player_id)`, not verified to exist).

## UI Requirements

Mobile-first at 375 px, MUI v5 through existing components only (`docs/standards/design-system.md`, `frontend.md`). A mockup of each new surface on a phone and a desktop is approved before the build (the repo's rule), listed under "Mockups".

**Where it lives.** `PLAYER_STATS_TABS` gains `{ value: 'matches', label: 'Matches' }` as the fifth and last entry; `resolvePlayerStatsTab` and `PlayerStatsTabs` pick it up from the same config, so `?tab=matches` works, an unknown value is still Batting, and the strip keeps its other parameters. `PlayerStatsPage` renders a new **`MatchesTab`** (`pages/manage/playerStats/MatchesTab.tsx`, a page part, so no four-file anatomy) for the tab. The page's `FilterBar`, persisted Season and League (`playerStats:filters:<clubId>`), per-visit Team, Section and Competition, the leaderboard link parameters, the filter options and the empty states are all reused as they are. **No new filter UI.** The basis line above the tab reads "Based on N published results with this player on the team list." (the Fielding/Awards wording of `basisLine`), which is the same N as the list's total.

**API and hook.** `ui/src/api/playerStatsApi.ts` gains the `PlayerMatchRow` types and `usePlayerMatches(clubId, playerId, filters)` built on `useInfiniteQuery` (`initialPageParam: 0`, `getNextPageParam` from `page.hasMore`), keyed under `['managed-club', clubId, 'players', playerId, 'stats', 'matches', filters]` so a result unpublish or guest link that invalidates the stats prefix refreshes it. A filter change is a new key and so restarts at page 0. Rows are appended page by page; nothing is sliced in the browser.

**Layout (recommended; open questions 1 and 11).**
- **From `sm`: a table** in one bordered panel with the look of `StandingsTable` (header 36 px, 12 px bold uppercase tracking 0.04em; data rows 40 px minimum with **14 px (0.875 rem) cells**, tabular numerals, zebra `zebraTint`, hover `hoverTint`, a 2 px primary inset focus ring). Columns, in order: **Date** (e.g. "14 Mar 2026"), **Opponent** (the name, with the team the player played for as a 12 px `text.secondary` sub-line; for a derby "Internal match" with both teams), **Competition** (league name or "Friendly"), **Result** (a text-and-dot badge, never colour alone (decision 3, not an icon): Won, Lost, Tied, Drawn, No result, Abandoned, Internal, through `badgeSx`), **Batting** as three narrow columns **Runs**, **Balls**, **How out** (the star after the runs for not out, "87*"), **Bowling** as one cell showing wickets over runs "2/21" with a sub-line "4.2 ov · 1 mdn · 8 dots" (parts left out when not recorded), **Fielding** as one cell naming only the non-zero credits ("2 ct · 1 st"), and a narrow **Awards** column holding the player-of-the-match markers. A cell with nothing to show is a muted dash.
- **Phone: a stacked card per match** (the row is a whole-card link, minimum 44 px high, equal structure on every card): first line the date and the result badge, second line the opponent in bold with the team as a sub-line, third the competition, then up to three labelled lines **Batting**, **Bowling**, **Fielding** (a line is left out only when its status says nothing happened, see below), and the award markers beside the date. Same data, same order as the table.
- **Tile strip, no Total row (decision 4).** Above the list, the same `StatTile` strip as the other tabs (4-up on desktop, 2-up on a phone): **Matches, Runs, Wickets, Catches**, taken from the `PlayerStats` the page already holds. No fours or sixes, no Total row, no Total card. It is not part of the paging.
- **Order.** Newest first. On the table the Date heading is a button with `aria-sort`; a click reverses (`?dir=asc`, open question 5). On a phone there is a separate 44 px sort button above the cards, "Date: newest first" ("Date: oldest first" once reversed).

**What a cell shows for each status.** Batting `BATTED`: runs, balls (dash if not recorded), how out in words ("caught", "bowled", "not out"); with several innings, the innings are listed one per line within the cell. `DID_NOT_BAT`: the muted text "Did not bat". `NO_CARD`: a dash. Bowling `BOWLED`: as above; `DID_NOT_BOWL`: "Did not bowl" muted; `NO_CARD`: a dash. Fielding `RECORDED`: the non-zero credits, or a dash when all are 0; `NO_CARD`: a dash. **A match with all three blocks `NO_CARD`** shows the result and one muted merged note **"No scorecard captured"** across the figure columns (desktop) or in place of the three lines (phone), with the row still opening the result (open question 2). Zeros appear only where a real zero was recorded (for example a bowler's 0 wickets for 14 runs), never as a stand-in for a missing card.

**Awards marker.** Player of the match: a filled trophy icon with the visible text "POTM" on a phone card and the icon with an accessible name "Player of the match" in the table; team player of the match: an outlined icon, "Team POTM" (open question 7). A row can carry both.

**Show more and counts.** Under the list, "Showing 20 of 63 matches" (from `page.totalElements`) and a real outlined, 44 px **Show more** button that requests the next page (`fetchNextPage`), hidden when `hasMore` is false. While fetching the next page the button shows a busy state and the rows stay. While filters change, the previous rows stay at reduced opacity (`placeholderData: keepPreviousData`, as the standings do) so the layout does not jump.

**Opening a match.** The date/opponent cell is a link to `/manage/results/:matchId` (the existing result page, read-only scorecard, `ResultDetailPage`), stretched over the row as `PlayerTable` does, with the accessible name "14 March 2026 against Riverside, open result" (date and opponent; the visible text is the short date, a label-in-name tension noted here and mandated by this spec). The link carries router state `{ backTo, backLabel }` holding the stats page URL with the current filters (`?tab=matches&seasonId=&leagueId=&teamId=&sectionId=&competition=`); `ResultDetailPage`'s "Back to Results" becomes "Back to <label>" when valid state is present (`backTo` must start with `/manage/`, otherwise it is ignored; a missing or blank `backLabel` falls back to the label "previous page"), and is unchanged when absent. The back URL always writes `seasonId` and `leagueId` (an empty value means All, so the saved Season and League choice does not override it on return) and the other filters, and `dir`, only when set. `PlayerStatsPage` already reads `seasonId`, `leagueId`, `teamId` and `sectionId` from the address once on load; it gains `competition` the same way so the link returns to the same list. Show more progress and scroll position are not restored; the list returns to the first page (open question 9).

**Empty, loading and error states** (through the page's existing `EmptyState` handling): a player with no published results is the page's existing "No published results yet" state (the Matches tab is the same, the tab strip stays); filters that match nothing are the existing "No published results match these filters" with Clear filters; while the first page loads, skeleton rows of the same height; a failed read shows "Couldn't load the matches" with a Try again button, leaving the tab strip and filters alone.

**Accessibility and size.** The table is a real `role="table"` with `rowgroup`, `row`, `columnheader`, `rowheader` (the date/opponent cell) and `cell`, with `aria-label="Matches played"`, `aria-colcount`, and `aria-busy` while fetching, the same pattern as `StandingsTable`. The result is conveyed by text and icon, not colour alone. Cells are 14 px (the larger readable size the owner asked for in `103`); on a phone every row and card, the Show more button and the Date sort heading are at least 44 px high; the whole row is the link with a visible 2 px focus ring; there is no totals row, so no totals label. Colours are theme tokens only.

**Shared components.** Composes from `FilterBar`, `PageHeaderBand`, `EmptyState`, `badgeSx`, `PlayerStatsTabs`, `zebraTint`, `hoverTint`, `useStatsFilterLookups`, `oversFormat` and `utils/dismissal.ts`. The row anatomy (table row and phone card) is page-level (`MatchesTab`, `MatchRow`, `MatchCard` in `pages/manage/playerStats/`), as `103`'s tabs are. **No new shared component is proposed.** If the table row look is reused by a second page later it is extracted then, with its story and test.

**Mockups (approved 2026-10-20, see the decisions above; the list is what was required).** (1) The Matches tab, populated, with Show more, on both (the Total row of the approved boards is superseded by the tile strip, decision 4); (2) a no-scorecard match and a batting-card-only match (bowling dashes, "Did not bowl" and "Did not bat" states) in the same list; (3) a derby match (one row, "Internal match", both teams) and a player who played for both sides; (4) a no-result/abandoned-with-play row and a player-of-the-match row (both award markers); (5) the empty and filtered-empty states and the loading and error states; (6) the filter bar open on a phone (the existing sheet, to show nothing new is needed); (7) the row focus and the Date sort heading; (8) the result page header with the new "Back to Matches" link.

## Test Plan

Per `docs/standards/testing.md`. Per the working rule, tests and the standards review run after the owner has browser-checked the function.

**Backend unit (JUnit 5, no Spring), `PlayerMatchRowBuilderTest`:** one case per rule in "What a row contains": the result mapping for every outcome and side (WON/LOST/TIED/DRAWN, NO_RESULT and ABANDONED giving their own value, a derby always `INTERNAL`); the three block statuses (no card, did not bat or bowl on a captured card, recorded); a team-list-only match gives `NO_CARD` on all blocks; fielding 0 only when a card was captured; a batting-only card gives `NO_CARD` bowling; a derby player on both sides is one row with both sides' figures combined and `ourTeams` of two; a multi-innings match gives two `innings[]` entries and a summed total; optional columns: `balls` null when not recorded and in the `balls = 0, runs > 0` case (so `ballsFaced` skips it), `fours`/`sixes`/`dots`/`wides`/`noBalls` null when unrecorded and sums over recorded values only; `notOut` for `NOT_OUT` and `RETIRED_NOT_OUT`, a dismissal for `RETIRED_OUT`; a bowling row with `balls = 0` and `runs = 0` is not a spell, yet is `BOWLED` with an empty `spells[]` and its stored wickets in the sums when any figure is non-zero, and an all-zero row stays `DID_NOT_BOWL` (decision 5); stored `wickets` used as given; award counts including two team awards in a derby; a free-text or opposition award counts for nobody; ordering inputs left alone (the builder does not sort).

**Backend integration (Testcontainers, `AbstractIntegrationTest`), `PlayerMatchListIntegrationTest`, services without an ambient transaction (`open-in-view=false`),** over the `PlayerStatsSeed`-style seeded club: a published result with a full scorecard; a draft (never appears); a result unpublished later (disappears); a guest linked after publishing (appears on the next read); a forfeit, and an abandoned and a no-result match with no ball bowled (never a row); an abandoned/no-result match with a ball bowled (a row, counts as played, result `ABANDONED`/`NO_RESULT`); a deactivated match with a published result (a row); an opposition row, a substitute row and an unconfirmed import guess (never a row); a summary-level result (no row); a super-over innings row (excluded by `kind`); a team list with no scorecard (a row with all three `NO_CARD`); a batting card with no bowling card; a derby (one row for a player on both sides; with the Team filter only that side); a player who moved teams (rows for both teams unfiltered, one team's rows with the Team filter); each filter alone and combined (Season, League, Section including a grandchild section, Team, Competition, an inconsistent combination giving an empty page); **paging** (default size 20, `hasMore`, `totalElements` stable across pages, stable order when several matches share a date, a page past the end empty, reverse with `direction=asc`, size 1 and 100 accepted, 0 and 101 refused); **tenancy** (another club's player, season, league, team or section is 404 and never a row); the fixed query count per page (no N+1); and an `EXPLAIN` of the heaviest realistic case recorded in the PR.

**Parity test (the key one), `PlayerMatchListParityIntegrationTest`,** in the style of `LeaderboardParityIntegrationTest`: for every player in the seeded club and a set of filter combinations (none, each of Season, League, Team, Section, Competition, and a combined one), load **all** pages (size 100) and assert every line of the "Parity rule" above against the same player's `PlayerStatsDto` for the same filters, including the optional-column coverage exception (a seeded innings with `balls` blank and one with `balls = 0, runs > 0`), a derby player on both sides, a multi-innings match, and a no-result match with play. A change to a `103` counting rule or to the builder that breaks parity fails this test.

**Controller (`PlayerStatsControllerIntegrationTest` additions):** `canAccessClub` required (403 otherwise); another club's player/season/league/team/section is 404; a bad `competition`, `size`, `page` or `direction` is 400; a section-scoped manager is narrowed to their sections' teams and a `sectionId` or `teamId` outside them is 403; a player with no appearances returns 200 and an empty page; only a draft existing changes nothing.

**Structural (ArchUnit):** `PlayerMatchRowBuilder` depends on no Spring, repository or entity class and takes no caller or access information (added to the existing `103` calculator rule); the new service touches the stats repository and not `MatchResultRepository`; DTOs only cross the controller; the existing rule that only statistics services touch the stats repositories still passes.

**Contract:** OpenAPI diff, additions only (one route, its three DTOs).

**Frontend component (Vitest + Testing Library):** `PlayerStatsPage` test additions: the fifth tab is last and bound to `?tab=matches`; the filters are sent as parameters; rows render in the order received; the three statuses render (dash, "Did not bat", figures) and a no-scorecard match shows "No scorecard captured" and no zeros; a derby row shows "Internal match" and both teams; the result badge has text, not colour only; the award markers have accessible names; the tile strip shows Matches, Runs, Wickets and Catches from the stats read and there is no Total row; **Show more** requests the next page and appends (never slices) and disappears when `hasMore` is false, "Showing N of M" is right; a filter change restarts at the first page; the row link goes to `/manage/results/:matchId` with the back state; `ResultDetailPage` shows "Back to Matches" for valid state and "Back to Results" otherwise, and ignores a `backTo` that does not start with `/manage/`; table roles and `aria-sort`; the empty, filtered-empty and error states. A `statsFormat`-style helper test for any new formatting function.

**End-to-end (Playwright, mobile and desktop), one golden path:** a player with published results across two seasons: open the stats page, choose the Matches tab, see the newest match first and the tile strip, change the Season filter and see the list and tiles change, press Show more, tap a match to open its scorecard, press Back and land on the same filtered list. (Playwright has not been run on `master` since `104`; the roadmap notes it. This test must be runnable before merge.)

## Acceptance Criteria

- The player statistics page has a fifth tab **Matches**, last in the strip, at `?tab=matches`, and an unknown `?tab=` is still Batting.
- The list has **one row per match the player played, newest first**; the number of rows (the `totalElements`) equals the page's `basis.matchesCounted` for the same filters.
- **Only `103`'s counted appearances appear:** published results of this club, our registered non-substitute player, WON/TIE/DRAW or NO_RESULT/ABANDONED with a ball bowled, `REGULAR` innings; never a draft, an unpublished result, a forfeit, an opposition, guest, substitute or import-guess row, or a summary-level result; a deactivated match with a published result still appears. The counting rules are not copied: the SQL starts from the shared `APPEARANCES` fragment.
- Each row shows the date, opponent, the team played for, the competition or Friendly, the result from the player's side (a derby is Internal), the player's batting (runs, balls, how out, a star when not out), bowling (wickets/runs, overs, maidens, dots) and fielding (catches, stumpings, run-outs) figures, and the player-of-the-match markers.
- **Parity:** the sum of the rows' figures equals the same player's `PlayerStatsDto` totals for the same filters (parity test, including the optional-column coverage rule); the tile strip (Matches, Runs, Wickets, Catches) shows the `PlayerStats` figures and matches the Batting, Bowling and Fielding tabs; there is no Total row.
- A match with a team list and no scorecard shows its result and dashes with "No scorecard captured", and **no zero stands in for a missing figure**; a captured card with no row for the player reads "Did not bat" or "Did not bowl".
- A player on both sides of a derby has **one** row with both sides' figures combined and the result Internal; with a Team filter only that side's figures.
- The page's **existing filters** (Season, League, Section, Team, Competition) narrow the list through backend parameters, offer only values with data, persist as `103` defines and honour the leaderboard link parameters; no new filter control exists.
- **Paging is backend-driven:** 20 rows by default, **Show more** fetches the next server page and appends, "Showing N of M matches" is correct, the size is capped at 100, and nothing is sliced in the browser.
- Tapping a row opens `/manage/results/:matchId`; the result page offers a way back to the same filtered list, and shows "Back to Results" when it was not opened from here.
- A section-scoped manager sees only their sections' matches and gets 403 for a section or team outside them; another club's player, season, league, team or section is 404; the endpoint is `canAccessClub`-guarded, returns DTOs only, is `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`, and logs no player name.
- A guest linked after publishing, an unpublish and a deleted result change the list on the next read with nothing to refresh by hand.
- The table is a real table with the roles of `StandingsTable`, cells are 14 px, and on a phone rows, cards, Show more and the Date heading are at least 44 px; results are never conveyed by colour alone.
- No migration, no new table, no new index, no new dependency; the OpenAPI change is additive.
- `PlayerMatchRowBuilder` is the only place that decides a row's content; it carries no Spring, repository, entity or access information; no figure is computed in the client.
- Own-record games, a player-facing view, export, per-ball detail, new filters and editing are **not** present.

## Open questions for the product owner

Each is a proposal with a recommended default; the owner answers after seeing the mockups. Any answer can still change them.

1. **Row columns on desktop versus phone.** Recommended: **desktop table** with Date, Opponent (team as a sub-line), Competition, Result, **Batting as three columns (Runs, Balls, How out)**, **Bowling as one cell** ("2/21" with "4.2 ov · 1 mdn · 8 dots" under it), **Fielding as one cell** (only non-zero credits), and an Awards column. Batting gets separate columns because most players bat in most matches; Bowling and Fielding are grouped because most players bowl or take a catch in few. A cell with nothing is a dash. **Phone: a stacked card per match** (question 11). Alternatives: (a) every figure its own column (about eleven numeric columns, too wide to read without sideways scrolling even on desktop); (b) a "Batting / Bowling / Fielding" tri-cell for all three, equal and compact but hiding the batting detail the most; (c) a column chooser (more UI, deferred).
2. **A match with a team list but no scorecard.** Recommended: the row is shown (it counts as a match played, as in `103`) with the result, a muted merged note **"No scorecard captured"** across the figure columns, and dashes, never zeros. A match with only one of the two cards shows its figures where captured and dashes elsewhere. A captured card with no row for the player reads "Did not bat" or "Did not bowl". Alternative: hide such rows (rejected: it would make the row count disagree with the Matches figure and hide history).
3. **Derby matches.** Recommended: **one row**, both sides' figures combined, result **"Internal"** (no win or loss, as in `103`), the opponent column reads "Internal match" with both team names, and a muted "Played for both sides" caption when the player is on both. Alternative: two rows (one per side), which would make the row count disagree with `103`'s one-match rule and the Matches figure. A per-side expansion of a single row is deferred.
4. **A totals row. Superseded by decision 4 (no Total row; a tile strip instead).** Originally recommended: a pinned **Total · N matches** row directly under the table header (a **Total** card at the top on a phone) showing the summed runs, balls, fours, sixes, wickets, runs conceded, catches, stumpings, run-outs and awards, **taken from the existing `PlayerStatsDto`** for the same filters (no new endpoint, correct even with one page loaded, and equal to the other tabs by construction). Alternatives: put it at the bottom (invisible until the end of a long list, so rejected); sum only the loaded rows (wrong while paging, rejected); no totals row (leaves the parity unseen).
5. **Sorting.** Recommended: **newest first only, with a reverse** on the Date heading (`direction=asc`, `?dir=asc`, as `103` decision 28 did for the standings). No sort by runs or wickets in version 1 because ordering by a figure interacts with paging and with rows that have no figure (`NO_CARD`); it is the first follow-up if managers ask "show me his best innings".
6. **No-result and abandoned matches with play.** Recommended: the Result badge reads **"No result"** or **"Abandoned"** (neutral tone, not Won/Lost), the row shows the figures (they count, decision 17) and the match counts as played; no extra note, since the badge is already the explanation. Alternative: add a muted "Play took place" caption to reassure a manager who wonders why a washed-out game has figures.
7. **Player of the match markers.** Recommended: a filled trophy icon for the official **Player of the match** and an outlined one for the club team's own **Team player of the match**, each with text in the phone card ("POTM", "Team POTM") and an accessible name in the table; both can show on one row. Alternative: a single star for either (loses the distinction `103` draws on the Awards & record tab).
8. **Paging, "Showing N of M" and the filters.** Recommended: page size **20** (a row is taller than a standings row), Show more appends the next 20, "Showing 20 of 63 matches" uses the server's `totalElements`, and any filter change restarts at the first page (new query key) while the old rows stay dimmed until the new ones arrive. Alternative: 10 per page (as the standings), or a 50-row first page for a desktop only.
9. **Opening the match and coming back.** Recommended: the route is the existing read-only result page **`/manage/results/:matchId`**; the row link passes router state so the result page's back button reads **"Back to Matches"** and returns to the stats URL with the same Season, League, Section, Team and Competition (the page gains `competition` as a link parameter). Show more progress and scroll position are not restored (the list returns to the first page); the browser Back button also works. Alternative: open the scorecard in a new tab (keeps the list, but breaks the single-page flow on a phone).
10. **Export.** Recommended: **deferred.** A CSV of the filtered list is the natural first output but needs its own decision on names, fields and the junior treatment; not in this spec.
11. **Mobile layout.** Recommended: a **stacked card per match** (whole card tappable, 44 px minimum), because this is a history log read top to bottom, not a table to compare players across columns (the reason `103`'s standings scroll sideways with pinned columns). Alternative: the same table as the standings, scrolling sideways with Date and Opponent pinned, which keeps one pattern across the stats pages but hides most figures off-screen on a 375 px phone.
12. **Accessibility and size.** Recommended: `role="table"` structure as `StandingsTable`, 14 px cells, 44 px phone targets, the whole row as the link with a focus ring, result by text and icon (not colour), `aria-sort` on Date. Confirm, or ask for larger text than `103`'s 14 px in this table.
13. **Default page size and the safety cap.** Recommended default 20, maximum 100 (matches `103`'s cap). A career of several hundred matches is reachable only by repeated Show more; confirm that is acceptable or say if a "load all" is wanted (not recommended).
14. **How out in words.** Recommended: the dismissal type only ("caught", "bowled", "lbw", "run out", "not out"), with no bowler or fielder name, which also keeps the endpoint free of other people's names. Alternative: include "c Smith b Jones" as on the scorecard (needs two more names per row and the junior-name rule from `114` for any later player-facing use).
