## Problem & Goals

The Match View page (`036`) is a stack of generic sections (Details, Availability, Home XI, Away XI) on `RecordDetailScreen`. It does not look like the League view the user prefers (`072`), it shows an opponent's empty XI card nobody can act on, and its Availability section is a second, weaker copy of what the poll's own Responses page does. The Edit Match page carries the same Availability data again as a tab with Home/Away sub-tabs, which is where poll links point today, so the "where do I manage this match's availability" answer is split across three places. The match card calls the same destination "Poll" while the pages call it Availability. Finally, a match has no place to record where it is scored or streamed (for example a CricClubs scoring page or a PitchVision stream).

This spec rebuilds the Match View on the League view header, removes the duplicate Availability tab from the Edit page in favour of one Availability button, aligns the card's wording, and adds two optional links to a match. It has a small backend part (two nullable columns) and a frontend part.

**Goals**
- The Match View header mirrors the League view header: Back and the match's badges on the top row, the title (with team logos) and Edit on the title row, a divider, then the match details, optional links and three action buttons (Select team, Availability, Share team sheet).
- The body shows one card per **own** team (a side that is a club `Team`) with its announced badge, "N of M picked" progress and the read-only Playing XI. An opponent that is not one of the club's teams gets no card.
- The Edit Match page loses its Availability tab; it gains one Availability button in its header with the same destination rule as the card and the view page. Its tab order becomes Details, Match Squad, Home XI, Away XI.
- Every link that targeted the removed tab is re-pointed (the new Match View, or the covering poll's own Responses page).
- The match card's footer button is renamed from Poll to Availability, handles a derby's two polls with a small menu, and shows scoring and streaming links as an icon row when set.
- A match can store an optional scoring link and an optional streaming link, edited on the Details tab and shown on the view page header and the card.

## Non-goals

- **A slot-based Selection planner.** Picking several teams' XIs together using availability, Coverage (`074`) and who is already picked elsewhere is a later spec. The user decided selection stays in the match edit screen (the Playing XI tabs) for now; Select team on the view page and the card simply opens it. Listed in Rollout Notes for `docs/roadmap.md`.
- **Poll controls on the edit page or view page.** Open, close, reopen, share invite and answer overrides already exist on the poll's Responses page (`066`/`067`) and the Availability hub; they are not rebuilt. The removed tab's "Open squad poll" prompt is replaced by the Availability button, which, with no poll, opens the New poll flow prefilled for the match (the card's existing no-poll behaviour).
- **Any opponent card or opponent XI.** A side that is not a club team (free-text, league team, or another club's team) has no `MatchSide` the club manages (`029`), so there is nothing to show; its name and logo appear in the title only.
- **Logos on the match card.** The approved design puts logos in the Match View title only. The card header keeps its cricket avatar (`069`).
- **Showing the scoring and streaming links anywhere else.** Public pages, the shared team sheet (PDF and WhatsApp text, `030`) and the schedule shares (`051`) do not show them yet (roadmap).
- **A results or completed status, and match naming.** Still not built (`071` Non-goals); the links are only pointers to an external scorer. Both stay on the roadmap.
- **Normalising a link entered without a scheme.** The form and the server require `http://` or `https://` and report an error; nothing prefixes `https://` silently (see section 6).
- **Link preview, link health checks or embedding a stream.** The links open in a new tab, nothing more.
- **Changing the Match Squad tab, the Playing XI tabs, `MatchForm`'s side fields, Deactivate/Reactivate, or how announcing and team sheets work.**
- **Any other in-app entry to the Match Squad tab.** The "Covered by a group poll" panel disappears with the Availability tab. The Match Squad tab stays a visible top-level tab on the edit page whenever a side is group-covered, `?tab=match-squad&side=` keeps working as a deep link, and the Match View's team card gains one **Pick match squad** button for a group-covered side (section 1b); no further entry points are added.
- **Any change to who may see or edit a match.** Access rules (`035`) are untouched.

## User Stories

- As a manager, I open a match and see, in one header, where it is (When, Venue, League and season), its status badges and the two team names with their logos.
- As a manager, I see at a glance whether each of my teams is announced and whether the poll is open, closed or not set up.
- As a manager, I click Select team, Availability or Share team sheet from the match page and land in the right place, with Availability asking which poll when both my teams in a derby have one.
- As a manager, I see my team's Playing XI and how many players are picked out of the league's XI size, with no card for an opponent I cannot select.
- As a manager of a derby (both sides are my teams), I see both teams' cards side by side.
- As a manager of a match none of my teams plays in, I see the match with the three actions disabled and a clear reason.
- As a manager, I open Edit Match and find Details, Match Squad (when a group poll covers the match), Home XI and Away XI, with one Availability button in the header instead of an Availability tab.
- As a manager, I follow "Open match" from a poll's Responses page and land on the match's view page.
- As a manager, I follow "Already covered by ..." in the New poll flow straight to that poll's Responses page.
- As a manager, I click Availability on a match card and get the same destination as on the pages (including a two-poll choice for a derby).
- As a manager, I record a match's scoring link (for example a CricClubs match page) and streaming link (for example PitchVision), and open them from the match page and the card.
- As a manager, I get a clear error if a link is not a valid http(s) address, and no link is saved silently altered.

## Data Model Changes

Two nullable columns on `match`, one migration, `backend/src/main/resources/db/changelog/v1/037-add-match-scoring-streaming-urls.sql` (latest existing is `036-add-league-team.sql`), included at the end of `db.changelog-master.xml`:

```sql
-- docs/specs/075-match-view-and-edit.md: optional external links for a match (a scoring page, a
-- live stream). Nullable, no default: every existing row keeps both as NULL.
ALTER TABLE match ADD COLUMN scoring_url   VARCHAR(1024);
ALTER TABLE match ADD COLUMN streaming_url VARCHAR(1024);
```

No index, no CHECK constraint (the rule lives in the service, as `homeTeamLogoUrl`'s does). `com.cricketlegend.domain.Match` gains `@Column(name = "scoring_url") private String scoringUrl;` and `@Column(name = "streaming_url") private String streamingUrl;`. The entity is built with `@Builder`; any positional `new Match(...)` all-args call in main or tests is updated if one exists.

**Validation rule (service layer, `ValidationException`, 400)**, one private helper in `MatchServiceImpl` applied to each field in both `create` and `update` before anything is built or saved:

- `null` or blank (whitespace only) is stored as `null` (so a cleared form field clears the link).
- Otherwise the value is trimmed, and: longer than 1024 characters is rejected (`"scoringUrl must be at most 1024 characters"`); a value `java.net.URI` cannot parse (for example containing a space) is rejected; a scheme that is not `http` or `https` (case-insensitive), or a missing host, is rejected (`"scoringUrl must be an http(s) URL"`). Restricting to `http`/`https` also keeps `javascript:` and similar schemes out of anything rendered as a link.
- The trimmed value is what is stored.

`streamingUrl` follows the same rule with its own field name in the message. `PUT` is a full replace (as for every other field), so omitting a field or sending `null` clears it.

## API Contract

No new endpoint. The existing match endpoints (`POST`/`PUT /api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}/matches[/{matchId}]`, `GET` list and single) carry two additive fields. Access is unchanged.

| Endpoint | Access | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `POST .../matches` | as today | `CreateMatchRequest` gains optional `scoringUrl`, `streamingUrl` (appended last); 400 `ValidationException` on a bad value (rule above) |
| `PUT .../matches/{matchId}` | as today | `UpdateMatchRequest` gains the same two optional fields, appended last; same validation |
| `GET .../matches` (list), `GET .../matches/{matchId}`, deactivate/reactivate, previous-matches and every other path returning `MatchDto` | as today | `MatchDto` gains `scoringUrl` and `streamingUrl`, appended last. They are plain stored fields, so every path returns them, not only the list (unlike the list-only `069` fields); MapStruct maps them with no `@Mapping` change |

Java changes, all additive:

- `CreateMatchRequest` / `UpdateMatchRequest`: `String scoringUrl, String streamingUrl` appended after `awayLeagueTeamId`. No bean-validation annotation (the rule is in the service so the message is consistent with the other match 400s).
- `MatchDto`: `String scoringUrl, String streamingUrl` appended after `awayLeagueTeamId`.
- `MatchServiceImpl.enrichList` builds `new MatchDto(...)` positionally: it passes `dto.scoringUrl(), dto.streamingUrl()` as the new last two arguments. `create` sets them on the builder and `update` on the entity from the validated values.
- Every positional constructor call in tests is updated (`MatchServiceImplTest` has roughly 31 `new MatchDto(`/`new CreateMatchRequest(`/`new UpdateMatchRequest(` calls: append `null, null`, or real values where a test is about the links).
- `backend/openapi/openapi.yaml`: additions only, no existing line changed: `scoringUrl` and `streamingUrl` (`type: string`) under `CreateMatchRequest`, `UpdateMatchRequest` and `MatchDto` (and so under `PageMatchDto`'s content, which references `MatchDto`). Regenerated or edited by hand per the repo's practice.

### 1. Match View page (`MatchDetailPage`, route unchanged: `/manage/fixtures/matches/:matchId`)

Hand-built on `PageHeaderBand` in the shape of `LeagueViewLayout` (`072`), not `RecordDetailScreen`. Reads `clubId` from `ManagerHome`'s Outlet context ("Not authorized" `EmptyState` as today); loading renders nothing; an error or missing match keeps today's "Couldn't load this match" `EmptyState`.

**Own team.** A side is an *own team side* when its `homeTeamId` / `awayTeamId` is set (`ownSides(match)`, a new helper in `matchCardHelpers.ts` returning `{ side: 'home' | 'away', teamId }[]`, home first). The page derives this from the ids because `getMatch` does not return the list-only picked counts (`069`). The card keeps its own list-count-based rule unchanged. Edge: a cross-club `Team` id counts as own here, as the Edit page already treats it (its XI tab appears for any side with a team id).

**Data the page loads** (all keys already in use elsewhere, so React Query dedupes): `getMatch`, `listTeamsForClub`, `listLeagues`, `listSeasons`, `listMatchSides` (key `['managed-club', clubId, 'matches', matchId, 'sides']`), `listSquad` per own side (key `['managed-club', clubId, 'teams', teamId, 'seasons', seasonId, 'squad']`), and, for the poll state and the Availability destination, `listPolls` (key `['managed-club', clubId, 'matches', matchId, 'polls']`) plus `getMatchSquad` per own side (key `['managed-club', clubId, 'matches', matchId, 'teams', teamId, 'squad']`, the coverage query `MatchFormPage`'s `useSideCoverage` uses). The old per-poll `getPollResponses` queries and `SideAvailability` are removed.

**Match overlay.** `getMatch` returns `homeSideAnnounced`/`awaySideAnnounced` as `false` and `polls` as `[]` (they are list-only, `040`/`069`). The page builds one overlay object once and feeds it to the existing helpers: `{ ...match, homeSideAnnounced, awaySideAnnounced, polls }` where the announced flags come from each own side's `MatchSide.announced` (sides query) and `polls` from a new helper `matchPollsFrom(ownTeamIds, squadPolls, squadsByTeamId): MatchPoll[]` in `matchCardHelpers.ts`, which mirrors the backend rule from `069`: for each own team, if its match squad has a `windowId` the match is group-covered and contributes one `{ type: 'GROUP', teamId: null, pollId: roundId, roundId, open: windowOpen }` (deduplicated by `roundId`: a group poll covers the match once); otherwise a squad poll from `listPolls` for that team contributes `{ type: 'SQUAD', teamId, pollId, roundId: null, open }`. The poll badge and the Availability button show nothing / stay disabled until the polls and squad queries have settled, so a still-loading page never shows "No poll" or opens the New poll flow for a match that has one.

### 2. Edit Match page (`MatchFormPage`)

**Tabs.** The **Availability** tab and its Home/Away sub-tabs are removed. In edit mode the tab bar is shown when at least one side has a team id (as today), in this order: **Details**, **Match Squad** (only when a side is group-covered, as today), **Home XI**, **Away XI** (each only for a side with a team id, as today). The Playing XI tabs move to the end.

Because Match Squad now sits before the XI tabs and appears only once coverage resolves, numeric tab indices would shift under a user who already clicked an XI tab. The page therefore keys its tabs by **string value** (`'details' | 'match-squad' | 'home-xi' | 'away-xi'`) instead of computed indices: `activeTab` is a string key, each `Tab` has a `value`, and the Save button still shows only on `details`. The index arithmetic and `activeAvailabilitySubTab` state are deleted.

**Deep links** (read once the target tab exists, as today):
- `?tab=playing-xi` selects Home XI, else Away XI when only the away side is a team.
- `?tab=match-squad&side=home|away` selects Match Squad and its Home/Away sub-tab once a side is group-covered.
- `?tab=availability` (with or without `&side=`) is no longer a tab: the page stays on **Details** (it is not redirected and the query string is left alone). Old bookmarks and any not-yet-updated link therefore land somewhere sensible.

**Header Availability button.** `RecordFormScreen` gains an additive optional `headerAction?: ReactNode` (section 7), rendered right-aligned on the title row ("Edit Match" on the left). In edit mode `MatchFormPage` passes the **Availability** button: outlined, `EventAvailableOutlinedIcon`, same destination rule as the card and view page (section 3), disabled with `None of your teams is playing in this match` when neither side has a team id. It is not rendered in create mode. The page already has both sides' coverage and polls (`useSideCoverage`); it builds the same `matchPollsFrom(...)` overlay from them, so no new request is added.

**Dead code removed.** `MatchAvailabilityPanel` (a function inside `MatchFormPage.tsx`) and its imports of `MatchAvailabilityTab`, `EditCloseTimeDialog`, `PollShareDialog`, `createPoll`/`openPoll`/`closePoll`/`setPlayerStatus` go. `components/MatchAvailabilityTab/` (component, test, story, index) has no other consumer and is **deleted**; the build greps first and keeps anything with another importer (`PollShareDialog` and `EditCloseTimeDialog` are used by the poll card and the Squad Responses page, so they stay; `utils/availabilityStatus.ts` and `utils/pollClose.ts` stay if anything else imports them, otherwise they go with it). `getPollResponses`, `listPolls`, `useSideCoverage`, `MatchSideTab`'s tinting and `MatchSquadPanel` stay: the XI tabs still use them.

### 3. One destination rule for Availability (card, view page, edit page)

`pollDestination` (`matchCardHelpers.ts`) is reshaped so the two-poll case no longer returns the removed `?tab=availability`:

```ts
export type PollMenuOption = { label: string; to: string }
export type PollDestination =
  | { kind: 'link'; to: string }
  | { kind: 'menu'; options: PollMenuOption[] }
export function pollDestination(match: Match, teamsById: Map<string, Team>): PollDestination
```

(the `editTo` parameter is dropped: nothing needs the edit route any more.)

- **0 polls:** `link` to the New poll flow prefilled, exactly as today: `/manage/availability/new?type=group[&sectionId=<club side's section>]&matchId=<id>`.
- **1 poll:** `link` to its Responses page: a group poll `/manage/availability/group/<roundId ?? pollId>`, a squad poll `/manage/availability/squad/<matchId>/<pollId>` (unchanged).
- **2 polls** (a derby with a poll per side, or one side group-covered and the other with a squad poll): `menu` with one option per poll, each opening that poll's Responses page. A squad poll's label is `<Team name> · Home poll` or `<Team name> · Away poll` (e.g. "Villagers 1 · Home poll"); a group poll's label is `<covered team name(s)> · Group poll`.

**Shared hook `useAvailabilityNavigation(destination)`** (new, `pages/manage/matches/useAvailabilityNavigation.tsx`): returns `{ openAvailability(event), menu }`. For a `link` it navigates on click; for a `menu` it opens an MUI `Menu` anchored to the clicked button listing the options (each a `MenuItem` that navigates and closes), `aria-label="Availability polls"`. The card, the view page and the edit page header each render `menu` once and call `openAvailability` from their button, so the rule lives in one place. Because the card's button is a `RecordCard` footer button, `RecordCardFooterButton.onClick` gains an optional event argument (section 7).

**Disabled rule and reason** are identical everywhere: disabled with the title `None of your teams is playing in this match` when no side is an own team. (Card: unchanged definition via its picked counts; view and edit pages: via `ownSides`.)

### 4. Re-pointed links (the removed tab's former callers)

- **`squadPollHref`** (`pollHelpers.ts`): now returns `/manage/fixtures/matches/${poll.matchId}` (the Match View). It no longer needs a side, so its argument narrows to `Pick<OpenAvailabilityPoll, 'matchId'>`; its two callers (`SquadPollResponsesPage`'s **Open match** button and `PollMatchesDialog`'s **Open match** link) keep their labels and pass the same data. `squadPollSideLabel` and the other helpers are untouched.
- **`coveredPollHref`** (`pollHelpers.ts`, used by `CoveredByNote`): now points at the covering poll's own Responses page: a `SQUAD` match to `/manage/availability/squad/${match.matchId}/${match.existingPollId}`, a `GROUP` match to `/manage/availability/group/${match.existingPollId}` (for a group poll the backend's `existingPollId` is the round id, confirmed in `SectionAvailabilityFixtureGroupResolverImpl`/`MatchPollCoverageService.Coverage.coveringPollId`). When `existingPollId` is null (it cannot be when `alreadyPolled`, but the type allows it) it falls back to the previous generic target `/manage/availability?showClosed=true`.
- **`pollDestination`'s two-poll case** is the menu above.
- The old "Covered by a group poll" panel's links (`View poll`, `Pick match squad`) disappear with the tab; the Match View's **Pick match squad** button (section 1b) is the in-app way to Match Squad.

### 5. Match card changes (`MatchCard`)

- **Rename:** the footer button **Poll** becomes **Availability** (same `EventAvailableOutlinedIcon`, same equal-column footer, same destination rule via the shared hook, same disabled rule and title); tests and `aria-label` follow. Footer order stays Edit, Select, Availability, Share.

Only the set ones render. The row has `position: 'relative'` so it paints above the card's stretched title link (`059`, same technique as the League card's social row, `071`): clicking an icon opens the link and never navigates the card.

The query keys are the ones the card already uses (sides `['managed-club', clubId, 'matches', matchId, 'sides']`, squads `['managed-club', clubId, 'teams', teamId, 'seasons', seasonId, 'squad']`), which the view page's own sides and squad queries also use, so the dialog opens with data already cached. Behaviour of the dialog from the card is unchanged.

### 6. Match form: the two link fields

`MatchForm`'s Details tab gains two optional `Input`s after Venue: **Scoring link** (helper text "Optional. Link to the match's scoring page, e.g. https://cricclubs.com/matches/34343") and **Streaming link** (helper text "Optional. Link to the live stream, e.g. a PitchVision page, starting with https://"). Plain `Input` text fields (no browser URL validation, `noValidate` stays) kept in form state like `venue`.

**Decision: no silent prefixing.** A value without a scheme (for example `cricclubs.com/matches/34343`) is an error, not corrected: the form shows the error under the field and does not submit. Reason: guessing `https://` can silently store a wrong or broken address, the server enforces the same rule, and it is one extra keystroke to fix. The helper text shows the scheme in its example so the requirement is visible before the error is.

Validation (a pure exported helper `validateMatchLink(value): string | null` in `MatchForm.tsx`, unit-tested): trimmed blank is valid (stored as `null`); otherwise it must match `^https?://\S+$` case-insensitively and be at most 1024 characters. Errors: "Enter a valid link starting with http:// or https://" and "Link must be 1024 characters or fewer". On submit the payload sends the trimmed value or `null`. `MatchFormPage` passes `scoringUrl`/`streamingUrl` from the loaded match as `initialValues`; the payload flows through `saveMutation` unchanged (a server 400 still shows through `errorDetail`).

### 7. Shared and API-layer changes (all additive)

- **`ui/src/api/matchApi.ts`:** `Match` gains `scoringUrl?: string | null` and `streamingUrl?: string | null` (optional so older fixtures stay valid, as `homeLeagueTeamId` is); `MatchPayload` gains the same two.
- **`RecordFormScreen`:** new optional `headerAction?: ReactNode`. The title row becomes a wrapping `space-between` flex row with the title left and `headerAction` right; with the prop omitted the markup is byte-for-byte unchanged. Component test and story updated.
- **`RecordCardFooterButton.onClick`:** type widens from `() => void` to `(event: React.MouseEvent<HTMLElement>) => void`. Existing callers ignore the argument, so nothing else changes; the `RecordCard` test gains a case asserting the event is passed.
- **`matchCardHelpers.ts`:** adds `ownSides`, `matchPollsFrom`, `matchLeagueValue`, `pickedLegend`, `announcedBadge(announced, prefix)` (the one-badge piece `announcedBadges` is now built from), the shared `NO_CLUB_TEAM_REASON`, and the reshaped `pollDestination`. `withPlayingXiTab` stays (the card's Select uses it).
- **Removed:** `MatchAvailabilityPanel` and `components/MatchAvailabilityTab/` (section 2). `AvailabilityRespondentAvatars` stays (the Availability dashboard and the Responses pages use it); the build confirms with a grep.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The Match View page's header has, on its top row, Back to Matches on the left and the match's colour-coded badges right-aligned (an announced badge per own team, prefixed with the team name only when both sides are own; Poll open / closed / No poll; Inactive), and on its title row the team names with logos (initials when missing; the cricket avatar when neither side has a logo) with Edit right-aligned on the same row; then a divider, the When / Venue / League details with icons (empty ones omitted), any Scoring / Watch live links, and the Select team, Availability and Share team sheet buttons.
- The separate Details card and the Availability section no longer exist on the Match View page.
- Select team opens the edit page's Playing XI tab. Availability opens the prefilled New poll flow with no poll, that poll's Responses page with one, and a small menu ("Villagers 1 · Home poll", "Villagers 2 · Away poll") with two. Share team sheet opens the same Team Sheet dialog as the card, through one shared hook. All three are disabled with "None of your teams is playing in this match" when neither side is a club team.
- The page shows one card per own team with the team name and Home/Away, the announced badge, "N of M picked" with a progress bar (no bar without a league) and the read-only Playing XI, or "No players selected yet." with a Select team button. With one own team there is exactly one card and no opponent card; with both sides own there are two cards side by side (stacked on a phone); with neither there are no cards.
- On a 375px screen the header stacks as Back, badges, title row (with Edit), divider, details one per line, then three equal-width actions; nothing is clipped and the page never scrolls horizontally.
- The Edit Match page has no Availability tab; its tabs are Details, Match Squad (only when a group poll covers a side), Home XI, Away XI. Its header has one Availability button, outlined with an icon, with the same destination rule and disabled reason as the view page. `?tab=playing-xi` and `?tab=match-squad&side=` still open their tabs; `?tab=availability` opens Details.
- "Open match" on a squad Responses page and in the Matches dialog opens the Match View. "Already covered by ..." in the New poll flow opens the covering poll's Responses page.
- The match card's footer button reads Availability (not Poll) with the same icon, destination and disabled rule, and a derby's two polls open a menu rather than the removed tab.
- A match can have an optional scoring link and an optional streaming link, each an `http(s)://` URL of at most 1024 characters. The form rejects a value without a scheme (no silent prefixing) and a value over the limit; the server rejects the same with a 400 and stores a blank as `null`.
- The links show on the Match View header (Scoring, Watch live) and as an icon row on the card, only when set, opening in a new tab; clicking a card icon never opens the card.
- `scoringUrl` and `streamingUrl` are returned on the matches list, the single match and every other match response; migration `037` leaves them `null` for every existing match; `openapi.yaml` gains the two fields with additions only.
- No dead code from the removed tab remains (`MatchAvailabilityPanel`, `MatchAvailabilityTab`), and nothing else that imported them breaks.
- On the Match View, a team card whose side is covered by a group poll has a **Pick match squad** button that opens that side's Match Squad tab; no other card has it.
