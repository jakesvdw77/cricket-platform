## Problem & Goals
The Seasons pages (`SeasonList`, `SeasonDetailPage`, `SeasonFormPage`, `seasons/SeasonTable`, `seasons/SeasonCard`) were built under spec 094 before the page standard was written down. They differ from the Leagues pages the owner has accepted as the model: the list has a floating filter bar, a separate "Showing N seasons" line, a raised table with a chevron on every row, raised cards with a footer action bar and no Add card; the season page has the old top row with a filled Edit button, icon-tile figures, a raised progress card and raised info cards; the form uses a bare Active switch in the footer and sentence-case labels ("Save changes", "Create season").

**Goals**
- The Seasons list works exactly like the Leagues list: toolbar fused into the table card (a standalone flat toolbar card above the Cards view), one kebab per row and card, flat cards on the dynamic grid ending in a dashed Add Season card, no chevron, no count text, Title Case labels, compact counters that remain quick filters.
- The season page works like the league page: Back alone on the top row, the title row with the season label and its status badge and ONE filled "Manage Season" menu, figures as compact static counter cards, flat info cards, return navigation on every link that leaves the page.
- The season form is compact like the league edit Details card, with a footer of Cancel, Save Changes and Deactivate or Reactivate.
- Back, Cancel and Save honour `returnTo` (`127`) on the form and the season page.

## Non-goals
- **No backend change, no migration, no new endpoint** (UI only). The existing `GET/POST/PUT .../seasons`, `deactivate`, `reactivate` and `GET .../seasons/summary` are used as they are. There is still no single-season GET, so the season page and form keep finding the season in the club's list (`029`).
- **No change to what a season is:** the status vocabulary (Current, Upcoming, Past, Inactive), how it is derived, the fields, the validation (end not before start) and the saved payload stay as `094` set them.
- **No Season pill on the Seasons list or on the season page or form** (the pages are the seasons; `094` and `page-anatomy.md` section 8 "Still open").
- **No mockup** (owner decision): the visual result is whatever the Leagues list and league page already show, so no new visual design is introduced.
- **No Share action for seasons.** There is no season share content, so the Manage Season menu has no Share item (the "Share last" rule has nothing to apply to).
- **No season rollover, no overlap or "one current season" rules, no season-scoped Leagues or Matches lists** (the Leagues and Matches pages still do not read a season from the address; roadmap items from `094`).
- **No change to `PageCounters`, `ManageMenu`, `ToolbarCard`, `FilterBar` or `AddCard` internals.** The pages compose them as the Leagues pages do; a change to a shared component is a separate decision.
- **No pagination** (a club's seasons are a small, bounded list, `029`, unchanged).
- **No breadcrumbs** (`127` "Later").
- **No other page is brought in line here** (Matches, Teams and the rest keep their "Not reviewed" rows).

## User Stories
- As a club admin, I use the Seasons list the same way as the Leagues list: search and sort in one toolbar that is part of the table card, Cards or List, one Actions button per season.
- As a club admin, I click a counter (Current, Upcoming, Past, Inactive) to filter the list, and click it again to clear it.
- As a club admin, I add a season from the filled Add Season button or from the dashed Add Season card at the end of the Cards view.
- As a club admin, on a season's page I see Back alone at the top, the season label and its status badge, and one "Manage Season" menu holding what I can do with the season.
- As a club admin, I see the season's Starts, Ends, Length and Matches as compact figure cards, and the Dates and In This Season cards below.
- As a club admin, I edit a season on one compact card with Cancel, Save Changes and Deactivate (or Reactivate) in its footer.
- As a club admin, when I open a season, its form or the Leagues and Matches pages from somewhere in the app, Back and Cancel return me there, and Save returns me there too.

## UI Requirements
Composes existing components only (`page-anatomy.md` section 8: consistency over cleverness). Where a Leagues counterpart exists, the Seasons page uses the same component with the same props pattern.

### Seasons list (`SeasonList`, `SeasonTable`, `SeasonCard`)
**Header (section 1).** `ManageScreenHeader` with the page name "Seasons" (uppercase page-name style) and the one filled compact **Add Season** button right-aligned on the title row (`flex: none`, full width on a phone). No Season pill, no Back. 16 px between bands on desktop, 12 px on a phone (section 8; the current 24 px gap goes). `Add Season` carries `returnTo` (below).

**Counters (section 2, `094`).** `PageCounters density="compact"`: Current (shown as the season's label, or "None"), Upcoming, Past, Inactive. They stay quick filters (`kind: 'filter'`): mutually exclusive, per visit, click the active one to clear it, a zero counter is a plain card unless active, Inactive takes the warning tone above zero, the figures are computed client-side and are not narrowed by search or Show Inactive (all as `094`). The "FILTER" tag does not appear (it is already removed app-wide): a pointer, a small lift on hover and a green ring when selected. No icons.

**Toolbar (section 3).** The one `FilterBar` as in `LeagueList` (`density="compact" bare inCard viewControlsInline`), search "Search by label" (client-side, as now), no select (a season has no format or section). On one row: search, then on the right the **Show Inactive** toggle chip (`ToolbarToggleChip`), then **Sort** (`ToolbarSortButton`, options Newest First, Oldest First, Name A to Z; Newest First the default) and **Cards | List** last (`ListViewToggle variant="toolbar"`). Tablet: the switch folds into the Filters sheet before the pair; phone: Filters and Search share the row, everything else (the toggle first) is in the sheet, the Quick filter row with Clear stays in the sheet as on Leagues. In List view the toolbar is the top of the table card (fused, `toolbar` prop on `SeasonTable`, hairline below, the empty message replaces the rows inside the card so the toolbar never remounts); in Cards view it is a standalone `ToolbarCard` above the grid, which also holds the empty message.
- The line "Showing N seasons · Current" (`ContentControlsLine` `scope`) is **removed**; no count text anywhere. The standalone `SortMenu`/`ContentControlsLine` use on this page is removed with it.
- The view stays remembered (`seasonList:view`); the quick filter, search, sort and Show Inactive stay per visit.

**List view (`SeasonTable`, sections 4 and 6).** One flat card (hairline border, no shadow; the current `boxShadow: 2` goes): toolbar, hairline, header row with the soft green tint, sticky, zebra with the first data row white and the second tinted (the parity is swapped from today's `nth-of-type(odd)` tint). Columns: Season, Dates, Status, Leagues, Teams, Matches, then a narrow actions column holding the kebab; **no chevron** (the visible `ChevronRightOutlinedIcon` and its column header "Open" go). The whole row opens the season (the stretched link keeps the row's accessible name), hover tint and pointer, "-" for a missing figure. A phone keeps Season (the status badge under the name), Dates and the kebab (the 24 px chevron column becomes the 44 px touch target of the kebab).

**Cards view (`SeasonCard`, sections 4 and 6).** `RecordCard flat`, with the hairline and the hover lift, the same parts for every status as `094` requires (calendar tile, label, ONE status badge below the title, the written date range with the length, the time strip, the Progress row showing "-" when not current, the Leagues, Teams and Matches figures). The **footer bar (Matches, Leagues, Edit) is removed** (`footerActions={null}`); the card carries one kebab in its top-right corner (`headerActions`). The grid is `dynamicCardGridSx` and ends with a dashed `AddCard` "Add Season" (same route as the title-row button, with `returnTo`; with no cards it is the only card).

**Kebab menu (section 6, `ManageMenu trigger="kebab" dense`).** One component for row and card, built like `LeagueRowMenu` (`SeasonRowMenu`, in `pages/manage/seasons/`): label and accessible name "Actions for {season label}", a dense list, icon and Title Case label per item, no descriptions, never navigates the row. Items, in order: **Open Season**, **Edit Season**, **Open Leagues**, **Open Matches**. (Deactivate and Reactivate stay on the edit page, `038`.) Open Leagues and Open Matches open the unfiltered lists exactly as the card's footer buttons did (the lists do not read a season from the address).

**Empty states.** As `LeagueList`: the empty message sits inside the fused card (List) or under the toolbar card (Cards), with the Seasons brand icon (`BrandIcon name="nav/season-cricket" size={48}`). Messages unchanged in meaning: "No seasons yet" (Create your club's first season to get started.), "No matching seasons" for a search or a quick filter, "No inactive seasons" when the Inactive filter finds none. The Add Season button inside the "No seasons yet" message is removed (the title row has Add Season and the Cards view has the dashed card), following the Leagues list.

**Return navigation (section 7a).** Add Season (button and card), Open Season (row, card, kebab), Edit Season and the Leagues and Matches destinations are built with `withReturnTo(destination, currentOrigin(location), 'Seasons')`.

### Season page (`SeasonDetailPage`)
**Skeleton (section 1).** Top to bottom, 16 px between bands (12 px on a phone):
1. **Top row:** Back alone on the left, quiet, "Back to Seasons" by default; with a valid `returnTo` it reads "Back to {label}" and goes there (`useBackLink('/manage/fixtures/seasons', 'Back to Seasons')`). The filled Edit button on this row is removed.
2. **Title row:** the season label as the record name (display face, 32 px, 26 px on a phone, weight 700, keeping its capitals, `pageTitleSx` with the transform removed; replaces today's 1.75 rem `h4`), then its **one status badge inline** (Current, Upcoming, Past or Inactive, `badgeSx`). No logo or avatar, no Season pill (the page is the season). Right-aligned on the same row: **ONE filled "Manage Season" `ManageMenu`** (a popover from `sm`, a bottom sheet on a phone; full width on a phone, wrapping under the title on a narrow screen). It is the only menu on the page, so the equal-width rule has nothing to match; it takes `MANAGE_BUTTON_WIDTH` like Manage League so the two pages' buttons look the same.
3. **Figures:** one row of **static compact `PageCounters` cards** (no icons, no FILTER tag, no pointer): **Starts**, **Ends**, **Length**, **Matches**, with the values from the existing helpers (`formatSeasonDate`, `seasonLength`, the summary's `matchCount`, "-" while the summary loads or has failed). The icon-tile `KeyFigureTile` strip goes. The page keeps hiding nothing on summary failure other than showing "-" for Matches (as today).
4. **Progress (current season only):** the "Day n of m" `SelectionGauge` as today, drawn as a **flat** panel with the hairline border (no shadow; today it is `boxShadow: 1`).
5. **Info cards:** **Dates** (Label, Start Date, End Date, Status) and **In This Season** (Leagues, Teams Entered, or "Nothing entered in this season yet") side by side from `md`, equal height, **flat with the hairline** (see Documentation gaps: `InfoCard` has no flat option today). The card's two cross-link buttons become text-only outlined buttons, **View Leagues** and **View Matches** (Title Case, no leading icons: icons only on Add, Edit and Share), inside the card.

**Manage Season menu** (`groups`: one group "Season"): **Edit Season** (first item, to `.../edit`), **View Leagues**, **View Matches**. No Share item (Non-goals). Each item that leaves the page is a link built with `withReturnTo(destination, currentOrigin(location), '{label} season')`.

**Return navigation (section 7a).** Every link that leaves the page carries the page as its origin: Edit Season (menu item and nothing else, the corner Edit is gone), View Leagues and View Matches (menu and card). The origin label is the season label plus the word "season" (kept within 60 characters, plain text). The page itself reaches its Back through `useBackLink`; it never copies its own `returnTo` forward (`127`).

### Season form (`SeasonFormPage`, `SeasonForm`)
**Shell.** `RecordFormScreen` as today with the title "Add Season" or "Edit Season" (page-name style), Back "Back to Seasons" by default and "Back to {label}" with a valid `returnTo` (the header does this on its own). No Season pill (the page is the season). The old `RecordStatusToggle`-style bare Active switch in the footer is removed.

**Card.** One flat white card, hairline border: the compact `FormSectionHeading` "Season Dates" (24 px icon, `compact`), then **Label, Start Date, End Date on a three-column grid** (two at `sm`, one on a phone), 36 px fields (`utils/compactFieldSx`, applied through a wrapper so the shared `Input` is unchanged), 16 px gaps, "(optional)" rules and placeholders instead of helper text; an end date before the start still shows inline under End Date. Fields, validation and payload are unchanged.

**Footer (one band, one filled button).** Cancel (white outlined, compact), **Save Changes** (edit) or **Create Season** (add), the filled one; and on edit only **Deactivate** (or **Reactivate** for an inactive season), outlined, from the existing deactivate and reactivate mutations (`038`, moved from the Active switch). The error text sits in the footer as today. Labels are Title Case (today's "Save changes" and "Create season" change).

**Return navigation.** Back, Cancel, and after a successful Save and after a successful Deactivate or Reactivate, the page goes to the origin through `useAfterSave('/manage/fixtures/seasons')`: the `returnTo` when present, else the Seasons list (today it stays on the form after a toggle; see Documentation gaps).
