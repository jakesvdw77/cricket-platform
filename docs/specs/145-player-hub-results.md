# 145: Player Hub Results and Stats: the experience that sells the app to parents (Schedule > Results first, on a player-facing stats foundation)

**Status:** approved 2026-11-06 (flow, structure, scope, charts, names, flag default decided in mockup rounds 1 to 4 and the owner's answers; the remaining open questions take their defaults: a five-result form strip, matches the player did not play are not listed, the hero's figures are plain cards until the Stats section exists, one PR for backend and frontend). Not built yet. Requested by the owner: add results to the Player view, showing the matches played and the player's recent results, in a striking, mobile-first experience that sells the app to parents; a drillable Player and Team stats section follows on the same foundation. **Recommendation followed: one spec**, because the effort is in the player-facing access and privacy rule over the stats reads, which Results and Stats both need; Results is the first and smallest screen on it. The Stats screens (`stats.mine`, `stats.teams`) stay "Soon" and get their own later spec that only adds UI.
**Depends on:** `121` (Hub menu, `hubNav.ts`, `MyPlayerAccess`, phone rules), `135` (Schedule > Matches, the Hub match row, `MyMatchService`, the Hub privacy rule and the dark console tokens `HUB`), `115` (the player match list: `PlayerMatchListService`, `PlayerMatchRowDto`, its Privacy section and decision H, "the later player view is a new controller and access rule over the same service"), `103` and `117` (the counting rules, `PlayerStatsDto`, `PlayerDashboardDto`, `FormStrip`), `114` and `122` (`shareName` and the junior rule), `143` (page anatomy, where the Hub differs).

## Problem & Goals
A parent or player in the Player Hub can see what is coming (Schedule > Matches, `135`) but not what has happened: **Schedule > Results** is a "Soon" item ("Published results for this player's teams"). The data exists and is already counted correctly for managers: `GET .../players/{playerId}/stats/matches` returns one row per counted match with the result from the player's side and the player's own batting, bowling and fielding. It has no player-facing route.

**Goals**
- **A. Schedule > Results goes live:** the matches the selected player has played, newest first, each with the team's result and the player's own figures, plus a recent-form strip at the top.
- **B. One player-facing foundation for stats:** a `MePlayersController` family of reads, guarded by `MyPlayerAccess.resolve`, that wraps the existing `103`, `115` and `117` services with no second copy of the counting rules, and a Hub privacy rule applied once. Results uses the matches read now; `Stats > My stats` and `Team stats` will use the other two later.
- **C. Built to be drilled into later:** the Results header tiles and the form strip are the entry points of the later Stats section, so nothing built here is thrown away.

## Non-goals
- **The Stats screens.** `stats.mine`, `stats.boards`, `stats.teams` stay Soon. This spec adds no Stats UI and no ranking; leaderboard visibility for players keeps the open questions of `103` decision 6.
- **Any change to the counting rules, the manager stats pages or the manager Matches list.** No schema change.
- **Sharing, export, live scoring.** As `135`.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-06)
1. **Names are shown.** The Player Hub is for authenticated users, so the Hub Result page shows the full scorecard with names; the earlier "no other person's name" rule of this draft is dropped (it was this draft's choice, not an owner rule).
2. **A flag publishes a result to the player view.** A result reaches the Player Hub only when a manager has switched it on (**Show In Player Hub**). Publishing the result to the club (`published_at`) and showing it to players are two separate steps, so a manager can capture and publish a result for the club's own pages and check it before parents see it.
3. **SUPERSEDED by Decision 11 (round 5).** *The flow was hero plus carousel (pattern C; owner, 2026-11-06, after mockup rounds 1b and 3).** The owner first chose the full-screen swipe deck (A), then found the half-drawn next card at its edge distracting, and chose the carousel. The page is: a **hero** (the player's season at a glance, the last-five streak), then a **carousel strip** of cards inside a normal scrolling page (one idea per card: the latest result, each earlier match, a milestone, and later the stats cards), with a position counter ("1 / 6") and dots, then **All Cards**, a list that jumps to a card. The player leads. On a desktop the hero and the list sit on the left and the selected card is large on the right with arrows, the counter and Left and Right keys. Patterns A and B are dropped. Build in slices: the Results page first, then the Player and Team stats cards on the same carousel.*
4. **Scope (owner, 2026-11-06):** the page can be filtered by **Season, Team and League**. The scope is shown in the page header and, for a player with several teams, in team tabs (Decision 5): `‹ Results   2026 ▾`, then the tabs; the League filter is a sheet (three selects in the header on a desktop). The hero, the cards and the list follow it; every card carries a small scope label so a screenshot is never misleading; a filter offers only values the player has data for (a one-team player sees no Team filter); defaults are the current season, all teams, all leagues, remembered per player on the device; a combination with no matches shows "No matches for this filter" with a Clear Filters link. The Stats section will reuse the same scope. Supersedes the "Season control" suggestion below and open question 5. The backend already supports it (`seasonId`, `leagueId`, `teamId` on the `115` list).
5. **The Hub structure is round 4 (owner, 2026-11-06, `docs/mockups/player-hub-results-round4-mockup.html`):**
   - **Results is a main menu item** (a leaf: it opens straight onto the page, no sub-menu), **second in the menu after Availability**. The Hub's main menu stays at **seven items**: Availability, Results, Schedule, Stats, Updates, Club, Profile.
   - **Account moves to the header avatar** (sign out, switch player, find players keep their behaviour). This changes `121`'s main-menu list, `hubNav.ts` and the unit test that pins exactly seven main items and two levels (the new list still satisfies both).
   - **Schedule > Results is removed** (Schedule keeps Matches, Leagues, Team sheets). **Stats stays a group** (My stats, Team stats, Leaderboards) and uses the same page frame as Results.
   - **The scope is progressive (replaces the single scope chip of Decision 4):** a **Season** dropdown in the header, always; **Team tabs** (All, then each team) **only for a player with more than one team**; a **League filter** button beside the tabs (it opens a bottom sheet) with a dot when set. A player with one team and one league sees only the Season dropdown. Defaults and the rest of Decision 4 (scope labels on every card, only values with data, remembered per player, "No matches for this filter" with Clear Filters) stand.
6. **The carousel shows no part of the next card (owner, 2026-11-06, after trying round 4 on a phone).** Each card is full width with a gap wider than the page margin. The cues that there is more are: the **counter** ("1 / 6") with **previous and next arrow buttons** beside it, the **dots**, the **"Next: <card> ›" label** at the foot of every card, swipe, and the Left and Right keys. If parents miss it, add a one-time nudge before bringing a peek back.
6a. **Copy never says "you" or "your" (owner, 2026-11-06).** The Hub is read by parents, agents and managers, never only by the player, so every label and sentence names the player by first name ("Sam batted", "Sam's Game", "Sam's story is just starting"); the fallback when no first name is known is "This Player".
6b. **Filter order is Season, League, then Team (owner, 2026-11-06, round 5).** Teams are registered in leagues, so a parent picks the league first and the Team chips then offer only the teams that played in that league (a team that is not in a newly chosen league resets to All). Supersedes the Team-first tab order of Decision 5. The filters read (`/results/filters`) will need to return which teams played in which league (a list of league and team pairs) so the cascade needs no extra request; round 5 (`docs/mockups/player-hub-results-round5-mockup.html`) is the reference for the page, which leads with the latest result, a recent form card, the filter chips and a responsive grid of wide result cards (no carousel).
11. **The Results page is round 5 (owner, 2026-11-06, `docs/mockups/player-hub-results-round5-mockup.html`; "immediately feels like a results page, not a player stats page").** It replaces the hero, carousel, All Cards list, section arrows and the two-column layout of Decisions 3 and 6. Top to bottom: the Season dropdown in the header; two rows of **equal-size filter chips**, **Leagues** first (All Leagues, each league played that season, Friendly when there are friendlies) and then **Teams** (only the teams that played in the chosen league; a team not in a newly chosen league resets to All), each chip two lines (name, then "N played · N won"); a **Recent Form** card (the last five results of the filtered scope as W, L, T chips oldest to newest, each with the player's figure and a three letter opponent code, and a sentence such as "2 won, 3 lost in the last 5"); the **latest result** as a large banner (result line, both scores, league and team, the player's contribution as a strip); then the earlier results as a **responsive grid of wide cards** (`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(310px, 1fr))`: one column on a phone, two or three on wider screens; **auto-fit, so the cards always fill the row: one card takes the entire width and two cards share it, never a lone card in a corner or a cramped pair**; the content uses the full width). **The result leads, not the player's figures:** every card's headline is the match (result line, both scores, league, team); the player's contribution is a slim strip at the foot ("<First name> batted 19, off 15 balls, 4 fours", a star for Player of the match). No carousel, no counter, no arrows, no dots, no browser scrollbar (hidden on the Hub console, scrolling still works). A card is one tap target to the Hub Result page. Empty and filtered-empty states as before ("No results yet", "No matches for this filter" with Clear Filters). Filters live in the URL (`season`, `league`, `team`, `competition`), saved per player as the fallback.
12. **The desktop menu collapses to one button after 10 seconds (owner, 2026-11-06).** On a wide screen, about 10 seconds after a Hub page loads (not while the pointer is over the menu or focus is inside it) the menu collapses into **one menu button at the left of the header band** and the content takes the whole width (the first version, a 64 px icon rail, repeated one icon per page and was dropped). **Hovering, clicking or focusing that button fades the full menu in as a panel over the content** (a slide in from the left edge over about 520 ms, no reflow) and it fades out shortly after the pointer leaves; choosing a page closes it; a pin button ("Keep Menu Open") returns the menu to the layout and a "Collapse Menu" button collapses it by hand. The in-layout to collapsed change is **a dissolve, not an animated resize** (the page fades to its background in about 240 ms, the layout swaps while covered, and it fades back over about 760 ms), instant with reduced motion. It applies to every Hub page (the shared frame), only from `md` up and only on a content page (the phone is unchanged); the duration is the named constant `MENU_COLLAPSE_MS`; a stored preference (`hub:menuAutoCollapse`, default on) is read now so a Settings switch can come later, no switch is built here.
13. **The objective: a game feel, a scoreboard or a TV broadcast (owner, 2026-11-06).** The whole idea behind the Player Hub layout is to give it a game feel: the view area should feel like a scoreboard or something broadcast on television. That is why it asks for the full use of the space; where the console frame cannot give that, the Hub should go full screen. **Every component designed for the Hub (cards, banners, chips, drawings, menu, motion) is judged against this objective first:** bold, high contrast, big legible type, a clear headline per tile (the result before the player's figures), purposeful motion, no timid narrow columns, the menu stepping aside (decision 12) so the content owns the screen. **To discuss:** check what percentage of a large screen the Hub uses today (the console frame is capped and centred, leaving wide margins on a big display) and whether it should grow or go full screen; not decided here.
14. **The filters are tucked away (owner, 2026-11-06, round 6, `docs/mockups/player-hub-results-round6-mockup.html`).** The always-visible chip rows of decision 11 are replaced by **one Filters button** in the header beside the Season dropdown (a gold dot when a league or team is chosen). It opens a **side panel docked on the right on a wide screen (from `lg`)**, which narrows the grid instead of covering it, **where the choices apply as you tap** (Reset and Done buttons; Escape or Done closes it), and a **dark bottom sheet below `lg`** whose button reads "Show N matches". Chosen filters show as **small removable tags** under the header (only while something is filtered). A player with **one league and one team** (nothing to choose) gets **no Filters button**: a quiet line names the league and the team. The League then Team cascade (6b), the equal-size two-line chips and the Friendly option are unchanged, now inside the panel or sheet.
15. **The Hub header band belongs to the page (owner, 2026-11-06).** The fixed band across the top of the Player Hub console, which today only says "Player Hub" and wastes the space, becomes the page's non-scrolling header on EVERY Hub page: the page title on the left (Results, Availability, Matches, Leagues, ...) and that page's controls on the right (Results: the Season dropdown and the Filters button; Leagues: its Season select). The words "Player Hub" go. Pages declare their header through one shared piece (`HubPageHeader`, drawn into the band when inside the console frame and inline when not, so standalone page tests keep working); the in-content page headings go. The scrolling content starts below the band, so the title and controls never scroll away. Whether the manager application should follow the same idea is a separate decision.
7. **Juniors are shown with full names on the Hub scorecard (owner, 2026-11-06).** The Hub is for authenticated users, so the platform's junior initials rule (`114`, `122`) does not apply to the Hub Result page: every player's name is shown in full, juniors included. **Consistency note:** `135`'s Hub match page still shows juniors as initials in an announced XI (its Privacy section); the owner may want that aligned later, which would be a change to `135`, not made here.
8. **The Show In Player Hub flag is off for every result, new and existing, with no backfill (owner, 2026-11-06).** A manager switches results on one at a time; there is no club default and no bulk action in this spec (a club-wide setting or a "show all published results" action can be added later if the backlog proves painful).
9. The other open questions (2 and 4 to 9 below; 3, 3b and 5 are answered by the Decisions) are answered separately.

## Design direction (owner, 2026-11-06): striking, mobile first, one idea per screen
Results and the Player and Team stats are what **sells the application to parents** (Availability stays the most important function and is already covered). So these pages are **not** the manager console in a dark skin:
1. **Striking, not dense.** Bigger charts, far fewer per page: **one idea per screen** (a hero number or chart, one line of words, nothing else competing). No tables of figures as the first thing a parent sees.
2. **A way to flip.** The content is a **carousel of cards the user flips through** (swipe on a phone, arrows and the keyboard on a desktop) under a hero, with a position counter and dots (Decision 3). Each card is one result, one chart or one milestone. The same carousel is the model for Results now and for Player and Team stats later.
3. **Mobile first.** Designed at 375 px first, thumb reach, 44 px targets, swipe with a visible fallback (buttons), no horizontal page scroll; the desktop is the same deck at a larger size (two panes where it helps), not a different design.
4. **Mockups first, several rounds.** The owner is happy to iterate. No build starts until a mockup direction is approved; every round is an HTML mockup in `docs/mockups/` (and an artifact for review on a phone). See "Mockup rounds".
5. **The chart technology is settled** (see "Charts"): `@mui/x-charts` for standard charts, our own small SVG for the bespoke Results drawings, Nivo eliminated, no third library without the owner's approval.
6. **Shareable later (idea, not built here):** each card is designed so it can become a share image (the `114` pipeline), because a proud parent sharing a card is the best advertisement.

### The cards (suggested; the mockup rounds test them)
A player's **Story** is a hero and a carousel of cards, newest first, each card one idea:
- **Hero card:** the season in one glance for this player (big number and one sentence: "7 matches, 212 runs, 9 wickets"), the team's season as a ring (Played, Won, Lost), the last five results as a streak.
- **Latest result card:** the last match, the score as a bold result banner, the player's own performance as one big figure ("46* off 68") with a one-line story ("top scorer in the win"), the award star if any.
- **One card per recent result:** the same shape, swipe back in time.
- **Stat cards (the later Stats section reuses the same deck):** Runs through the season (a bold line), Batting positions or scoring zones, Wickets and economy, Catches, Personal bests, Form vs the team average (never ranked against other players), Team season (results ring, run rate), Milestones ("first fifty").
- **Index:** a compact grid or list of all cards to jump to one (the fallback to flipping).

## What the player sees (suggestions, for the owner to pick from; the deck above is the presentation, this is the content)
**Schedule > Results page** (dark console, `HUB` tokens, rows follow `135` section 0: one tap target, 44 px, no chevron, no kebab):

1. **Season at a glance** (four compact static tiles, no ranking): **Played**, **Won**, **Runs** or **Wickets** for the player's role?, **Catches**. Suggested set: Played, Won, Runs, Wickets (a parent wants "how is the team doing" and "what has my child done"). Each tile later drills into `stats.mine` (a link target reserved in `hubNav.ts`, not shown now).
2. **Recent form strip:** the last five results as W / L / T chips in date order (the team's result) with the player's own score under each ("46*", "1/24"), built from `117`'s `FormStrip`. Honest about gaps: a match with no scorecard shows the result and a dash.
3. **Matches played list** (the main content), newest first, a Show More button (backend paged, as `115`):
   - date, the team the player played for (a chip when the player has more than one team), "v Opponent", competition or Friendly;
   - the result in words from the player's side ("Won by 8 wickets"), with a Won, Lost, Tied, No Result chip;
   - the player's own line ("46* off 68 · 1 for 24 · 1 catch"; "Did not bat" or "No scorecard captured" in muted words, never zeros) and a player-of-the-match star;
   - the row opens the Hub **Result page** (below).
4. **Scope control:** superseded by Decision 4 (the scope chip: Season, Team and League).
5. **Empty and error states:** "No results yet. Finished matches will appear here." and the standard Try Again.

**Hub Result page** (`/account/players/:key/results/:matchId`): the result line and both scores, date, league and venue (the `135` match header and figures, static compact), the held player's own card highlighted first, then the **full scorecard** of both innings with names (Decision 1: batting with how out, bowling, extras, fall of wickets only if the manager's result page shows it, the awards), and a footer Back to Results. Only results with **Show In Player Hub** on are reachable and listed (Decision 2); any other result is a 404.

## Data model and the manager control (Decision 2)
- **Column:** `match_result.visible_to_players boolean not null default false`, plus `visible_to_players_at timestamptz` and `visible_to_players_by` (who and when), in the next sequential Liquibase migration (`new-migration` skill). Existing published results are **not** backfilled (default off: nothing becomes visible to parents by the upgrade).
- **Manager UI:** on the result page, **Show In Player Hub** and **Hide From Player Hub** in the **Manage Result** menu (after the status transitions, `page-anatomy.md` section 6b), each with a one-line explanation; a published result shows a small badge ("In Player Hub") beside the Published badge when on. The Capture wizard's Review step offers the same switch beside Publish (off by default, "Parents and players can see this result"). A draft or unpublished result cannot be shown to players (the item is disabled with its reason).
- **Rule:** a result is visible to players only when it is published (`published_at` set) **and** the flag is on; unpublishing a result also turns the flag off. Importing a result (`104`) sets nothing: it stays off until a manager switches it on.
- **API:** `POST` and `DELETE` `/api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}/results/{resultId}/player-visibility` (club admin or the result's section manager, as publish), audited like publish; the result DTO gains `visibleToPlayers`.

## Charts (decided 2026-11-06)
**Decision (owner):** **Nivo is eliminated.** The chart rules for the Player Hub, kept deliberately small so the project does not accumulate libraries:
1. **Charts with axes, several series, hover or a legend** (the Stats section's typical charts) use **`@mui/x-charts`** (v7, already approved and tested for the manager Dashboard), composed from its parts and styled to the Hub look, not the one-line default (a gradient area, a thick line, rounded and padded arcs, large bar radii, a centre label, a themed tooltip). The spike (`scratchpad` only, nothing added to the repo) showed the styled version is close to the hand-built look for about 25 to 35 lines per chart and almost no extra weight beyond the MUI X chunk the app already ships.
2. **Brand drawings and cricket-specific visuals** (the Results cards' how-the-runs-were-made bar, the over blocks, the streak chips, the results ring, and later a wagon wheel, worm or partnership chart) are **our own small SVG components** in one shared folder (pure scale and path helpers, a reduced-motion rule, an accessible text alternative). The Results slice needs **no chart library at all**.
3. **No third chart library** (Nivo, ECharts, Recharts, Victory, Chart.js, visx) without the owner's approval first; `d3-scale` and `d3-shape` are the first thing to add if our own drawings need more maths, since both MUI X and any alternative already bundle them.
4. Measured gzipped weight in the spike (a scratch app, public registry): our own three charts 1.4 kB; `@mui/x-charts` about 86 kB plus a 24 kB `d3-scale` chunk shared with it; Nivo about 98 kB plus the same `d3-scale` chunk (the reason it was not chosen, along with a second styling and accessibility system).

## Backend (new reads)
| Endpoint | Wraps | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/me/players/{playerKey}/results?seasonId=&page=&size=` | `PlayerMatchListService` (`115`) | The held player's counted matches as `MyResultListDto` |
| `GET /api/v1/me/players/{playerKey}/results/summary?seasonId=` | `PlayerStatsService` (`103`) | The four tiles and the form strip inputs (only the fields the page shows) |
| `GET /api/v1/me/players/{playerKey}/results/{matchId}` | the match row + the `135` match header | One result as `MyResultDto` |

Same access as `135`: `MyPlayerAccess.resolve` only, **and only for results with the flag on** (the list, the summary tiles and the form strip all count flagged results only, so what a parent sees adds up); key not held, malformed or dropped-out player is **404**, SUSPENDED is **403**, a `matchId` that is not one of the held player's counted matches is **404**. All three go in `MePlayersController`, thin, delegating to a new `MyResultService` (interface and impl, `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`) that calls the existing services and maps to Hub DTOs. The counting rules stay in `PlayerStatsSql` (`APPEARANCES`); the parity rule of `115` (sum of the rows equals the `103` totals, rows equal `basis.matchesCounted`) is tested again through the Hub route. **Later:** `GET .../stats` and `GET .../stats/dashboard` for the Stats section are added to the same family with the same access and the same DTO mapping rule.

**Privacy (server, one place).** The Hub DTOs carry what a signed-in parent sees on the published scorecard: player names (Decision 1), team and opponent names, scores and awards. They never carry draft or unpublished data, a result with the flag off, internal ids beyond the match id, contacts or medical data, guests' or import-guess rows' internals, or another club's data. A result is read only through a held player who has a counted appearance in it. A test asserts the exact JSON keys of each DTO (as `135`) and that a flag-off result is 404 on every read. **Juniors:** shown with full names on the Hub scorecard (Decision 7); this is a deliberate exception to the platform's junior initials rule for this authenticated screen only.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Schedule > Results lists the held player's counted matches whose result is shown in the Player Hub, newest first, with the team result and the player's own figures; no ranking.
2. The form strip shows the last five results with the player's own score under each.
3. A row opens a Hub Result page with both scores, the player's own card and the full scorecard with names; Back returns to the same Season.
3a. A manager can show or hide a published result in the Player Hub (Manage Result, and the Review step); a result with the flag off is absent from every Hub read (404) and from the tiles and the form strip; unpublishing turns the flag off.
4. A held-player key not held, a dropped-out player and another club's match are 404; SUSPENDED is 403.
5. The rows equal `basis.matchesCounted` and the tiles equal the `103` totals for the same Season.
6. Stats > My stats and Team stats remain Soon; nothing here blocks them.
