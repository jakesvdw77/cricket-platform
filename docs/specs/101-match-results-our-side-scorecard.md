## Problem & Goals

`100` captures what happened (outcome, toss, innings totals) but not who did it. There is no batting, bowling or fielding record, so there is nothing to feed player statistics (`103`), no standouts or player of the match, and the batting order a coach actually used on the day is lost (`093` shows the announced plan only, and its roadmap item waits on this slice). The scorecard must also be quick to enter: a manager copies it from a paper or app scorecard, usually on a phone, often with last-minute player changes, borrowed players and a scorecard that does not quite add up. Many managers also have the **opposition's** card and want it in one place, but the opposition's players are not our players: they must never become `Person` or `PlayerProfile` rows, never feed statistics, and must be quick to type because they are only names. And a manager may want to **list the teams before the game starts**, long before there is a score.

Goals:
- **A. A scorecard for either side** per regular innings: batting (order, runs, balls, fours, sixes, how out, **dismissing bowler and fielder**), bowling (balls, maidens, runs, wickets, wides, no-balls), fielding (catches, stumpings, run-outs) and a full extras breakdown. Our side uses registered players; the opposition uses typed names. Both use the same grids behind a side toggle.
- **B. Pre-filled from the selection, never constrained by it:** our line-up and batting order come from `MatchSidePlayer`; every part can be changed, and **edits belong to this result only and never change the selection**.
- **C. Teams first, and before the game:** a **Teams** step confirms who played (our side: registered, swappable, guests; opposition: typed or pasted names). It can be saved as a draft **with no innings and no outcome**, and skipped; a side has a list only if one is created.
- **D. Minimal clicks:** a keyboard-first grid that is also phone-first, with defaults (batting order from the team list, did-not-bat for the rest, bowlers from the list) and a form driven by the wickets already known; **typing a name in a Batting or Bowling row creates the team-sheet entry** so no step forces a trip back to Teams.
- **E. Actual batting order recorded** (planned order kept as a snapshot) for the rotation item in `093`'s roadmap.
- **F. Reconciliation against the result and across both sides,** as acknowledgeable warnings in the shared `ResultValidator` (so `104` imports get them too).
- **G. Standouts and awards:** standouts are **suggestions** computed by relative rules, for our players only, kept or removed with one icon toggle. One **official** player of the match per result (either side or free text) and the club team's own player of the match per ours side.
- **H. Derived credits:** where dismissals name the bowler and fielder, the bowlers' wickets and fielders' catches, stumpings and run-outs are **suggested** from them, never forced.

## Non-goals

- **Super-over scorecards** — their own spec, [`105`](#rollout-notes). It is not known whether super-over runs and wickets count for statistics, and the answer changes how entries are marked. Every scorecard entry belongs to a `REGULAR` innings; an entry sent for a `SUPER_OVER` innings is rejected. Super-over innings totals from `100` are untouched.
- **Statistics, averages, career pages** — `103`. This spec only guarantees the rows `103` needs: a stable link to `player_profile_id`, `NULL` for unlinked guests, and which rows may count (see "Guests and borrowed players" and "The opposition").
- **Statistics, standouts or any record of opposition players as people.** An opposition `result_player` has a name and nothing else: no profile, no `Person`, no link route, no "match played", no standout, no award that counts for anyone.
- **A guests concept for the opposition.** Opposition names are just names: no Guest chip, no `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER`, no "Use this player".
- **An opposition player directory** and remembering opposition names between fixtures (the roadmap's "Opposition player directory"). The data allows it later (opposition rows keyed by the fixture's opposing team); not built.
- **Free-text bowler or fielder as a loose string on a dismissal.** A dismissal points at a `result_player` of the right side; typing a new name in a dismissal control creates that player (one representation, so a rename or a removal cannot leave stray text behind).
- **Importing a pasted scorecard with figures** ("J Smith c X b Y 23 (18)"). Pasting a **list of names** is supported; reading figures out of text is `104`'s and the AI-suggest work, behind a confirm step (imports always confirm).
- **Import adapters** — `104`. The canonical `ResultDraft` gains the scorecard section so adapters can fill it; no provider code here.
- **Ball-by-ball detail:** fall of wickets, partnerships, over-by-over, minutes batted, wagon wheels, catches dropped. This is not a scoring app (`100`). **Dot balls are kept as one optional number per bowler** (`bowling_entry.dots`, decided 2026-10-14: the earlier decision to drop them is reversed); there is still no ball-by-ball record.
- **Standout thresholds, a club settings table or a thresholds dialog.** Decided 2026-10-14: no `club_result_settings`. Per-league, format-aware thresholds are deferred (Rollout Notes).
- **Match graphics** (a broadcast-style strip of the top batters and bowler). Future consumer of the featured flags; not specified here.
- **Captain and wicketkeeper flags on the scorecard.** Dropped (decision 2026-10-14).
- **Creating a `Person` / `PlayerProfile` for a guest** ("create guest profile": no `Person` or `PlayerProfile` is ever created from a scorecard) and **bulk linking** of a guest name across many past matches. Both are tied to the roadmap's player-linking item; this spec builds single-match linking only.
- **Rotation rules and warnings** ("opened in the last N matches"): only the data they need (actual and planned order) is recorded.
- **Per-role capture rights,** player- or public-facing scorecards, notifications on publish: unchanged from `100`'s non-goals. Public or player-facing views of opposition names stay out until that work exists (minors' names, open question).
- **Multi-innings formats:** limited overs only, as in `100`.
- **Changing the Team Selection.** The Teams step reads the selection and never writes it.
- **A forced two-sided team list.** Nothing is required except Result and Scores and margin. A team list exists for a side only if it is created (confirming Teams, or typing or picking names while capturing); a missing or short list never blocks, it only raises a gentle INFO notice for our side (`OUR_TEAM_NOT_SELECTED`).
- **Identity for opposition names across matches.** Suggestions from earlier fixtures are text only: no person, no stats, no link; they are tenancy-scoped to the club.

## User Stories

- As a club manager, I can list the teams **before the game starts** and save that as a draft with no score and no result yet, so that the line-ups are on record and I can finish after the game.
- As a club manager, I can choose **Who is playing?** between the two sides with a logo toggle, so that I enter our team and the opposition's with the same screens.
- As a club manager, I can see our team pre-filled from the selection in the planned batting order (12th man marked), change it for this match only, and never touch the selection.
- As a club manager, I can swap a selected player for the person who played instead (a last-minute change) in one place, and every row of that player follows, so that I never retype the figures.
- As a club manager, I can add a registered player from the match squad, team squad or club, or type a name for a guest or borrowed player who is not in the system, so that the scorecard is complete and adds up.
- As a club manager, I can type or paste the opposition's names one per line (Enter adds a row), reorder them and mark a substitute, or leave them incomplete or empty, so that entering eleven names takes seconds and never blocks me.
- As a club manager, I see a gentle notice when our team is not properly selected (a friendly with no selection), but can still capture the scorecard, with the names I enter becoming guests unless they match a registered player.
- As a club manager, I pick opposition names from a select over the names already on their team sheet (type-ahead, with **Add a new name** last) instead of retyping them, on batting rows, bowling rows and dismissal bowler and fielder pickers.
- As a club manager, I see "Played for CBC 1 before" suggestions of names from earlier fixtures against the same opposition and add them with one tap, with the paste box still available.
- As a club manager, I can skip any optional step (**Don't capture the team** on Teams), so that I record only what I have.
- As a club manager, I can type a name directly into a Batting or Bowling row (for the opposition, or for a player of ours when no team was captured) and the player is added to the team for me, so that I never go back to Teams.
- As a club manager, I can change the batting order by moving a batter up or down, so that the order I record is the order they actually batted.
- As a club manager, I am only asked for dismissal details for as many batters as wickets fell, and the remaining batters are marked not out or did not bat for me, so that I do the minimum.
- As a club manager, I can say which bowler took each wicket and which fielder caught, stumped or ran out the batter, picking from the other side's list, so that their credits follow.
- As a club manager, I can see the bowlers' wickets and fielders' catches, stumpings and run-outs filled in from those dismissals and marked "from dismissals", and type over them when the scorecard I copied says otherwise.
- As a club manager, I can enter the extras as wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes and penalty runs, so that the innings total can be checked and bowlers are charged correctly.
- As a club manager, I can enter each side's bowlers' overs, maidens, runs, wickets, wides, no-balls and (optionally) dot balls, and record catches, stumpings and run-outs per fielder.
- As a club manager, I am warned (never blocked) when a scorecard does not agree with the result's totals or with the other side's scorecard, and I can acknowledge the warning at publish, so that an honest scorer's mismatch does not stop me.
- As a club manager, I can see suggested standouts for our players marked with an icon, and remove or add one with a single tap.
- As a club manager, I can name the official player of the match from either side or free text, and the club team's own player of the match for each of our sides (two in a derby).
- As a club manager, I can link a guest row to a registered player afterwards, so that a mistake about who played can be fixed.
- As a club manager of a derby, I enter both teams with the same toggle, and a player's wickets and catches work between the two registered lists just as against an opposition.
- As a club manager, I can remove the opposition's entries again, so that I can go back to totals only without leaving stray data.
- As a coach, I can later see where each player actually batted against where they were planned, so that rotation can be judged (data only in this slice).
- As a coach or player later, I can open a result and read both scorecards, with dismissals written as "c Smith b Jones".

## Data Model Changes

Migrations: `backend/src/main/resources/db/changelog/v1/044-match-results-our-side-scorecard.sql` (**built**) and `045-match-results-opposition-scorecard.sql` (new; confirm the number at plan time, another branch may take it). All tables are scoped through `match_result` (tenancy is `match_result.club_id`; no duplicate `club_id` on child rows). Enums are `CHECK`-constrained varchars, as in `043`. Each table below is marked **(044)** for what is built and **(045)** for what is added.

**No data-model or API change for the UI decisions.** Reordering writes the existing `position` (batters) and row order (bowlers) through the whole-scorecard replace; the Full name / First name toggle is display-only and stores nothing; the side toggle is wizard state; "Teams captured" is derived, not stored.

### Why the player list is its own table: `result_player`

A guest can bat, bowl and field in the same match; a swap, a link to a registered player and a duplicate check are each **one decision about one person**. So the scorecard first records **who played for each side**, once, and the batting, bowling and fielding rows point at that list. Swapping or linking is then one update, every row follows, and "matches played" (for `103`) is a row in this table. The same list holds the opposition's typed names.

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | uuid pk | |
| `match_result_id` | uuid not null | fk `match_result`, on delete cascade |
| `side` | varchar | `HOME` / `AWAY`. **(044)** a side that is ours. **(045)** also the side that is not ours (opposition rows) |
| `player_profile_id` | uuid null | fk `player_profile` (this club's profile; stats roll up by `person_id` through it). Always null for the opposition |
| `display_name` | varchar null | the name **as written**; required when `player_profile_id` is null (a guest, or an opposition name). Kept after linking, as an audit of what was typed |
| `linked_by`, `linked_at` | uuid null, timestamptz null | set by the link route (audit of a link made on a draft or published result) |
| `planned_batting_order` | int null | **snapshot** of `MatchSidePlayer.batting_order` when the line-up was pre-filled; never changed by edits |
| `replaced_player_profile_id` | uuid null | the selected player this person replaced, when swapped ("planned X, Y played") |
| `substitute` | boolean | a substitute fielder who is not one of the eleven (or the 12th man until he takes part). On ours: does not count as a match played. **Appearance rule for `103`:** every non-substitute ours `result_player` of a published result is a match played, even with no batting, bowling or fielding row. On the opposition it is a **display marker only** |
| `opposition` | boolean not null default false | **(045)** true for a player of the side that is **not** ours (the side without a team of this club). Set by the client on save and **validated by the server against the match**: `opposition = true` only on the non-ours side of a match with exactly one ours side; `opposition = false` only on an ours side |

Constraints: `CHECK (player_profile_id IS NOT NULL OR display_name IS NOT NULL)`; `UNIQUE (match_result_id, side, player_profile_id) WHERE player_profile_id IS NOT NULL` (a registered player appears once per side; in a derby the same person on both sides is allowed by the schema and flagged by `PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES`, a warning, see Derby); `UNIQUE (match_result_id, side, lower(display_name)) WHERE player_profile_id IS NULL` (two unlinked guests, or two opposition names, on one side must be told apart: "John", "John (Dave's friend)"; **per side**, so the same guest name may appear once on each side of a derby). **(045)** `CHECK (NOT opposition OR (player_profile_id IS NULL AND display_name IS NOT NULL AND planned_batting_order IS NULL AND replaced_player_profile_id IS NULL AND linked_by IS NULL))`: an opposition row is a **name, a side and an optional substitute marker, nothing else**.

A `result_player` list can exist with **no innings and no outcome** (a draft before the game, see API Contract).

### The opposition: who counts for what (rule for `103`, fixed here)

Only rows with `opposition = false AND player_profile_id IS NOT NULL` feed any player's statistics. Opposition rows are never a "match played", never a standout, never a player's award, never linkable. The UI reuses an existing row when a name already on the side's list is typed again (case-insensitive, trimmed), so "J Smith" typed as a bowler is the same player as the batter "J Smith". Opposition names stay free text (never feeding stats, never creating a person). **Suggestions from earlier fixtures (no schema change):** the Teams step for the opposition can offer the distinct names of earlier opposition `result_player` rows of this club against the **same opposition**, identified by the league team id when the match has one, else by the normalised opposition team name (trimmed, case-insensitive). Most recent first, with a count. These are suggestions only: choosing one copies the text into this result's list; nothing links the rows across matches, and nothing is read from another club. The plan confirms which match columns carry the opposing team's id and name; the query reads `result_player` joined to `match_result`/the match, scoped by `club_id`.

### `match_innings` (altered; `043` fixed the base columns) **(044)**

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `wides`, `no_balls`, `byes`, `leg_byes`, `penalty_runs` | int null | the extras breakdown, **in runs** (a wide that runs four is 5 wides), ≥ 0. The batting side's extras, entered once and shown on the Batting step of that side and the Bowling step of the other (same data) |
| `extras` | int null | existing column, now the **stored total**. When any component is non-null the server **derives** `extras` as their sum; a client total that disagrees is a save error (`EXTRAS_TOTAL_MISMATCH`). With no components (a summary-level result) `extras` is whatever was sent, as in `100` |
| `non_bowler_wickets` | int null | wickets in this innings not credited to a bowler (run-outs, obstructing the field, handled ball, hit ball twice, timed out, retired out). **Derivation (merged rule):** whenever the innings has **at least one batting entry** (either side), the server derives it from the batters' dismissals (every wicket `how_out` that is not bowler-credited, which includes `RETIRED_OUT`), writes it into this column on every save, and the manual input becomes a read-only line ("1 from the batting scorecard"). With no batting entry it is the typed single number, as before. Removing the batting entries leaves the last derived value as the starting manual one |
| (constraint) | | `ux_match_innings_sequence` is `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED`, so innings can be resequenced in place (see below) |

**Who is charged (cricket rules, enforced as the meaning of the columns):** wides and no-balls are charged to the bowler (they appear in the bowler's `runs`, and optionally in their `wides` / `no_balls`); byes, leg byes and penalty runs are **not** charged to any bowler. Runs off the bat from a no-ball are charged to the bowler and credited to the batter. Therefore, for an innings: `bowlers' runs + byes + leg byes + penalty runs = innings runs`, and `batters' runs + extras = innings runs`.

### Innings identity must survive saves (a change to `100`'s upsert) **(044)**

`100`'s `PUT .../result` deletes and recreates the innings on every save. Scorecard entries cascade from `match_innings`, so that would silently erase the scorecard every time the Scores step is re-saved. The upsert therefore **reconciles innings in place**, matched on `(kind, batting_side)` (limited overs: one regular innings per side), updating `sequence`, runs, wickets, balls, `all_out` and the extras columns on the existing row and keeping its `id`. A changed toss re-orders (resequences) the innings without touching their entries, which belong to a side. An innings removed from the body is deleted with its entries; the wizard asks first when entries exist (`ConfirmDialog`: "This removes the scorecard for that innings"). Invisible when no scorecard exists.

### `batting_entry` (one row per player in the innings, including those who did not bat)

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | uuid pk | |
| `match_innings_id` | uuid not null | fk, on delete cascade; the innings must be a `REGULAR` innings **in which `result_player.side` batted** |
| `result_player_id` | uuid not null | fk `result_player`, on delete cascade; **unique with `match_innings_id`** |
| `position` | int null | **the actual batting order**, 1-based and distinct per innings; `NULL` for `DID_NOT_BAT`. Planned order stays on `result_player.planned_batting_order` |
| `how_out` | varchar | `DismissalType` (below), including `NOT_OUT` and `DID_NOT_BAT` |
| `runs`, `balls`, `fours`, `sixes` | int null | `runs` required once the row batted (validated at publish); balls, fours and sixes are optional and `NULL` means "not on the scorecard", never 0 |
| `featured_override`, `featured` | varchar null, boolean null | standout marking, ours only, see "Standouts" (the same two columns exist on `bowling_entry` and `fielding_entry`) |
| `bowler_result_player_id` | uuid null | **(045)** fk `result_player`, **on delete set null**; the player credited with the dismissal. Must be a player of the **fielding side of that innings** (the side that did not bat). Indexed |
| `fielder_result_player_id` | uuid null | **(045)** fk `result_player`, on delete set null; the catcher, the keeper who stumped, or the fielder credited with the run-out. Same side rule. Indexed |

Both new columns are optional always (a manager may know only the bowler) and work **for both sides, and in a derby between the two registered lists**. With no dismissal detail entered the columns stay null and the step behaves as `how_out` only.

**`DismissalType` enum:** `NOT_OUT`, `DID_NOT_BAT`, `BOWLED`, `CAUGHT`, `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED`, `LBW`, `STUMPED`, `RUN_OUT`, `HIT_WICKET`, `HANDLED_BALL`, `OBSTRUCTING_FIELD`, `HIT_BALL_TWICE`, `TIMED_OUT`, `RETIRED_NOT_OUT`, `RETIRED_OUT`. A caught-behind is `CAUGHT`. Bowler-credited dismissals: `BOWLED`, `CAUGHT`, `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED`, `LBW`, `STUMPED`, `HIT_WICKET`. A **wicket** (counts toward the innings wicket count) is every value except `NOT_OUT`, `DID_NOT_BAT` and `RETIRED_NOT_OUT`. A wicket that is not bowler-credited (`RUN_OUT`, `HANDLED_BALL`, `OBSTRUCTING_FIELD`, `HIT_BALL_TWICE`, `TIMED_OUT`, `RETIRED_OUT`) is a **non-bowler wicket** for bowling reconciliation.

**Which dismissal takes which bowler and fielder (045; structural rule, save error `DISMISSAL_DETAIL_NOT_ALLOWED`; the client clears a field when `how_out` changes):**

| `how_out` | Bowler | Fielder | Credit it produces (derived, see below) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BOWLED`, `LBW`, `HIT_WICKET` | allowed | not allowed | bowler: wicket |
| `CAUGHT` | allowed | allowed (the catcher; the keeper for a caught-behind) | bowler: wicket; fielder: catch |
| `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED` | allowed (the bowler, who also took the catch) | not allowed | bowler: wicket **and** catch |
| `STUMPED` | allowed | allowed (the keeper) | bowler: wicket; fielder: stumping |
| `RUN_OUT` | **not allowed** (no bowler is credited) | allowed (the fielder who broke the wicket or made the throw; one name) | fielder: run-out; a non-bowler wicket |
| `HANDLED_BALL`, `OBSTRUCTING_FIELD`, `HIT_BALL_TWICE`, `TIMED_OUT`, `RETIRED_OUT` | not allowed | not allowed | non-bowler wicket, no credit |
| `NOT_OUT`, `DID_NOT_BAT`, `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` | not allowed | not allowed | none |

The side rule is a save-time error (`DISMISSAL_PLAYER_WRONG_SIDE`).

**Retirement rules (decided 2026-10-14, adopted as written):**

| Kind | When | May return? | Recorded as | Wicket / dismissal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retired not out (retired hurt: injury, illness or other unavoidable cause) | Unavoidable cause | Yes, with umpire consent, at the fall of a wicket or another retirement | `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` | **No**, not a wicket or dismissal. A returning batter is **one row** |
| Retired out (tactical or voluntary exit for any other reason) | Any other reason | No, unless the opposing captain gives permission | `RETIRED_OUT` | **Yes**, counts for the innings wicket count, but **no bowler is credited**, so it is a non-bowler wicket like a run-out for bowling reconciliation |

Edge case, not specially built: a `RETIRED_OUT` batter permitted back by the opposing captain is a **new row** (the manager adds the player again as a separate entry). The one-row-per-player rule applies to `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` returns.

### `bowling_entry` (a side's bowlers; belongs to the innings in which the *other* side batted)

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id`, `match_innings_id` | | fk cascade; the innings must be a `REGULAR` innings in which the opposing side batted |
| `result_player_id` | uuid not null | unique with `match_innings_id` |

### `fielding_entry` (a side's fielders; same innings as the bowling)

`id`, `match_innings_id`, `result_player_id` (unique pair), `catches`, `stumpings`, `run_outs` (all int, ≥ 0). `featured_override` and `featured` as on `batting_entry` (ours only). **(045)** `credits_source` varchar not null default `MANUAL` (`MANUAL` / `DERIVED`; one flag for the row's three counters). One row per fielder with at least one credit; a keeper's catches are catches.

### Derived or manual credits **(045)**

`wickets`, `catches`, `stumpings`, `run_outs` stay the **stored effective values** (what `103` and every check read). A `DERIVED` row's stored values are **recomputed by the server on every scorecard save, in the same transaction**, from the dismissals; a `MANUAL` row's are never touched. The client's numbers on a `DERIVED` row are ignored on save (the server writes the derivation). One shared pure class, `DismissalCredits` (unit tested), serves the save, the validator and, later, the imports.

- **Derivation, per innings, from that innings' batting entries:** a bowler's `wickets` = batters with a bowler-credited `how_out` whose `bowler` is that player. A fielder's `catches` = `CAUGHT` with that fielder, plus `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED` where that player is the bowler. `stumpings` = `STUMPED` with that fielder. `run_outs` = `RUN_OUT` with that fielder.
- A `DERIVED` `fielding_entry` is created when the derivation gives a player at least one credit and removed when it falls to zero. A `DERIVED` `bowling_entry` is **never created by the server's derivation**: a dismissal's bowler needs a bowling row (it carries balls and runs, which a dismissal cannot supply); see `DISMISSAL_BOWLER_NOT_IN_BOWLING_LIST`. (The wizard helps: choosing such a bowler offers to add an empty bowling row, see Bowling and fielding.)
- **Default:** a bowling or fielding row created while that innings **has batting entries** starts `DERIVED`; one created with no batting entries starts `MANUAL`. Typing in a derived field switches the row to `MANUAL` (client, and the PUT carries it); **Use from dismissals** switches it back. Rows are never switched automatically, so existing `MANUAL` rows keep their typed numbers when batting entries are added later (a confirmed bulk "Use dismissals for all" is offered).
- Existing `044` rows migrate as `MANUAL`.

### Standouts: relative suggestions, a per-entry override, frozen at publish (our players only)

Decided 2026-10-14: a threshold such as "30 runs" cannot be a single club-wide number, because it depends on the format and the age group. So there is **no `club_result_settings` table and no thresholds dialog**. Standouts are **suggestions**, computed on read, in code, by rules **relative to the innings and independent of format**. The constants live in one place (`StandoutRules`, one class, unit tested): `TOP_N = 3`, `BATTING_SHARE = 0.25`, `ECONOMY_FACTOR = 0.75`, `MIN_BALLS_FOR_ECONOMY = 12`, `FIELDING_MIN = 2` (placeholders to try on real scorecards).

- **Batting** (per innings, our batters): rank by runs descending, then fewer balls. A batter is suggested when they are in the top `TOP_N` **and** their runs are at least `BATTING_SHARE` of the innings' batting total, **or** they are the top scorer of the innings with at least one run.
- **Bowling** (per innings, our bowlers): rank by wickets descending, then lower economy. Suggested when in the top `TOP_N` **and** (at least 1 wicket, **or** at least `MIN_BALLS_FOR_ECONOMY` balls bowled with an economy at or below `ECONOMY_FACTOR` of the innings run rate).
- **Fielding** (per innings): rank by catches + stumpings + run-outs descending; suggested when in the top `TOP_N` and at least `FIELDING_MIN` dismissals.
- Guest rows qualify (they performed) and show the Guest tag.
- **Never a standout (user, 2026-10-14):** a batter who is Did not bat or scored 0 (or no) runs, and a bowler who bowled no balls. The server never suggests them and refuses a `FEATURE` override on them.
- **The opposition has no standouts:** none computed, no star shown, and the server refuses a `featuredOverride` on an opposition entry (`OPPOSITION_NO_STANDOUTS`). `StandoutRules` reads the stored (effective) `wickets` and credits of **our** rows, so derived values flow into our suggestions with no extra code.

**These are suggestions, not facts.** The manager can add or remove a standout for any of our players with a simple star toggle on the scorecard rows. A tap on a suggested row removes it; a tap on any other row adds it. Nothing is forced.

**Where it is stored (per entry, computed and edited apart, so recomputing never erases an edit):**
- `featured_override` (`batting_entry`, `bowling_entry`, `fielding_entry`): `FEATURE` (manager added), `HIDE` (manager removed a suggestion) or `NULL`. Written only by the manager's toggle, through the scorecard `PUT`; never overwritten by a recompute or re-save.
- The **computed** suggestion is never stored on a draft; it is derived on every read.
- The **effective** value is `FEATURE` => featured, `HIDE` => not featured, `NULL` => the computed suggestion. Returned on every ours entry as `suggested`, `featuredOverride` and `featured`.
- **Frozen on publish:** `featured` is `NULL` on a draft and set at publish to the effective value; a published result reads it as stored, so a later rule change or link never rewrites history. Unpublish clears `featured` (overrides kept).
- **Future use:** dynamic match graphics will read the featured flags. **Deferred:** per-league or per-format thresholds (Rollout Notes, `docs/roadmap.md`).

### `match_result` (altered behaviour)

`detail_level` is **set by the server**, never by the client:
- `SUMMARY`: no `result_player`.
- `OUR_SIDE`: at least one **ours** `result_player` and no opposition rows (always the case in a derby).
- `FULL`: at least one ours `result_player` **and** at least one opposition `result_player` **(045)**.
- **Opposition rows without any ours row** (for example the ours rows were deleted afterwards) are a publish error `OPPOSITION_WITHOUT_OUR_SCORECARD`; on a draft save they are allowed so the manager can edit freely. An opposition-only scorecard therefore **cannot be published** (open question 1).

`detail_level` describes the *team lists*; a list with no entries yet is still `OUR_SIDE` or `FULL`. No other column changes.

### Guests and borrowed players (ours)

A guest or borrowed player is a `result_player` row with `player_profile_id = NULL` and a `display_name`. Decisions:

1. **Free-text row, no automatic profile.** Nothing creates a `Person` or `PlayerProfile` behind the manager's back.
2. **Borrowed from another team of our own club** is not a guest: the **Add player** picker on Teams offers the **Club** tab (default tabs are the match squad and the team squad for the match's season), and the person is a real, linked player. Selecting someone outside the squad shows a warning (`PLAYER_NOT_IN_SQUAD`), not a block.
3. **What an unlinked row counts for:** it **is** part of the scorecard, the innings, the totals and every reconciliation check. It **does not feed any player's statistics** and is not a "match played" for anyone. It can win a standout or the player of the match; both then show the "Guest" tag, and neither counts toward a person's awards until linked.
4. **Linking later** (`POST .../result/players/{id}/link`) sets `player_profile_id` on that one row; every batting, bowling, fielding, standout and award row follows. `display_name` is kept as written. Linking is allowed on a published result as the **only edit allowed on a published result**, because it changes no figure on the scorecard, only who is credited. It is **audited** (`updated_by`/`updated_at`, plus `linked_by`, `linked_at`). The route refuses an opposition row (**045**: `OppositionPlayerNotLinkableException`, 400).
5. **Duplicate-name risk:** (a) the partial unique index stops two identical guest names on a side; (b) a guest whose name matches a registered club player (case-insensitive, trimmed) raises `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER` offering **Use this player**; (c) the Guest tab offers previously used guest names for the club (`results/guest-names`, never opposition names); (d) the same name in different matches is **not** assumed to be the same person: only the manager's explicit link says so.
6. **A registered player at another club** is, to this club, a guest until the roadmap's cross-club link exists.

### Derby (internal fixture, both sides ours)

A derby is a match where both `MatchSide`s have a team of this club. Decided 2026-10-14:

1. **Each side is its own scorecard** behind the same side toggle as any match. Home and Away each have their own `result_player` list, batting, bowling and fielding entries, extras and non-bowler-wicket numbers. Each side's list pre-fills from **that team's own** `MatchSide` / `MatchSidePlayer`, via `scorecard/prefill` (one list per ours side). **There is no derby-specific step logic**: a derby is two ours sides (both registered lists) behind the toggle, subject to the rules below.
2. **Awards in a derby:** exactly **one official** `PLAYER_OF_MATCH` per result (a player from either side's list, or free text) and **one optional** `TEAM_PLAYER_OF_MATCH` per ours side, so up to two.
3. **Guest names are unique per side, not per match.** The same guest name on both sides is **not** an error: it raises the info issue `GUEST_ON_BOTH_SIDES` ("also on <other team>"). Nothing blocks or needs acknowledging.
4. **A registered player on both sides is a warning, not an error** (`PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES`, acknowledgeable at publish): a player can be borrowed between teams of the same club. The message names the player and both teams.
5. **Dismissal bowlers and fielders work between the two registered lists** exactly as against an opposition (the mechanism is a `result_player` of the fielding side); the derived wickets and catches then credit registered players on the other team. This resolves the former `102` open question on derby dismissals.
6. **Statistics (note for `103`):** a player who appears for both sides of a derby counts **one** match played, not two. Their rows from both sides still count in full. `103` must aggregate matches played by distinct match per `person_id`, not by `result_player` row.

**Save-time (structural) rules, errors on `PUT`:** all counts ≥ 0; `position` distinct and ≥ 1; `how_out` valid for the table; a row's `result_player` belongs to this result and its `side` matches the innings (batting entries on an innings that side batted; bowling and fielding on an innings the opposing side batted); one batting, bowling and fielding row per player per innings; `result_player` has a profile or a name, a profile belongs to this club, a profile appears once per side; no entry on a `SUPER_OVER` innings (`SCORECARD_SUPER_OVER_NOT_SUPPORTED`, pointing at `105`); an extras total that disagrees with its components; **an entry (batting, bowling or fielding) for an innings that is not in the result (`SCORECARD_INNINGS_MISSING`) — players alone no longer need an innings (changed)**; **(045)** `opposition` only on the non-ours side of a one-ours-side match (`OPPOSITION_NOT_ALLOWED`); an opposition row has a name, no profile and no link/swap fields; a dismissal bowler or fielder is a player of the **fielding side** of that innings (`DISMISSAL_PLAYER_WRONG_SIDE`) and only where the table allows (`DISMISSAL_DETAIL_NOT_ALLOWED`); `featuredOverride` on an opposition entry (`OPPOSITION_NO_STANDOUTS`); valid `wicketsSource` / `creditsSource`; a bowler's `dots` above their `balls` (`DOTS_EXCEED_BALLS`). A draft may otherwise be incomplete or inconsistent, as in `100`.

**Draft before the game (backend change).** `ScorecardRules` currently rejects any scorecard that has players but no regular innings (`SCORECARD_INNINGS_MISSING`, "a scorecard on a result with no innings for that side"). That check moves to the **entries**: batting, bowling and fielding entries still need their innings; a players-only scorecard saves. `match_result.outcome_type` is already nullable while `DRAFT` (`043`), and capture of upcoming matches already works (`includeUpcoming`); publish still needs an outcome. A saved list on a match later recorded as `FORFEIT`, `ABANDONED` or `NO_RESULT` is kept as a record but **never counts as an appearance** (note for `103`; the appearance rule applies to published results with outcome `WON`, `TIE` or `DRAW`).

**Validation (`ResultValidator`, shared with imports; run by validate and publish).** Errors block publish; warnings are acknowledged at publish with `acknowledgedWarnings`, exactly `100`'s mechanism. None run for an innings that lacks runs or balls, nor for `FORFEIT`/`ABANDONED`/`NO_RESULT`; only regular innings are considered. **Every rule below runs for either side's entries**, with the issue's `side` naming the side the entries belong to (the batting or bowling side) and its `step` (`BATTING`, `BOWLING`).

*Errors (logically impossible):* a batting row that batted (`how_out` not `DID_NOT_BAT`) with no `runs`; `fours × 4 + sixes × 6` exceeds `runs`; a `DID_NOT_BAT` row with figures; maidens above complete overs bowled; two players claiming the same batting `position`; official player of the match missing both player and name; more than one official player of the match, or more than one team player of the match for a side; **(045)** `OPPOSITION_WITHOUT_OUR_SCORECARD` (publish only: opposition players exist but the result has no ours `result_player`).

*Warnings (reconciliation; the codes name the rules in code and tests):*

| Code | Rule |
|---|---|
| `BATTERS_OUT_NOT_WICKETS` | A batting innings: the count of batters with a wicket `how_out` (including `RETIRED_OUT`, excluding `RETIRED_NOT_OUT`) ≠ the innings wickets |
| `NOT_OUT_COUNT` | More than two batters `NOT_OUT` when the innings is not all out, more than one when all out |
| `BATTING_TOTAL_MISMATCH` | Σ batters' runs + extras ≠ innings runs (the message gives both sides of the sum) |
| `BOWLER_BALLS_MISMATCH` | Σ a side's bowlers' balls ≠ the other side's innings balls |
| `BOWLING_RUNS_MISMATCH` | Σ bowlers' runs + byes + leg byes + penalty runs ≠ the innings runs. With no extras breakdown on that innings, only Σ bowlers' runs ≤ innings runs is checked |
| `BOWLER_WICKETS_MISMATCH` | Σ bowlers' wickets + `non_bowler_wickets` (which includes retired-out batters) ≠ the innings wickets. A derived total that falls short because bowlers were not named shows up here, which is the intended nudge |
| `FIELDING_EXCEEDS_WICKETS` | Σ catches + stumpings + run-outs credited > the wickets in that innings, or credited run-outs > `non_bowler_wickets` |
| `WIDES_MISMATCH`, `NO_BALLS_MISMATCH` | Σ bowlers' wides (no-balls) differs from the innings' wides (no-balls) when both are entered |
| `BOWLER_OVERS_EXCEED_LIMIT` | A bowler's balls exceed the league's maximum overs per bowler (`LeaguePlayingConditions.maxOversPerBowler`, converted to balls as `100` does) |
| `LINEUP_TOO_LARGE` | More than eleven non-substitute players **who took part** for a side (a batting row that batted, a bowling row or a fielding credit). The Teams list may hold a 12th man or extra names without a warning. Applies to the opposition list too (open question 5) |
| `PLAYER_NOT_IN_SQUAD` | Ours: a linked player is not in that team's squad for the match's season (`TeamSquadMember`), the same test `029` applies to selection |
| `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER` | Ours: an unlinked name equals a registered club player's name |
| `PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES` | Derby only: the same registered player is on both sides' lists. Acknowledgeable |
| `DISMISSAL_BOWLER_NOT_IN_BOWLING_LIST` | **(045)** A dismissal names a bowler who has no bowling row in that innings. The wickets cannot be credited to anyone's figures |
| `BOWLER_WICKETS_DIFFER_FROM_DISMISSALS` | **(045)** A `MANUAL` bowling row's `wickets` differs from the wickets the dismissals credit to that player. **Checked only when every bowler-credited dismissal of that innings names a bowler** (otherwise nothing is raised) |
| `FIELDING_DIFFERS_FROM_DISMISSALS` | **(045)** A `MANUAL` fielding row's catches, stumpings or run-outs differ from the dismissals' credit. Checked per counter, and only when every dismissal that counter depends on names a fielder; a `CAUGHT_AND_BOWLED` always names its bowler |

A `DERIVED` row cannot trigger the "differ" warnings (it equals the derivation by construction). Rules that do **not** apply to opposition rows: `PLAYER_NOT_IN_SQUAD`, `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER`, `PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES`, `GUEST_ON_BOTH_SIDES`, standouts.

*Info (never blocks, nothing to acknowledge):*
- `GUEST_ON_BOTH_SIDES` (derby only): the same guest name (case-insensitive, trimmed) is on both sides, shown as "also on <other team>".
- `OUR_TEAM_NOT_SELECTED` (`step` `TEAMS`, `side` the ours side; `severity` `INFO`): **exact trigger.** For an ours side that has a scorecard being captured (the side has at least one `result_player` or any batting, bowling or fielding entry), raised when EITHER: (a) the match has no selection for that team (no `MatchSide`, or no `MatchSidePlayer` rows); (b) the side's list has **fewer than 11 non-substitute players** (the count of list rows, not of players who took part; the threshold is a placeholder, open question 9). **Announcement is irrelevant** when recording a result: a selection of eleven or more never raises it, announced or not (decision 2026-10-14, user). Never raised for an opposition side, for a side with no scorecard at all, or for `FORFEIT`/`ABANDONED`/`NO_RESULT`. It needs no acknowledgement and never blocks publish: friendlies are normal. Message for (a): "Our team is not selected for this match (a random friendly?). You can still capture the scorecard; names you enter become guests unless they match a registered player."; for (b): "Only N players are on our team list for this match. You can add the rest on Teams."

**Validation issues carry `code`, `step` and `side` (decided 2026-10-14).** Every validation issue, errors and warnings alike, is an object with an optional `code`, an optional `step` (the wizard step to jump to: `TEAMS`, `BATTING`, `BOWLING`, `AWARDS`, `SCORES`), an optional `side` (`HOME` / `AWAY`, set on every scorecard issue and null for match-level issues, so Review can say "Home batting" and **Fix** can open the right side's tab), alongside the existing message. Info issues use `severity` `INFO`. This is **additive** (existing `errors` and `warnings` message strings stay for current callers) and **amends `100`'s "message strings only"**. Codes come from the existing `ResultValidator.WarningCode` enum, extended with the codes above; errors get a parallel `ErrorCode` enum. This **resolves the roadmap item "stable warning codes" for results** and lets the UI drop message-text deduplication (`ReviewStep`'s `dedupeWarnings`) later.

**Exceptions:** reuse `InvalidResultException` (structured `errors`/`warnings`) and `ResultPublishedException`. The link route raises `PlayerAlreadyInResultException` (extends `ConflictException`) and, for an opposition row, `OppositionPlayerNotLinkableException` (extends `ValidationException`, 400). `backend/openapi/openapi.yaml` regenerated by hand per the repo's practice, additions only.

### Wizard steps

- **Result** keeps the outcome, the winner, Normal | DLS | Super over and the DLS figures. Its note (changed): *"Now record how the match ended. The teams and the scorecard are optional..."* followed by a line that batting, bowling and the rest can be added any time (final copy at the mockup). The margin is not asked there.
- **Toss and field** flows into Result with a plain Next. The quiet prompt **Add the batting and bowling** (leads into Batting) or **Skip to scores** is at the END of the **Result** step (Result directly precedes Batting). There is no opposition opt-in switch any more: the side toggle replaces it.
- **Overs come from the bowling card only** (decision 2026-10-15, user): the innings' balls are the sum of the opposing bowlers' balls; the batters' balls faced never feed the innings overs, because scorers differ on byes and leg byes. Until the bowling card has balls, the Overs field is empty with the helper "Overs are worked out from the bowling card; type them here if you have the official figure" and stays editable as the official override.
- The innings figures (runs, wickets, overs) are typed on the scorecard steps in an **Innings card** (Batting: the innings that side batted; Bowling and fielding: the innings the other side batted), editing the same draft innings; with no toss the scorecard steps ask who batted first. **Scores and margin** shows both innings (editable) with figures **suggested from whichever scorecards are entered** where a field is empty ("Use these") and a hint where it differs, the calculated winner and margin ("Use this") with an editable Margin and Runs | Wickets, the toss line and the super over pairs; with no scorecard it is plain score entry. It is required.
- Autosave on step change and **Save draft** behave as in `100`; a draft that holds only a team list is a real draft (not blank) and appears in the results list. *(Assumed:)* if the stepper is crowded on a phone, the optional steps are grouped visually; the steps themselves and their order do not change.

The **name display toggle** sits at the top of every scorecard step, the Review step and the result page.

### Side toggle (Teams, Batting, Bowling and fielding, Awards)

A two-tile toggle in the **Who-won** style (`TileChoiceGroup`, the side's logo and team name, for example **Irene Villagers 1** | **CBC 1**), shown on every scorecard step **in every match** (not only derbies). On Teams its label is **Who is playing?**; on Batting and on Bowling and fielding it names the side being entered; on Awards it sits under the official award (below). It is the first ours side by default (Home in a derby). A small dot on a tile marks a side with unsaved or incomplete entries. The toggle switches which side's list, innings and strips are shown; the draft keeps both. A derby is two ours sides behind the same toggle, with no further derby logic in the steps.

### Player name display toggle (Full name / First name)

A small `CompactToggleGroup` at the top of the scorecard steps, the Review step and the result page: **Full name** | **First name**. Junior coaches often do not know players' surnames.

- **First-name mode:** the plain first name. Where two players *visible in the same view* share a first name, a short surname tag is added: the first letter of the surname ("Sam V"); for particle surnames the particles' initials in lower case plus the first letter of the main surname ("Sam vdW"). If two tags still collide, the tag lengthens one letter at a time; last resort the full surname. A name whose first word is a single initial ("J Smith", "J. Smith") shows the whole typed name in first-name mode, because an initial cannot be shortened further (applies to guests and opposition names).
- **Display-only.** Nothing is stored and no API field changes. **Guests and opposition names follow the same rules as registered players:** a free-text name is split into first name and surname the same way (first word first name, rest surname, same particle handling) and the same collision rule applies across registered players, guests and opposition names in the view. A single-word name has no surname to tag, so it falls back to the full typed name and the duplicate warning does the telling apart.
- **One pure helper**, `playerDisplayName` in `ui/src/utils/`, and **one shared `PlayerName` component** used by every scorecard row, picker row, awards, standouts, dismissal text and every result-page line. Accessible names ("Runs, A. Smith") use the same label.
- **Remembered per user in browser storage**, in the manner of `useListViewPreference` (try/catch around storage, default **Full name**). *Later:* a club or section default; out of scope.

### Standout icon toggle (our players' rows only)

Every ours batting, bowling and fielding row, on the entry steps, the Awards step and the result page, shows a **star icon button** (`StandoutToggle`, 44 px target on a phone, accessible name "Featured, A. Smith"). Filled = featured (effective), outlined = not. Tapping writes `featuredOverride` (`HIDE` on a suggested row, `FEATURE` on any other; tapping again returns it to `NULL` when it matches the suggestion). A suggested-but-not-edited star is tinted as "suggested". On a published result the stars are read-only. **Opposition rows show no star.** No star on a Did-not-bat or 0-run batter or a bowler with 0 balls (`canFeature` in `utils/standout.ts`); when a row becomes such a row any `FEATURE` override on it is cleared and the Awards list drops it. Icons under 32 px, so MUI `@mui/icons-material` (`Star`, `StarBorder`).

### Step: Teams (first step; who is playing)

Decision 22 (renamed from "Our team", moved first, extended to both sides; it keeps Decision 21's team-first pattern). Who played is settled **once, first**, and every later step works from that list. It is optional: **Don't capture the team** skips it.

**Our side (an ours tile selected):**
1. **Pre-fill.** On first open the step calls `scorecard/prefill` and builds the list locally (nothing is written until a save): the selected eleven in the planned batting order, then the 12th man marked **12th man**. A strip shows where it came from ("From the announced team") with **Reset to selection** (`ConfirmDialog`; adds missing planned players and re-snapshots the planned order, never silently removes players who already have figures). **Edits belong to this result only; they never change the Team Selection.** If the selection changes later, the saved list is not touched until the manager chooses Reset. This works before the game: a proposed selection is enough.
2. **Not-selected banner.** When `selectionState` is `NONE`, or the list has fewer than 11 non-substitute players while a scorecard is being captured (the trigger of `OUR_TEAM_NOT_SELECTED`), a gentle banner (an `InfoCard`, not an error) shows, for no selection, "Our team is not selected for this match (a random friendly?). You can still capture the scorecard; names you enter become guests unless they match a registered player.", and, for a short list, "Only N players are on our team list for this match. You can add the rest on Teams." An unannounced but selected team never warns. The same banner shows at the top of Batting and Bowling and fielding for that side. It is dismissible per visit and never blocks. **No selection.** With no team selected the step says **No team selected** and offers **Populate from the team** (the season squad as a fallback, which the manager trims) and **Add player**.
3. **Per player a name popup (Team Selection pattern).** Tapping the name opens a popup with **Reorder** items (**Move up**, **Move down**, **Position**), **Swap player...**, **Mark as 12th man / substitute** (and back) and **Remove** (`ConfirmDialog`: "This removes the player and all their figures"; deletes that player's `result_player` and every batting, bowling and fielding row). **Swap player** means the selected player did not play and this person played instead: it opens the same picker as Add player, the swapped-out player is remembered in `replaced_player_profile_id` and shown as "replaced A. Smith", and the figures follow (one `result_player` update). A guest row's popup also holds **Link to a registered player...**. The planned batting order is the displayed order; the actual order is set on Batting.
4. **Add player** opens `PlayerSlotPicker` with four tabs: **Match squad** (the players selected for this match, including the 12th man), **Team squad** (the season squad), **Club** (anyone in the club) and **Guest** (name only; previous guest names as suggestions; a name that matches a registered player offers **Use this player**). Players already on the list are shown disabled with the reason. A guest carries the visible **Guest** chip everywhere.
5. **Linking and duplicate handling live here:** registered versus guest, `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER`, the per-side duplicate-name rule, `PLAYER_NOT_IN_SQUAD`, `PLAYER_ON_BOTH_SIDES`, and the `LINEUP_TOO_LARGE` hint (a quiet "more than eleven players have taken part" note).
6. **12th man and substitutes.** The 12th man and any player marked substitute are in the list but are not a match played until they take part: a 12th man who bats, bowls or fields is **promoted to a real player** (`substitute = false`) automatically when the first row for him is saved; one who only fields as a substitute stays `substitute = true`.

**Opposition (the non-ours tile):** free-text names only; there is no selection, no Swap, no Link, no registered picker.
1. **Rows, in batting order.** The list is the opposition side's `result_player` rows in the order typed (the likely batting order). **Enter** in the last row adds a row below and focuses it; Enter on an empty last row does nothing. **Reorder** by the drag handle on desktop and, on every device, by the name popup (**Move up**, **Move down**, **Position**); **Mark as substitute** (and back) and **Remove** (`ConfirmDialog`, clears references to the row) are in the same popup. Names are edited in the cell, and a rename follows everywhere.
2. **Empty state:** a large multi-line "Type or paste their names, one per line" field with **Add names**, and an **Add a row** button. Above or beside it, when earlier fixtures exist against the same opposition, a **"Played for CBC 1 before"** list of chips (name, with a count; most recent first; paged "Show more") from `results/opposition-names`: **one tap adds a name** to the list; **Add all** is not offered (they are suggestions). The paste box stays. Names already on the list are hidden from the suggestions. Suggestions are text only (no identity across matches).
3. **Paste a list:** pasting text with line breaks into any name cell splits it into names, trims each, drops blank lines, strips a leading list marker ("1.", "1)", "-", "•"), puts the first into the current row and **creates the following rows in order**, so the batting order is the paste order. At most 15 names are taken at once (a notice says so). Duplicate names (case-insensitive) already on the list are skipped with a notice. One pure helper, `parseNameList` in `ui/src/utils/`, unit tested.
4. **Select, do not retype.** Once the opposition has names on its list (typed or pasted on Teams, or typed earlier in Batting or Bowling), **every opposition picker** (Batting name cells, Bowling name cells, and the dismissal Bowler and Fielder pickers on our batters) is a **select over that list with type-ahead**, with **Add a new name** as the last option (it adds to the list). Before any names exist the cells are plain typing cells. The suggestions from earlier fixtures also appear as a final group in these pickers when the list is still short. **May stay incomplete or empty** without blocking: the opposition list never blocks a step or a publish. **One player, one row:** a name appears once on the list, so the same person is one `result_player` across batting, bowling and fielding; typing an existing name reuses the row.
5. The opposition list is also what a **derby never has**: in a derby both tiles are ours and both use the registered flow.

### Step: Batting

The grid works for **either side** (the side toggle). Rows are that side's Teams list; there is no Swap or Remove here (they live on Teams).

1. **Rows come from Teams, and typing adds to the team.** The grid lists the side's team list in the planned batting order (ours: the 12th man last as Did not bat; opposition: the typed order, substitutes as Did not bat). A row's **Name** cell is a type-ahead: for the **opposition** it is a select over the opposition list with type-ahead and **Add a new name** last (plain typing while the list is empty); for **our side** it searches the registered match and team squad players first and falls back to a **guest name** (with the Guest chip and the same `GUEST_NAME_MATCHES_PLAYER` offer). Typing a name creates the `result_player` for you (and reuses an existing row for the same name). When the side's list is empty (no team captured) the grid starts with blank typing rows (**Enter** adds another row; pasting a list creates rows in order); when a team is captured the grid is its rows plus one quiet **Type a name to add a player** row at the end (open question 3). Nothing here requires a trip back to Teams.
2. **Wicket-driven form.** The innings' **wickets and balls come from the Innings card at the top of this step** (the same figures Scores and margin shows; editable there, they may be typed after the rows were built) and are shown in a meter (`CardProgressBar`, "Out 4 of 6"). The grid starts with the first `wickets + 2` players as batted and the rest as **Did not bat**. As the manager marks how each batter was out, once the number of dismissals equals the wickets the remaining batted rows become **Not out** automatically (a cleared row reverts them to undecided); the manager is asked only for dismissals up to the wicket count. Wickets 10 means all out. A `RETIRED_OUT` row counts as a dismissal; a `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` row does not, and a returning retired-hurt batter is still one row. More dismissals than wickets turns the meter amber ("1 more than the result says") and shows `BATTERS_OUT_NOT_WICKETS` on review; nothing is forced. Batting can **reorder** rows and set **Did not bat** on any row.
3. **The grid.** Columns: `#`, Name, **Dismissal**, R, B, 4s, 6s. The **Dismissal** cell is the `HowOutSelect` followed by, where the type allows them, a **Fielder** and a **Bowler** control that read like a scorecard line ("caught [fielder] b [bowler]"). Both are small type-ahead selects over the **other side's** Teams list (for an opposition list, with **Add a new name** last): **Fielder** lists everyone on it (including substitutes and the 12th man), **Bowler** lists that side's bowlers of this innings (the rows of its Bowling and fielding step, in bowling order). **Typing a name not on the list creates it** (an opposition name, or for our side a registered player or a guest), so no trip to Teams is needed; a chosen bowler with no bowling row offers to add an empty one (see Bowling and fielding). Controls that the type does not allow are absent (not disabled): a run-out shows only a fielder, a bowled only a bowler. Tab order is how out, fielder, bowler, then R. A phone batter card shows the same as chips. Nothing in the dismissal cell is required.
4. **Totals and extras.** A totals strip reads **Total 148 = 135 off the bat + 13 extras ✓** (or an amber `BATTING_TOTAL_MISMATCH`, never a block), and an **Extras** row holds Wides, No-balls, Byes, Leg byes, Penalty with the sum written to `extras` (the same fields as on the other side's Bowling step: editing either edits the same innings).
5. **Keyboard-first.** Tab/Shift+Tab and Enter/Shift+Enter move along a row and wrap to the next row's first number cell; Arrow Up/Down move within a column; number cells select their content on focus and use `inputMode="numeric"`; **How out** is a type-ahead (`c` Caught, `b` Bowled, `l` LBW, `r` Run out, `s` Stumped, `n` Not out, `d` Did not bat; `cb` Caught and bowled; `ro` Retired out, `rn` Retired not out; Enter accepts); `?` lists the shortcuts. Focus is visible; every cell has an accessible name; the grid is real inputs in a table, not a custom widget.
6. **Phone.** Each batter is a compact card: name, a How-out chip row (with fielder and bowler chips), and R / B / 4s / 6s steppers on a numeric keypad; same order and auto-marking. No dragging on a phone: tapping the name opens the name popup, whose Move up / Move down / Batting position are the reorder route, 44 px targets.
7. **Reordering: the Team Selection pattern.** Reordering follows `ui/src/components/TeamSelectionList/TeamSelectionList.tsx`: on desktop native HTML5 drag and drop with a handle; on every device the name popup with **Move up**, **Move down**, **Batting position** and **Set to Did not bat**. Moving a row changes `position`; `plannedBattingOrder` is untouched ("planned 3"). Swap, Link and Remove are only on Teams. No Alt+Arrow shortcut. Names go through `PlayerName`.
8. **`PlayerSlotPicker`** (used by Teams for Add player and Swap player). On Batting, Bowling and fielding and Awards it appears only as a **team-only picker** listing the side's Teams list, with a **Not listed? Add them on Teams** link (inline typing is the other route).

### Step: Bowling and fielding

For the side selected: a **Bowling** table for the innings in which the **other** side batted. Ours pre-lists the Teams players whose selected role is bowler or all-rounder (opposition: nothing pre-listed; pick or type), and the manager adds others by picking from the list or **typing a name** (creates the entry, as on Batting). Columns Bowler, O (`OversInput`, "8.2"), M, R, W, with Wd, Nb and Dots behind **More**; a **Bowled so far** strip ("Balls 238 of 240 · 2 left", "Runs 131 of 140 conceded incl. 6 extras not charged"); the batting side's **Extras** row (the shared fields, with the note "Byes, leg byes and penalty runs are not charged to a bowler"); and **Wickets not charged to a bowler**: when the innings has batting entries it is a read-only line ("1 from the batting scorecard"), otherwise a typed single number that closes the wickets reconciliation ("5 + 1 = 6 ✓").

**W** shows a **from dismissals** marker while the row is `DERIVED`; typing in it makes the row manual and the cell shows "the dismissals say 2" with **Use 2**. A **Fielding** list below holds the side's fielders with three counters (Catches, Stumpings, Run-outs) derived the same way from the dismissals' fielders and marked "from dismissals"; only names with a credit appear unless added from the team list or typed. When batting entries are added after these figures were typed, a banner says "Bowling and fielding figures were typed in. Use the dismissals for all?" with a `ConfirmDialog` bulk action (it overwrites typed numbers, so it asks) and per-row **Use N** otherwise. A dismissal that names a bowler without a bowling row offers **Add to bowling** (creates an empty bowling row with `DERIVED` wickets, figures to fill). A 12th man or substitute who gets a row is promoted to a real player if he bowls; a substitute who only fields stays one. Keyboard behaviour and phone cards are those of Batting. Bowling order is the row order, reordered like batters (name popup: Move up, Move down, **Bowling position**; Swap, Link and Remove are on Teams). The step shows the **Innings card** of the innings the other side batted.

### Step: Awards

Official award above the toggle, standouts and team award under it, as `InfoCard`s in the poll-page style.

1. **Player of the match (official), above the toggle:** one pick for the whole result, from **either side's list** (grouped by team, rendered through `PlayerName`; an opposition pick snapshots the name) or **Someone not listed...** free text, or **None**. There is exactly one official pick per result. The result card shows it.
2. **The side toggle** (below the official award).
3. **Standouts (ours tile only):** the effective list grouped Batting, Bowling, Fielding, each row the player (Guest chip when unlinked) with figures ("62 off 48", "3 for 21 (8.0)", "2 catches") and the **star toggle**; "Suggested from the scorecard. Tap the star to remove or add one." On the opposition tile the block shows "The opposition has no standouts."
4. **The team's own player of the match, per ours side under the toggle:** one pick from that side's list, or none. In a derby both tiles are ours, so the toggle shows each side's own pick (up to two in all); on the opposition tile it reads "The opposition's own player of the match is not recorded."

Every pick from a Teams list is team-only (a Match squad that includes the 12th man and substitutes), with a **Not listed? Add them on Teams** link.

### Review step and result page

Review shows a scorecard line **per side** with its status `None` / `Partial` / `Complete` (for example "Irene Villagers 1: complete; CBC 1: partial"), merges the scorecard warnings into the existing single **Checks** list (each with **Fix** jumping to its step and the right side's tab via `step` and `side`; each issue prefixed with its side's team name; `GUEST_ON_BOTH_SIDES` and `OUR_TEAM_NOT_SELECTED` show as quiet info lines with no acknowledgement), and the card preview gains the player of the match. The result page (`ResultDetailPage`) gains a **scorecard section per side** (the team's name as the heading): batting, bowling and fielding as zebra tables (the section scrolls inside its card on a phone), the extras line, and for our side the standouts and the awards (the opposition has no standouts); the batting table shows "planned 3" only where it differs *(assumed)*. Dismissals are written as a scorecard line by one pure helper, `dismissalText` in `utils/dismissal.ts` ("c Smith b Jones", "c & b Jones", "st Smith b Jones", "b Jones", "lbw b Jones", "run out (Smith)", "hit wicket b Jones", "retired out"; a missing name falls back to the plain word with the names known, "caught b Jones"). Every label goes through `PlayerName` with the toggle at the top of the page and on Review. Edit remains disabled on a published result ("Unpublish to edit"); **Link to player** on a Guest row is the single allowed action on a published result.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Teams first:** the wizard order is Teams, Toss and field, Result, Batting, Bowling and fielding, Awards, Scores and margin, Review. Only Result and Scores and margin are needed to publish; Teams, Batting, Bowling and fielding and Awards are optional, each with a Skip (Teams: Don't capture the team).
- **A draft before the game:** a team list can be saved with no innings and no outcome and read back; the server accepts a players-only scorecard (entries still need their innings); a draft that holds only a team list appears in the results list and is not "blank"; editing it never changes the Team Selection.
- **Side toggle:** Teams, Batting, Bowling and fielding and Awards show a logo-tile toggle for both sides in every match; our side is pre-filled from the selection (planned order, 12th man marked), the opposition is a free-text list; a derby is two ours sides behind the same toggle with no extra step logic.
- **Team not selected is a notice, not a gate:** nothing is required except Result and Scores and margin; a side's list exists only if created. When our team has no selection, or fewer than 11 non-substitute players on the list while a scorecard is captured, a gentle banner shows on Teams and at the top of Batting and Bowling and fielding, and Review lists `OUR_TEAM_NOT_SELECTED` as an INFO issue (`step` `TEAMS`, `side`) that never blocks and needs no acknowledgement; typed names become guests unless they match a registered player.
- **Select, do not retype (opposition):** once the opposition has names, every opposition picker (batting rows, bowling rows, dismissal bowler and fielder) is a select over the list with type-ahead and **Add a new name** last; the Teams step offers "Played for <opposition> before" suggestions (same league team id, else same normalised name, this club only, most recent first with counts) that add with one tap; they are suggestions with no identity or statistics, and the paste box stays.
- **Opposition names:** typed or pasted one per line (Enter adds a row, Enter on an empty last row does not; list markers and blanks dropped; 15-name cap; duplicates skipped), reorderable, substitute-markable, removable; may stay incomplete or empty without blocking; a name typed again reuses the same player. Opposition players are `result_player` rows on the non-ours side with a name only; no `Person` or `PlayerProfile` is created, they cannot be linked, are never a standout and never counted in any statistic or "match played" (unit tested).
- **Our team on Teams:** per-player name popup with Swap player (a swap is one `result_player` update carried to every row), Mark as 12th man / substitute and Remove (confirm; deletes that player's rows); Add player via Match squad, Team squad, Club and Guest; a 12th man who bats, bowls or fields becomes a real player (`substitute = false`); a swapped-out player is shown as "replaced".
- **Inline typing:** typing a name in a Batting or Bowling row (or a dismissal control) creates the team-sheet entry and reuses an existing name; for our side it offers registered squad players first and falls back to a guest name; for the opposition it is free text; nothing forces a trip back to Teams.
- **Dismissals:** each dismissal can name a bowler and a fielder where the type allows (a run-out never has a bowler; a caught-and-bowled has only the bowler), drawn from the other side's list, for both sides and in a derby; `non_bowler_wickets` is derived from the batting entries of either side whenever they exist and is read-only in the UI then.
- **Derived credits:** bowlers' wickets and fielders' catches, stumpings and run-outs are derived by the server from the dismissals, marked "from dismissals", recomputed on every save for `DERIVED` rows and never touched on `MANUAL` rows; typing over one makes it manual and **Use N** returns it; a `MANUAL` value that differs from complete dismissals raises an acknowledgeable warning and an incomplete attribution raises none.
- **Reconciliation both ways:** batters' runs plus extras against the innings; each side's bowlers' balls, runs (plus byes, leg byes, penalty), wickets (plus non-bowler wickets), wides and no-balls and the fielding credits against the other side's innings; each mismatch is an acknowledgeable warning with `code`, `step` and `side`, enforced by the shared `ResultValidator` (a unit test drives it with no wizard involved). Opposition entries are included; the rules that are ours-only do not apply to them.
- **Awards:** one official player of the match per result above the toggle (either side's list or free text; an opposition pick keeps its name if the row is removed); the club team's own player of the match per ours side under the toggle (two in a derby); at most one official award per result; standouts only for our players, with a star toggle that removes a suggestion or adds any of our players, the override stored apart from the computed value, frozen at publish.
- No star is offered on a Did-not-bat or 0-run batter, a bowler with no balls, or any opposition row; any such override is cleared when the row changes, and the server does not suggest them.
- **Detail level:** `detail_level` is server-set: `SUMMARY`, `OUR_SIDE` (ours only) or `FULL` (ours and opposition); an opposition-only scorecard cannot be published; a derby is never `FULL`.
- Batters and bowlers can be reordered by drag and drop with a handle on desktop and by the name popup on any device (Move up, Move down, Batting or Bowling position, plus Set to Did not bat on batting); Swap, Link and Remove are on the Teams popup only; no dragging is needed on a phone and no Alt+Arrow shortcut exists.
- A Full name / First name toggle at the top of the scorecard steps, Review and the result page changes every player label (rows, awards, standouts, review lines, result page, opposition names, guests) through one `PlayerName` component; surname tags only where two players in the same view share a first name; a single initial keeps the whole typed name; nothing is stored on the server; remembered per user; default Full name.
- A row can hold a free-text guest name; it is tagged Guest; it counts in the totals and checks but is excluded from any player's statistics until linked; two identical guest names on a side are refused; a guest name equal to a registered player's offers **Use this player**; a guest can be linked, including on a published result (the only edit allowed there, audited).
- With wickets = 6, marking six batters out leaves the rest of the batted rows not out and the remaining list did-not-bat without further input; marking seven shows the over-count and, on review, `BATTERS_OUT_NOT_WICKETS`. A `RETIRED_OUT` batter counts as a wicket (and a non-bowler wicket); a `RETIRED_NOT_OUT` batter does not, and a returning one is a single row.
- Every non-substitute ours `result_player` of a published result with outcome won/tie/draw is a match played for `103`, even with no rows; a substitute is not; a player on both sides of a derby counts one match.
- A bowler's optional dot balls (`dots`) are stored per bowler, never above the balls bowled (`DOTS_EXCEED_BALLS` on save); `NULL` is "not entered", never 0.
- Extras are stored as wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes and penalty runs; `match_innings.extras` always equals their sum; wides and no-balls are in bowlers' runs and byes, leg byes and penalty are not.
- Re-saving the Scores step, or changing the toss, never deletes scorecard entries.
- A full innings can be entered from the keyboard alone on desktop, and on a 375 px viewport with no horizontal page scroll.
- **Derby:** same toggle, each side pre-filled from its own team; same guest on both sides is an info issue, a registered player on both sides an acknowledgeable warning; two identical guest names on one side are still refused.
- Review shows each side's status and every check says which side it belongs to; the result page shows both scorecards with dismissals written as "c Smith b Jones".
- A scorecard entry for a super-over innings is rejected, and super-over innings totals from `100` behave as before.
- No `captain` or `wicket_keeper` flag exists; no `club_result_settings` table, endpoint or dialog exists.
- OpenAPI changes are additive; new services have unit tests and new queries integration tests in the same PR.
