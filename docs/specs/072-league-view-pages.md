# 072 — League View Pages

**Depends on:** `062-league-detail-redesign.md` (the single-page `LeagueDetailPage` layout this spec amends and supersedes; 062's data fetching, `RecordQuickViewDialog` contact pattern and page-local `LeagueTeamTile` are carried over, its section order, hero Schedule card, chip row, Details card and Contacts card are replaced), `050-league-schedule-and-fixtures.md` (`NextMatchCountdown`, `LeagueFixtures`, reused unchanged), `051-league-schedule-sharing.md` (`ShareScheduleDialog`, reused unchanged), `052-league-playing-conditions.md` (the Playing Conditions rows, `PlayingConditionsShareDialog` and the `DocumentUpload` PDF, reused), `053-league-extended-profile.md` (`League.format`/`logoUrl`/`phone`/`email`/`website`/`socialLinks`), `054-league-contacts.md` (`LeagueContact`, reused), `056-club-profile-overview.md` and `057-team-extended-profile.md` (the `RecordIconButton` + `RecordQuickViewDialog` pattern), `070-league-teams.md` (league teams, listed on the Teams view alongside the club's own affiliated teams), `069-match-card-redesign.md` (the badge-tone approach and `RecordCard` conventions the header badges follow). Sibling: `071-league-card-redesign.md` (the list card whose footer buttons deep-link into the three routes this spec creates; **this spec is built first**).
**Status:** approved (design approved by the user). Design: https://claude.ai/artifact/SkLSFF8G4yaTUqJ4cJvmQo

## Problem & Goals

`LeagueDetailPage` (`062`) is one long page: a hero Schedule card, then Details, Contacts, Teams and Playing Conditions stacked below it. A manager who wants only the schedule, only the teams or only the conditions scrolls past the rest, and the page has no addresses of its own for those parts, so nothing can link straight to "this league's teams". It also has a real bug: it loads only the first page (20) of the league's matches via `listMatches` (`page: 0`, default `size = 20`), so a long season's schedule is silently truncated and the next-match countdown and the teams/fixtures chip are computed from an incomplete list.

This spec splits the page into three views, **Schedule**, **Teams** and **Conditions**, under one header that is always visible and now carries the league's contact details, social icons and contact people. Each view has its own route, so the new league card (`071`) can link to each directly and the browser Back button works between views.

**Goals**
- One header on every view: a top row with the **Back to Leagues** button on the left (the existing label, linking to the leagues list `/manage/fixtures/leagues`) and the badges (format, team count, Active/Inactive) with the Season select on the right; a title row with the logo and name on the left and Edit on the right; a divider; then the league's contact people avatars, and under them the links row (phone, email, website on the left, social icons on the right).
- A Schedule | Teams | Conditions switcher under the header; each view is its own route under the league, rendered through a shared layout route so the header and the selected season persist across views.
- The selected season applies to all three views and lives in the URL (`?seasonId=`), so switching views and pressing the browser's Back preserve it.
- Each view shows only its own section: Schedule (Share schedule, next-match countdown, fixtures), Teams (the season's own affiliated teams and the `070` league teams, read-only), Conditions (today's playing-conditions rows, Share, the PDF).
- Fix the truncation bug: Schedule loads **all** matches of the selected league and season.
- Show the Playing Conditions PDF as "Playing Conditions.pdf" instead of the stored uuid-prefixed file name, on the Conditions view and on the edit screen's Playing Conditions tab.

## Non-goals

- **Any backend change.** Confirmed: no migration, endpoint, DTO or `openapi.yaml` change. Every call below already exists (`listLeagues`, `listSeasons`, `listTeamsForClub`, `listLeagueAffiliations`, `listLeagueTeams` from `070`, `listMatches`, `getPlayingConditions`, `listLeagueContacts`).
- **The league card and list.** That is `071`. This spec only creates the routes it links to.
- **Editing from the view pages.** Managing teams, league teams, contacts and conditions stays on `LeagueFormPage`'s tabs; the view pages are read-only (the Edit button leads there), as `062` established.
- **Storing the original PDF file name.** The backend still generates a uuid file name and the original name is not stored; the label is a fixed display string, not the uploader's file name. Persisting the real name is not planned here.
- **Showing Playing XI size and Age range on the view pages.** `062` put them in the header chip row; the approved header badges are now format, team count and Active/Inactive only. They remain on the league card (`071`) and the edit screen. Draft `055` will later move their source to `LeaguePlayingConditions`, at which point they naturally belong on the Conditions view; this spec does not add them there so it does not depend on `055`.
- **A season badge or the `062` "N teams · M fixtures" chip in the header.** The Season select already shows the season; the fixtures count was derived from the truncated list and is dropped.
- **Public or player-facing league pages.** Manager (`/manage`) screens only.
- **Changing `NextMatchCountdown`, `LeagueFixtures`, `ShareScheduleDialog`, `PlayingConditionsShareDialog`, `RecordQuickViewDialog` internals or props.**
- **Results, standings or match naming.** Not built; unchanged from `050`/`070`.
- **Tabs-as-state.** The switcher is real navigation (links), not local tab state, so each view has an address and history entry.
- **Changing the Back button's label or destination.** It stays "Back to Leagues", to the leagues list, as `LeagueDetailPage` has it today; it does not become a "back to the previous view" button (the browser's Back does that).

## User Stories

- As a manager, I open a league and land on its Schedule, with the header showing the league's name, badges, season, contact people, contact details and social links.
- As a manager, I switch between Schedule, Teams and Conditions with a switcher under the header, and the header and chosen season stay put.
- As a manager, I pick a season once in the header and it applies to the schedule, the teams and the conditions, and survives the browser's Back and view switches.
- As a manager, I use "Back to Leagues" in the header from any view to return to the leagues list.
- As a manager, I see every match of the season on the Schedule, however long it is, and the next-match countdown is right.
- As a manager, I see the club's own teams and the registered league teams for the season on the Teams view, clearly labelled.
- As a manager, I see the season's playing conditions, can share them, and open the PDF, which is labelled "Playing Conditions.pdf".
- As a manager, I tap a contact person avatar in the header to see their details and jump to their edit screen.
- As a manager on a phone, nothing in the header is clipped and the switcher scrolls sideways in its own strip.
- As a manager, a link or card button that points at `/teams` or `/conditions` of a league opens that view directly.

## Data Model Changes

None.

## API Contract

None. No endpoint is added or changed, and `openapi.yaml` is untouched. The one data-loading change is client-side: the Schedule fetches all pages of the existing paginated `GET /api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}/matches` (see UI Requirements, "Loading all matches").

## UI Requirements

Composed from existing shared pieces: `PageHeaderBand`, `Card`, `Input`, `Button`, `RecordIconButton`, `RecordQuickViewDialog`, `SocialLinksRow`, `badgeSx`/`avatarSx` (from `RecordCard`), `EmptyState`, `DocumentUpload`, plus the page-local `LeagueTeamTile` carried over from `062`. The only shared-component changes are additive: three new badge tones, a `compact` option on `RecordIconButton`, and a `displayName` option on `DocumentUpload`.

### 1. Routes (`ui/src/App.tsx`)

Replace the single route `fixtures/leagues/:leagueId` -> `LeagueDetailPage` with a layout route and three children, all inside the existing `/manage` routes:

```
fixtures/leagues/:leagueId                -> <LeagueViewLayout />   (header + switcher + <Outlet />)
  index                                   -> redirects (replace) to ./schedule, keeping the query string
  schedule                                -> <LeagueScheduleView />
  teams                                   -> <LeagueTeamsView />
  conditions                              -> <LeagueConditionsView />
```

So `/manage/fixtures/leagues/:leagueId` redirects to `/manage/fixtures/leagues/:leagueId/schedule` (e.g. the Back link on `LeagueContactDetailPage`, which targets the bare league URL, and old bookmarks keep working). The redirect preserves `?seasonId=`. The sibling routes `fixtures/leagues/new`, `:leagueId/edit` and `:leagueId/contacts/...` are unchanged; React Router's specificity ranking keeps `edit`/`contacts` and `new` from being captured by `:leagueId`. An unknown child path under a league (`/:leagueId/foo`) shows the existing not-found behaviour of the `/manage` shell.

`LeagueDetailPage.tsx` and `LeagueDetailPage.test.tsx` are removed; their content is redistributed as below (the file's `LeagueTeamTile` moves to `LeagueTeamsView`). New files live in `ui/src/pages/manage/league/`: `LeagueViewLayout.tsx`, `LeagueScheduleView.tsx`, `LeagueTeamsView.tsx`, `LeagueConditionsView.tsx` (pages compose from components and need no story/test anatomy, but each gets a test file per Test Plan).

### 2. Shared layout: `LeagueViewLayout`

Reads `clubId` from `ManagerHome`'s Outlet context (same guard as today: "Not authorized" `EmptyState`) and `leagueId` from the route. It owns, and passes to the three views through its own `<Outlet context={...} />` (context also forwards `clubId`):

- **Queries carried over unchanged from `062`'s `LeagueDetailPage`:** `listLeagues` with `select` find-by-id (same query key `['managed-club', clubId, 'leagues']`), `listSeasons`, `listTeamsForClub`, `listLeagueAffiliations`, `listLeagueContacts`. New for the header's team count and the Teams view, keyed under `070`'s league-teams key: `listLeagueTeams(clubId, leagueId, selectedSeasonId, { activeOnly: true })` (`enabled` once a season is selected).
- **Selected season, in the URL.** `useSearchParams`; the effective `selectedSeasonId` is `?seasonId=` when it is the id of one of the club's seasons, otherwise `pickDefaultSeasonId(seasons)` (the same rule `LeagueFormPage` and the backend's `resolveCurrentSeasonId` use). The default is not written into the URL; only a user change is: the Season select sets `?seasonId=` with `replace: true` (changing season does not add history entries). An invalid or stale `seasonId` falls back to the default silently. The switcher links and the index redirect carry the current query string, so the season survives view switches, and each view's URL is shareable with its season.
- **Derived values** carried over: `teamsById`, `affiliationsForSeason`, `seasonLabel`.
- **Contact quick view state** (`openContactId`) and the `RecordQuickViewDialog`, with the same fields and `editTo` as `062` (Status when `contactBadgeFor` returns one, Role, Email, Phone; `editTo` = `/manage/fixtures/leagues/${leagueId}/contacts/${contactId}/edit`).
- Loading and error handling as today: `isLoading` renders nothing, an error or an unknown league renders the "Couldn't load this league" `EmptyState`.

### 3. Header (always visible, inside `PageHeaderBand`)

Desktop (`md` and up), top to bottom, matching the approved mockup:

1. **Top row**, `justify-content: space-between`, wrapping gracefully: the **Back to Leagues** button left-aligned (the same `MuiButton` text link with `ArrowBackIcon` that `LeagueDetailPage` uses today: label "Back to Leagues", `to="/manage/fixtures/leagues"`, the leagues list, on every view); on the right of the same row, right-aligned, a wrapping group of the **badges** (below) followed by the **Season** `Input select` (`minWidth: 130`, `maxWidth: 280`, options from `listSeasons`, hidden when the club has no seasons). On a narrow width the right-hand group wraps below the Back button rather than squashing anything.
2. **Title row**, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`: on the left the logo `Avatar` (`logoUrl`, `rounded`, `avatarSx(44)`, `EmojiEventsOutlinedIcon` fallback) and the league name (`h1`, `h6`/bold, wraps with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, never ellipsised) in a block that is `flex: 1 1 auto; min-width: 0`; on the right, right-aligned on the same row, the **Edit** button (the outlined, primary-tinted style `062` uses, `startIcon` `EditOutlinedIcon`, `flex: none`, destination `/manage/fixtures/leagues/${league.id}/edit`). The name gives way (wraps) before Edit ever drops to a second line.
3. **Divider**, then the **contact section**, in this order:
   - **(a) League contact people row first:** a caption "League contacts" and one compact avatar per `LeagueContact` (`RecordIconButton` with the new `compact` option below, `shape="circular"`, initials via `initialsFromName(contactFullName(contact))`, `label` "Full name — role", `onClick` opens the quick view dialog). Omitted when the league has no contacts (no "No contacts yet" text in the header; managing contacts stays on the edit screen).
   - **(b) Links row beneath it**, a single wrapping row, `justify-content: space-between`: on the **left**, the phone, email and website, each a small icon (`PhoneOutlinedIcon`, `EmailOutlinedIcon`, `LanguageOutlinedIcon`, 16px) plus a link (`tel:`, `mailto:`, and the website as stored, which `LeagueForm` already validates to start with `http(s)://`, `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`), each shown only when set, text `text.secondary` at 13px, left-aligned; on the **right**, the league's social icons, `SocialLinksRow size="small"`, right-aligned (`margin-left: auto`; omitted when `league.socialLinks` is empty). When the row is too narrow the social icons wrap onto their own line beneath the phone/email/website links, left-aligned there.
   - The whole contact section, divider included, is omitted when the league has no contacts, phone, email, website or social links; each row is omitted on its own when it has nothing to show.

Phone (`xs`, 375px first): everything stacks in one column, in this order: the Back to Leagues button; the badges row (wrapping); the Season select (full width); the title row (logo and name, with Edit right-aligned on the same row, the name wrapping to make room); then, below the divider, the contact people row, the phone, email and website lines stacked one per line, and the social icons on their own line beneath them, left-aligned. Nothing clipped or horizontally scrolling at 375px.

**Badges** (a wrapping row of small `Chip`s, `badgeSx(tone)`, shared helper `leagueBadges` in `ui/src/pages/manage/leagues/leagueBadges.ts`, see section 6): **format** (purple, only when `league.format` is set, label `LEAGUE_FORMAT_LABELS[format]`), **team count** (blue, `N team(s)`), **Active** (green) / **Inactive** (grey). The team count is the selected season's own affiliated teams plus its active league teams: `affiliationsForSeason.length + activeLeagueTeams.length` (the same number the `071` card shows for the current season). No season label badge here: the Season select shows it.

### 4. View switcher

Under the header (outside the header surface, as in the mockup), a strip of three navigation links with icons: **Schedule** (`EventOutlinedIcon`), **Teams** (`GroupsOutlinedIcon`), **Conditions** (`DescriptionOutlinedIcon`). Built from MUI `Tabs` (`value` derived from the pathname's last segment, each `Tab` `component={RouterLink}` with `to={{ pathname, search }}` so the query string is kept), `variant="scrollable"` with `scrollButtons={false}`, so on a phone the strip scrolls sideways inside its own row and the page itself never scrolls horizontally. Active tab uses the primary underline; a bottom divider spans the strip. Below it renders `<Outlet />`.

### 5. The three views

**Schedule (`LeagueScheduleView`).** A single plain `Card` (the `062` hero border/shadow is dropped: it is now the only section) with a header row: title "Fixtures" and the **Share schedule** `Button` (`variant="ghost"`, `ShareOutlinedIcon`, opens `ShareScheduleDialog`, unchanged props and handlers carried over from `062`: `handleSharePdf`/`handleSharePoster`/`handleShareCalendar`, `shareTeams`). Body, in a `Stack`: `NextMatchCountdown` then `LeagueFixtures`, both unchanged. Empty states as today ("No seasons yet..." when the club has none; `LeagueFixtures`' own empty state for a season with no matches).

*Loading all matches (the truncation fix).* The matches endpoint is paginated (`Pageable`, default `size` 20; no custom max is configured, so Spring Data's default cap of 2000 applies; sort defaults to `matchDate desc`) and the schedule, countdown and every share generator need the whole season. Add to `ui/src/api/matchApi.ts` a helper `listAllMatches(clubId, { leagueId, seasonId })` that requests pages of `size: 200` (the size `TeamDetailPage` and `useTeamCardData` already use), `sort: 'matchDate,asc'`, starting at `page: 0` and continuing while `page + 1 < totalPages` (the `Page` already carries `totalPages`), concatenating `content`, with a hard stop at 25 pages (5000 matches) as a safety bound that simply returns what it has. A league's single season is a small bounded set (a few dozen to low hundreds of fixtures), so this is a bounded read of one season, not browser-side pagination of an unbounded collection (`docs/standards/frontend.md`), and it matches what the PDF/poster/ICS generators already assume. The query key stays `['managed-club', clubId, 'leagues', leagueId, 'matches', selectedSeasonId]`, replacing the `listMatches({ page: 0 })` call. `resolveNextMatchCountdown` and `LeagueFixtures` therefore receive every match. The identical `page: 0` call in `LeagueFormPage.tsx`'s Schedule tab (same key, same truncation) is switched to `listAllMatches` in the same change, so the two screens agree.

**Teams (`LeagueTeamsView`).** One `Card` titled "Teams in {season label}" (read-only; the Edit button in the header is the route to managing teams). Two labelled groups when both exist: **Our teams** (the season's affiliated teams, `affiliationsForSeason` resolved through `teamsById`, each a `LeagueTeamTile` with the stretched link to `/manage/sections/${team.sectionId}/teams/${team.id}` carried over from `062`) and **League teams** (the season's active `070` league teams, tiles without a link: a league team has no detail page). Tiles are a responsive grid, `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(120px, 1fr))` with a 10px gap (the approved mockup's tiles): a centred avatar (44px, circular: the logo when present, else the abbreviation, falling back to `initialsFromName(name)`; own teams solid `avatarSx` primary, league teams the neutral wash used on the `071` card) over the team name (wraps to two lines, centred). `LeagueTeamTile`'s horizontal layout from `062` becomes this vertical tile. A group with no entries is not rendered; when both are empty the card shows "No teams registered for this season yet." ("No seasons yet..." copy kept when the club has no seasons).

**Conditions (`LeagueConditionsView`).** One `Card` titled "Playing conditions" with, on the right of its header, a wrapping group of actions: the **PDF button** (shown only when the season's `documentUrl` exists; `DescriptionOutlinedIcon`, label **"Playing Conditions.pdf"**, opens `documentUrl` in a new tab; replaces `062`'s "View full document") and **Share** (opens `PlayingConditionsShareDialog`, unchanged props and `handleSharePlayingConditionsPdf` carried over). Body: `062`'s `DetailFieldGrid` of all the conditions rows, unchanged in content, fields and omit rules, driven by `resolvePlayingConditionsPayload(getPlayingConditions(...))`; when there is no structured payload and no document, the existing empty copy "No Playing Conditions set for this season yet." (so the `071` Conditions button, always enabled, lands on a sensible empty state). A document with no structured fields shows just the PDF button and the empty copy.

The display string lives in one constant, `PLAYING_CONDITIONS_PDF_NAME = 'Playing Conditions.pdf'`, exported from `ui/src/api/leaguePlayingConditionsApi.ts` and used by both screens.

### 6. Shared pieces changed (all additive)

- **`RecordCard` badge tones** (`ui/src/components/RecordCard/RecordCard.tsx`): add three tones to `RecordCardBadgeTone` and `badgeSx`, matching the approved mockup: `format` (purple tint, fill `alpha(purple, 0.10)`, border `alpha(purple, 0.5)`, text a darker purple), `season` (amber, `tintedBadgeSx('warning', 'warning.dark')`) and `active` (green tint, `tintedBadgeSx('success', 'success.dark')`). The purple has no counterpart in the palette, so it is added to `ui/src/theme.ts` as one structural token (`#7B5CC4` main, `#5B3D99` dark; never varies per club) and read from there, with its row added to `docs/standards/design-system.md`'s colour table by the `design-token-sync` skill. The blue team-count badge reuses the existing `side` tone (info tint) and Inactive reuses `muted`; no tone is changed. Colour is always paired with the text label.
- **`ui/src/pages/manage/leagues/leagueBadges.ts`** (new): `leagueBadges(league, { teamCount, seasonLabel? })` returns the ordered `RecordCardBadge[]`: format (if set), `N team(s)` (`side`), season label (`season`, only when `seasonLabel` is passed), then Active (`active`) / Inactive (`muted`). The header passes no `seasonLabel`; the `071` card does. The old `badgeFor`, `leagueSeasonBadges` and `leagueRecordFields` exported from `LeagueList.tsx` stay in place and untouched by this spec (still used by the list card until `071` replaces them); `LeagueDetailPage`, their only other importer of `badgeFor`, is deleted here.
- **`RecordIconButton`**: new optional `compact?: boolean`. When true it renders only a 32px avatar (`avatarSx(32, '0.6875rem')`, no caption, `width: auto`), still a `ButtonBase` with `title`/`aria-label` = `label`. Default unchanged, byte for byte. Test and story updated (component anatomy).
- **`DocumentUpload`**: new optional `displayName?: string` prop. When provided, it replaces `filenameFromUrl(value.documentUrl)` as the line shown for an uploaded document (the "Uploaded {date}" caption and the View/Replace buttons are unchanged); when omitted the current behaviour is unchanged. `LeagueFormPage`'s Playing Conditions tab passes `displayName={PLAYING_CONDITIONS_PDF_NAME}`. Test and story updated.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `/manage/fixtures/leagues/:leagueId` opens the Schedule; `/schedule`, `/teams` and `/conditions` are separately addressable and each shows only its own section.
- The header is identical on all three views: a top row with the "Back to Leagues" button on the left (linking to the leagues list) and the format, team count and Active/Inactive badges plus the Season select right-aligned on the same row; a title row with the logo and name on the left and Edit right-aligned on the same row (Edit never wraps to a second line; the name wraps instead); a divider; then the league contact people avatars, and beneath them a links row with phone, email and website on the left and the social icons right-aligned. The old Details and Contacts sections no longer exist.
- "Back to Leagues" returns to `/manage/fixtures/leagues` from every view, with the label unchanged from today.
- Tapping a contact person avatar opens the quick-view dialog with an edit link, as before.
- The Season select applies to all three views, is kept in `?seasonId=`, survives view switches and the browser's Back, and defaults to the current season when absent or invalid.
- The Schedule shows every match of the selected league and season (not just the first 20), and the next-match countdown reflects all of them; the league edit screen's Schedule tab does the same.
- The Teams view lists the season's own teams (linking to each team) and its active league teams (not linked), grouped and labelled, read-only.
- The Conditions view shows the season's rows, a Share button and, when a document exists, a button labelled "Playing Conditions.pdf"; the edit screen's Playing Conditions tab also labels the uploaded file "Playing Conditions.pdf". The stored uuid file name is never shown.
- No backend, migration or `openapi.yaml` change is part of this spec.
- On a 375px screen the header stacks as Back to Leagues, badges, Season, logo/name/Edit, contact people, contact lines with the social icons wrapping beneath them (left-aligned); nothing is clipped, and the switcher scrolls sideways inside its own strip.

## Rollout Notes

- **Build this spec first.** The `071` card's Schedule, Teams and Conditions buttons link to the routes created here, so `072` must be merged before `071` is built. Frontend-only, one PR, no migration, no flag.
- **Supersedes `062` in part.** `062`'s data fetching, `RecordQuickViewDialog` contacts pattern and `LeagueTeamTile` carry over; its section order, hero Schedule card, header chip row (including the Playing XI and age chips and the teams/fixtures chip), Details card and Contacts card are replaced. The build PR updates `062`'s Status line to note it is superseded by `072`; `029`/`050`/`052` references to "the detail page" are unaffected.
- **Why the `LeagueFormPage` schedule fix is included.** It carries the identical `page: 0` call and truncation; leaving it would make the view and the edit tab disagree.
- **Draft `055` (not built, not touched):** it moves `maxPlayingXiSize`/`minAge`/`maxAge`/`ageCutoffDate` onto `LeaguePlayingConditions` and adds rows for them to the detail Playing Conditions section. `072` does not conflict (it shows none of them), and `055` later adds those rows to `LeagueConditionsView` rather than the retired detail page; its "Details section loses its Playing XI/Age rows" wording is already stale after `062`/`072`.
