## Decisions

These are already settled and bind this spec. They come from `100`, `101`, `103` and `115`; nothing here re-opens them.

1. **Compute on read, no stats table, no cache** (`103`, Architecture proposal). A materialised table needs a measured problem first.
2. **Published results only**, through the shared constant `ResultVisibility.PUBLISHED_STATUS` (`103`, `100`). An unpublish, a deleted result or a corrected score is right on the next read.
3. **What is a match played (`103` decision 17).** A result contributes when its outcome is WON, TIE or DRAW, **or** NO_RESULT or ABANDONED **and at least one ball was bowled**. A FORFEIT is not a played match for players; **for teams a forfeit counts in the record (decision 15)**. A no-result or abandoned match with play counts as a match played and adds to none of won, lost, tied or drawn.
4. **A deactivated match with a published result still counts** (`103` decision 21).
5. **One pure class defines every figure.** `TeamStatsCalculator` (`service.support`) is the only place a team figure is defined; SQL only sums, counts and takes encoded maxima of stored columns (`103` Architecture, point 2).
6. **Manager-only now; designed for a later player or public view** (`103` decisions 6 and 13): the calculator, ranker and DTOs carry no access information.
7. **Missing data is shown as "Not captured yet" in one panel, never as a dash tile or a zero; results capture is not changed** (`103` decision 3).
8. **Filters offer only values that have counted data** (`103` decisions 22 and 30), and a section-scoped manager is narrowed to their sections' teams (`103`, `035`).
9. **Standings behave as in `103` decisions 26 and 28:** fixed columns, any column sortable, a second click reverses, competition ranking with equal ranks (1, 2, 2, 4), teams with no defined figure for a rate sort last and unranked, an optional minimum (default: none) on rate sorts, backend paging.
10. **A table figure equals the same team's page figure** (the parity rule, `103`).
11. **Per-team results (`100`, review decision 3).** Each of the club's teams in a match gets its own result; a derby (both sides ours) gives one team a win and the other a loss, or both a tie. Team results are counted per team.
12. **Super-over innings never feed a figure** (`101`, `103`): the queries filter `match_innings.kind = 'REGULAR'` until `105` decides otherwise.
13. **Net Run Rate uses the full-quota rule (owner, 2026-10-21; open question 5):** an all-out innings counts at the full scheduled overs, other innings at the overs used, over WON and TIE results with usable innings, DLS, draws and no results excluded; shown only for one league in one season, with "N of M results". It takes a result only with exactly one regular innings each way and a league (decision 24). The rule is **not** printed on screen (decision 22) and the figure has **2 decimals** (decision 20, not the 3 first written here). A per-league rule setting stays deferred.
14. **A washed-out match with no ball bowled counts for nothing (owner, 2026-10-21; open question 8):** consistent with `103`; it is reported in the basis line, not counted as a No Result.
15. **Forfeits count in the record (owner, 2026-10-21; open question 9, the alternative chosen):** a forfeit is a **loss for the team that conceded and a win for the team that received it**, in Matches Played, Wins, Losses and Win Percentage. A forfeit has no innings, so it feeds **no** runs, wickets, averages, highs, lows, extras or Net Run Rate figure. Forfeits received and conceded are also shown as counters on the team page. This overrides `103` decision 17's "a forfeit never counts" for **team** statistics only (player statistics are unchanged: no innings, no appearance). The plan confirms the forfeiting side from `winning_side`.
16. **Win Percentage = Wins / (Wins + Losses) x 100** (owner, 2026-10-21; open question 7), ties, draws and no results out of the denominator; 1 decimal, a dash when there are no decisive results.
17. **Competition filter defaults to All competitions (owner, 2026-10-21; open question 13)**, with the Competition filter (League, Friendlies) available on both the table and the team page alongside Season, League, Section and Team; the filters offer only values with data, as in `103`.
18. **Home as proposed (owner, 2026-10-21; open questions 1 to 3 as recommended in the spec):** a Teams tab on the Leaderboards page, a team page at `/manage/teams/:teamId/stats`, and one Statistics card with an outlined button on Team detail; no new menu item or icon. All other open questions stay on the list for review after the mockups.
19. **Mockups approved (owner, 2026-10-21):** canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/3cw6pVcWQE74i9ubCUnrLo (3 boards: the Teams tab on phone and desktop, the team page, and the unusual cases: unranked group, Net Run Rate states, empty/error, derby and forfeit in the form strip, the Statistics card on Team detail, partial-data captions). Approving them accepts the recommended default of every open question not answered above as the working answer; **all questions stay on the list for review, and any answer can still change.** As drawn: the table columns P, W, L, T, D, NR, Win %, Runs, Avg, RR, High, Wkts, Bowl avg, Econ, NRR with the Team column pinned on a phone; Played includes forfeits (decision 15); a forfeit shows as "forfeit" on the form strip and a note in the table footer; a derby shows as "internal" on the form strip; the team page has four tiles (Played, Won, Lost, Win %), the form strip, then Record, Batting, Results highlights, Bowling, Extras conceded and Net run rate cards, and the Not captured yet panel.
20. **Two decimals throughout (owner, 2026-10-21):** every team figure that is a rate or average, **including Net Run Rate**, is shown and rounded to 2 decimals (HALF_UP once), for example +0.84; Win Percentage stays 1 decimal. This supersedes the 3 decimals of the Net Run Rate formula above.
21. **Stats shortcut on the Teams list (owner, 2026-10-21, after trying the first build), like Players (`103` decision 20):** each team card gets a fourth footer button (icon over caption "Stats") and each row of the list view a Stats icon button, both opening `/manage/teams/:teamId/stats` directly; the card/row itself still opens Team detail. Together with the Statistics card on Team detail and the Teams table rows, these are the ways in.

Supersedes the "Labelled" bullet of the Net Run Rate section and decision 13's "labelled with its rule".
23. **As built: forfeits are counted and shown (decision 15 applied).** Forfeits are counted in the record and in `resultsCounted` and Played; only a NO_RESULT or ABANDONED match with no ball bowled is "not counted" (`resultsNoBallBowled`). A forfeit is gated on `outcome_type = 'FORFEIT'`, never on missing innings, and its innings are never read. The form entries carry a `forfeit` flag, drawn as a "forfeit" marker on the chip.
24. **As built: Net Run Rate usability.** A result enters only when it is WON or TIE, not DLS, in a league, with **exactly one regular innings each way**, both usable, and the quota known for any all-out innings. `matchesCounted` for Net Run Rate is the counted results **minus forfeits**; `matchesUsed` is out of that. 2 decimals, HALF_UP once.
25. **As built: a chase that finishes exactly on the last ball** (`balls >= scheduled_balls`, quota known, not DLS-decided) is a **complete innings** for the lowest total; a chase that ends early is not.
26. **As built: the table basis is its own query**, counting distinct results across the scope (a derby once, `internalResults` for how many). The page basis counts that team's results. The table's "Partnerships are not captured yet" line is static text: `TeamStatsTableDto` has no `notCaptured`; the page has.

30. **As built: the cross-check against `115` holds only after setting derbies aside.** `103` files a derby under `internal` (no win or loss for a player); the team counts it as a win, loss or tie. So for a player present in every match of a team, the player's won, lost, tied and drawn equal the team's W, L, T, D **over the same filters with derby results and forfeits taken out** (the player has no forfeit appearance), and the match list length equals the team's played minus its derbies and forfeits; the player's `internal` count equals the team's derby results.

## Problem & Goals

`103` answers "how is each player doing?". Nothing answers "how is each team doing?". A manager who captures every result cannot see a team's record, its run rate, its biggest win or its worst total without adding the scorecards up by hand, and the committee cannot compare the club's teams at the end of a season. The data is already there and it is the most complete data the module holds: every published result, **summary level included**, has a `match_result` and its `match_innings` (runs, wickets, balls, extras), so team statistics cover far more matches than player statistics, which need a captured scorecard.

This spec is **Part 2 of statistics: team statistics** for the club's own teams, computed on read from published results, with one table of all the club's teams and one page per team. It reuses `103`'s filters, published gate, counting discipline, sortable-table and ranking machinery, "Not captured yet" panel and parity rule. It captures nothing new and stores nothing.

Goals:
- **A. Every statistic the owner listed** (the list in `103` Part 2), either computed or flagged **Not captured yet** (the Data availability table says which and why).
- **B. One definition of every number**, in `TeamStatsCalculator`, so the table, the team page and the tests can never disagree.
- **C. Honest by construction:** only published results, each figure carrying how much of the data it rests on (summary-level results have no extras breakdown, some innings have no balls), so a manager is never misled by a half-captured result.
- **D. A team table** (all the club's teams, one row each, fixed columns, sortable by any column) and **a team page** (record, form, totals, highs and lows, biggest wins, run rate, net run rate), on the same filters as `103`.
- **E. Rules stated for every awkward case:** derbies, no-result and abandoned matches, forfeits, DLS, super overs, shortened or unfinished innings, free-text and other-club opponents.
- **F. Reusable for a later player or public view:** team figures name no person, so the later access rule is only "which teams may this viewer see".

## Non-goals

- **Partnerships (runs by wicket).** Fall of wickets is not captured (`101` Non-goals, `103` decision 3). They are listed under **Not captured yet** exactly as `103` does (one panel, never a dash tile) and wait for the future scorecard-capture spec ("Deferred by `103`"). This spec changes nothing in results capture.
- **League standings and points tables for a whole league.** Another club's teams are not in this data model as teams of ours, and `100` and `029` defer points and standings. This is **the club's own teams only**; an opponent appears only as a name on a match line. Points per win and loss (`LeaguePlayingConditions.pointsForWin` and the others) are not computed here.
- **A per-league Net Run Rate rule setting.** One fixed rule is proposed (open question 5). A configurable rule per league needs a league setting and a migration and is deferred.
- **Home and away splits** (open question 11): `HOME` and `AWAY` on a match are the fixture's listing, not a ground, and are arbitrary for friendlies.
- **A player-facing or public view of team statistics.** Manager-only now. The service and DTOs are reusable; a player or public view needs its own access rule (which teams, which viewer) and is decided with the player view.
- **Cross-club or cross-league tables, opponent-by-opponent records, head-to-head, projections, "what do we need to win the league" calculations.**
- **Export** (CSV, PDF) of the table or a team page. Deferred, as in `103` and `115`.
- **Sort by the detail-only figures** (lowest total, highest chase, largest victory, extras): the sort columns are the table columns only (open question 2).
- **Any change to results capture, to a scorecard rule, or to `PlayerStatsCalculator`.** Counting rules shared with `103` are extracted into shared SQL constants with no behaviour change (see "SQL approach").
- **Super-over runs and wickets** (`105`), **format-aware thresholds**, **materialised or cached statistics**, **club time zones** (the known gap, roadmap), **a new table, column, index or migration** unless the plan's `EXPLAIN` proves one is needed.
- **Player-versus-team statistics** (for example "runs by opening pair"). Needs fall of wickets.

## User Stories

- As a club manager, I can see a table of all the club's teams with their record (played, won, lost, tied, drawn, no result, win percentage) and their batting, bowling and net run rate figures for a season, so that I can compare the club's teams at a glance.
- As a club manager, I can sort the table by any column and click again to reverse it, so that I can answer "which team scores fastest?" or "who concedes most?" in one click.
- As a club manager, I can filter the table by Season, League, Section (with its sub-sections) and Competition (league or friendly), so that I can look at one league's season or one age group.
- As a club manager, I can open a team and see its record, its last five results, its runs, wickets, averages and run rates, its highest and lowest totals, its highest successful chase, its largest wins by runs and by wickets, its extras conceded and its net run rate, so that I can tell the story of a season.
- As a club manager, I see how many published results each figure is based on and a note where it is partial (an innings without balls, a summary-level result without an extras breakdown), so that I am never misled by a half-captured result.
- As a club manager, I see what is not counted (a washed-out match with no ball bowled) and how many forfeits are counted, in the basis line, so that a team's played total never silently disagrees with the fixture list.
- As a club manager, I see a derby counted once for each of its two teams, one win and one loss, so that internal fixtures work like any other.
- As a club manager, I see Net Run Rate only where it means something (one league in one season) with a short note and "N of M results", so that I never read a number the competition would not recognise.
- As a club manager, I see partnerships listed under **Not captured yet**, so that I know the capture does not yet support them.
- As a section-scoped manager, I see only my sections' teams, as on the Leaderboards.
- As a club manager, I know that unpublishing a result changes every team figure at once, with nothing to refresh by hand.

## Data Model Changes

**None proposed: no table, column, migration or index.** Every figure is computed on read from `match_result`, `match`, `match_innings` and `team`. The columns used were read from `100`, `101` and the `MatchInnings`, `Match` and `League` entities. Anything not verified is marked.

### What counts for a team (the "counted team result")

A **counted team result** is one row for one of the club's teams in one published result: a `(match_result, side)` pair where the match's team on that side (`home_team_id` for `HOME`, `away_team_id` for `AWAY`) is set, i.e. the side is ours. A derby gives two rows, one per team (decision 11).

| Rule | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The result is published | `match_result.status = 'PUBLISHED'` through `ResultVisibility.PUBLISHED_STATUS` | Decision 2 |
| The result belongs to this club | `match_result.club_id = :clubId` | Tenancy |
| The match was played, or forfeited | WON, TIE, DRAW, or NO_RESULT / ABANDONED with a `REGULAR` innings of `balls > 0` or a `bowling_entry.balls > 0`; **or FORFEIT** (decision 15) | Decision 3; the played predicate is the same as `103`, extracted into one shared constant (`PLAYED_RESULT`) so the two can never drift; the forfeit is added in `TeamStatsSql` only |
| The side is ours | the side's team id is set (the team belongs to the club) | A free-text or other-club opponent side has no team row of ours and never gets a team result |
| Innings are regular | `match_innings.kind = 'REGULAR'` | Decision 12 |

`match.active` is not a rule (decision 4). Neither is the scorecard detail level: a summary-level result counts in full.

**The team's own innings** are the regular innings with `batting_side = side`; **the opposition's innings** are those with `batting_side <> side` in the same result. Opposition totals therefore come from the opposing innings of the same result and do not need the opponent to be a team of ours (a free-text opponent, or a team of another club, works the same).

**Team result** (derived in the calculator from stored columns, as `103`'s "Record while playing"):

| Team result | When |
|---|---|
| Won | `outcome_type = WON` and `winning_side = side` (regular, DLS or super-over decision alike), **or FORFEIT and `winning_side = side`** (the receiver) |
| Lost | `outcome_type = WON` and `winning_side <> side`, **or FORFEIT and `winning_side <> side`** (the conceder) |
| Tied | `outcome_type = TIE` |
| Drawn | `outcome_type = DRAW` |
| No result | `outcome_type` NO_RESULT or ABANDONED, with a ball bowled (the played rule) |

A no-result or abandoned match with **no ball bowled** is not a counted result (decision 3); the page discloses it (see "Basis" below). A **forfeit** is counted in the record only (decision 15): it has no innings, so it feeds no run, wicket, extras or Net Run Rate figure.

**Matches Played** = Won + Lost + Tied + Drawn + No result (forfeit wins and losses are inside Won and Lost). **Decisive results** = Won + Lost (open question 7).

## Precise definitions

All figures are computed once, in `TeamStatsCalculator`, from additive per-team counters (see "SQL approach"). Rounding is `RoundingMode.HALF_UP`, applied **once at the end**; intermediate overs keep at least 10 decimal places. A figure with no valid denominator is `null` in the API and a dash in the UI, **never infinity, never 0, never NaN**. An innings is **usable for runs figures** when `runs`, `wickets` and `balls` are all non-null (a draft allows blanks, `100`; a published result may still lack an innings, for example a win with one innings entered, which `100` warns about and allows); an innings that is not usable is left out of every figure it would feed and counted in `coverage`, never treated as 0.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Runs Scored / Wickets Lost** | Σ `runs`, Σ `wickets` over the team's own usable innings. Wickets Lost is the stored `wickets` of the innings, so an all-out innings with fewer than ten wickets (a short side) counts the wickets that fell |
| **Team Batting Average** | Runs Scored / Wickets Lost over the same innings, 2 decimals. No wickets lost: dash (never ranked 1 on a table; see the unranked group) |
| **Average Runs per Innings** | Runs Scored / number of own usable innings, 2 decimals |
| **Team Run Rate** | Σ runs / Σ (balls / `balls_per_over` of that match) over own usable innings with `balls > 0`, 2 decimals. An all-out innings counts its **actual** balls here (this is the plain run rate, not net run rate) |
| **Highest Team Total** | The largest own `runs` over usable innings of results whose outcome is WON, TIE or DRAW. Shown "212/6" with the match line. On equal totals the more recent match is shown. No-result and abandoned matches never set a high or a low |
| **Lowest Team Total** | The smallest own `runs` over **complete innings** of results whose outcome is WON, TIE or DRAW. An innings is **complete** when it ended all out (`all_out`), **or** it used its full quota (`balls >= scheduled_balls`, with `scheduled_balls` known; a chase that finishes **exactly on the last ball** is therefore complete, decision 25). An innings is **not** complete, and so never the lowest total, when it was cut short by a successful chase (the team won batting second and was not all out), by DLS (a `decided_by = DLS` result counts only when the innings was all out), by rain or by a declaration, or when the quota is unknown and the team was not all out. The figure reports how many innings were classifiable (`coverage`). Reason: "Lowest total" must mean a team that batted its innings out and scored little, not a team that won in 12 overs (open question 17) |
| **Highest Successful Chase** | Among results with `outcome_type = WON`, `winning_side` the team, `decided_by = REGULAR`, where the team's innings is the **later** of two regular innings (it batted second) and both innings are usable: the largest of the team's runs in that innings (the score reached when the target was passed), shown with the target (the opposition runs + 1) and the match line. DLS, super-over and tied results never qualify (a revised target is not comparable, and a tie has no successful chase) |
| **Largest Victory by Runs** | Among results with `outcome_type = WON`, the team the winner, `decided_by = REGULAR`, `margin_type = RUNS`: the largest `margin_value`. Ties broken by the more recent match. DLS and super-over wins are excluded (no comparable margin). The margin is the stored one, never recomputed from the scores (`100` keeps it as entered and warns on a mismatch) |
| **Largest Victory by Wickets** | The same with `margin_type = WICKETS`: the largest `margin_value` (10 is the most); ties broken by the **most balls remaining** (the chase innings against `scheduled_balls`, when both are known), then the more recent match |
| **Total Wickets Taken** | Σ `wickets` over the opposition's usable innings, **every dismissal including run-outs** (it is the team's wickets, not bowler-credited). It can exceed the sum of the team's bowlers' `wickets` by the innings' `non_bowler_wickets` (`101`); the two figures answer different questions, and the Bowling tab of `103` is unchanged |
| **Team Bowling Average** | Opposition runs / Total Wickets Taken, 2 decimals. No wickets: dash |
| **Team Economy Rate** | Opposition runs / Σ (opposition balls / `balls_per_over`), 2 decimals; extras are included (they are runs the opposition scored). No balls: dash |
| **Extras Conceded** | Σ `extras` over the opposition's innings that recorded it (byes, leg byes and penalty runs included: they are runs the team conceded even though no bowler is charged, `101`). `coverage` = innings with extras recorded out of opposition innings counted |
| **Average Extras per match** | Extras Conceded / number of opposition innings with extras recorded, 2 decimals (one opposition innings per match in limited overs) |
| **Extras breakdown** | Σ of each of `wides`, `no_balls`, `byes`, `leg_byes`, `penalty_runs` over the opposition innings that recorded that component, each with its own coverage |
| **Win Percentage** | Wins / (Wins + Losses) x 100, 1 decimal. Ties, draws and no results are out of the denominator (open question 7). No decisive result: dash |
| **Net Run Rate** | See below |

**Basis (disclosure counters).** Besides the counted results, the read reports what it left out so a played total never silently disagrees with the fixture list: `resultsCounted`, `internalResults` (derbies, counted once per team), `resultsNoBallBowled` (NO_RESULT or ABANDONED with no ball bowled), `forfeits` (split into received and conceded; **counted** in the record and in `resultsCounted`, decision 15). Only `resultsNoBallBowled` is a result that is not counted; these are counts only. `resultsCounted` includes forfeits, so for Net Run Rate `matchesCounted` is `resultsCounted` minus forfeits (decision 24).

### Net Run Rate (competition defined: a proposal, flagged as the owner's decision)

Net Run Rate is not one formula: leagues differ on whether an all-out innings counts as the full quota of overs, how DLS matches are treated, and which results enter. The simplest defensible rule, proposed as the default (open question 5):

- **Formula.** NRR = (Σ own runs / Σ own overs faced) minus (Σ opposition runs / Σ opposition overs faced), overs as balls / `balls_per_over` of that match, **2 decimals**, signed. An innings that ended **all out counts at the full scheduled quota** (`scheduled_balls`); any other innings counts the balls actually faced (so a successful chase counts the overs it used).
- **Results that enter.** WON (decided by REGULAR or by SUPER_OVER, using the regular innings only) and TIE, where the match has a league, there is **exactly one regular innings each way**, both are usable and, for any all-out innings, `scheduled_balls` is known. **Excluded:** DLS-decided results (the revised target and overs are not stored in a form this rule can use consistently), DRAW, NO_RESULT, ABANDONED, FORFEIT, a result missing an innings, and friendlies (no league). The figure reports `matchesUsed` out of `matchesCounted` (the counted results minus forfeits) so the exclusions are visible.
- **Where it is shown.** Only when the team's usable results all belong to **one league in one season** (`league_id` and `season_id` each have a single value across them); otherwise the status is `NOT_ONE_COMPETITION` and the cell is a dash with the hint "Choose one league and season to see Net Run Rate". With no usable result the status is `NO_USABLE_RESULTS`. An NRR is never computed across competitions.
- **Rule label (superseded by decision 22).** The response carries `rule: "FULL_QUOTA"` for a later per-league rule, but the UI does **not** print the rule's name; the team page card keeps a short note and "N of M results, one league in one season", and the column help reads "Shown for one league in one season".
- **Not in v1:** a per-league rule (full quota or actual overs), DLS-aware NRR, and points tables (open question 5).

## API Contract

Base `/api/v1/manage/clubs/{clubId}`; `@PreAuthorize("@access.canAccessClub(authentication, #clubId)")` (any club manager), as the other statistics routes. `TeamStatsController`; `TeamStatsService` interface plus `TeamStatsServiceImpl` (`@Transactional(readOnly = true)`, so the lazy-field rule is met from the start); DTOs only. Tenancy, 404s, the 403 outside a section-scoped manager's reach and section narrowing are `StatsScopeResolver`'s, **called, not copied**.

| Endpoint | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /team-stats?seasonId=&leagueId=&sectionId=&competition=&sort=&direction=&minimum=&page=&size=` | club manager (`canAccessClub`), section-narrowed in the service | The team table: one row per team with a counted result in scope, ranked by the sort column |
| `GET /teams/{teamId}/stats?seasonId=&leagueId=&competition=` | club manager (`canAccessClub`); a team outside a section-scoped manager's reach is 403 | One team's full statistics, same definitions and filters |
| `GET /team-stats/filter-options?teamId=` | club manager | The `{ seasonIds, leagueIds, sectionIds, competitions }` that have counted team results (no `teamIds`: the table lists teams); the optional `teamId` narrows them to that team's own options (decision 27); a section-scoped manager is narrowed to options inside their sections |

**Filters and errors (as `103`):** every parameter optional and combinable; an unknown or other-club season, league, section or team is 404; `competition` other than `LEAGUE` / `FRIENDLY` is 400; a `sectionId` or `teamId` outside the caller's reach is 403; a section-scoped manager is otherwise narrowed to the teams of their accessible sections (intersected with the Section filter, descendants included). A Section filter chooses **which teams are listed**; it never changes a team's own figures (so a team's table row equals its page, which takes no `sectionId`). A scope with nothing in reach is an empty table (200). A team with no counted result returns 200 with zero counters and `basis.resultsCounted = 0` (not 404), so the empty state is a normal read.

**Table rules:** `sort` is a lower-case column code (below), unknown is 400, default `won`; `direction` is `asc` or `desc` (absent means the column's natural direction; anything else 400); `minimum` is a non-negative integer, **Minimum matches**, applying to the rate sorts only (absent means none; negative or non-numeric is 400; ignored on a count sort); `page` starts at 0, `size` defaults to **25** and is at most 100 (below 1 or above 100, a negative `page`: 400, never clamped). Ranking is bounded server-side over one aggregate row per team (a club has tens of teams; refused with a 400 and logged above 1,000), the same documented exception as the leaderboards (`backend.md`, "Statistics (spec 103)"); the browser never receives more than the page.

### The table (`TeamStatsTableDto`)

`columns[]` `{code, label, longLabel, sortable, direction, minimumKind, help}` in display order (the client hard-codes no column), `sort {code, direction}`, `scope` (`kind: CLUB_VERIFIED`, `seasonId`, `leagueId`, `sectionId`, `competition`), `minimum {applies, value, unit: MATCHES}`, `basis {resultsCounted, internalResults, resultsNoBallBowled, forfeits, teamsListed, teamsRanked, belowMinimum}`, `page {number, size, totalElements, hasMore}`, and `rows[]` `{rank (null for the unranked group), teamId, teamName, sectionName, active, values}` where `values` is a map keyed by column code to a cell `{value, ...}` (a plain number or null; highest total carries `runs` and `wickets`; net run rate carries `status`, `rule`, `matchesUsed`). Every value comes from `TeamStatsCalculator`. `basis` is its own query counting distinct results across the scope (a derby once); the response has no `notCaptured` (the table's partnerships line is static text, decision 26).

| Code | Column | Natural direction | Rate (minimum applies) | Unranked when | Order after the value (tie-breaks, in order) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `played` | P | higher | no | never | wins desc; name |
| `won` | W | higher | no | never | win percentage desc; fewer played; name |
| `lost` | L | lower | no | never | fewer played; name |
| `tied` | T | higher | no | never | wins desc; name |
| `drawn` | D | higher | no | never | wins desc; name |
| `noResult` | NR | higher | no | never | wins desc; name |
| `winPercent` | Win % | higher | yes | no decisive result | wins desc; more played; name |
| `runs` | Runs | higher | no | no usable innings | runs per innings desc; fewer played; name |
| `battingAverage` | Avg | higher | yes | no wickets lost | runs desc; more played; name |
| `runRate` | RR | higher | yes | no balls recorded | runs desc; more played; name |
| `highTotal` | High | higher | no | no innings | more recent match; name |
| `wicketsTaken` | Wkts | higher | no | no usable opposition innings | fewer runs conceded; name |
| `bowlingAverage` | Bowl avg | lower | yes | no wickets taken | wickets taken desc; more played; name |

| `netRunRate` | NRR | higher | yes | status is not `SHOWN` | wins desc; more played; name |

Equal values with equal tie-break criteria share a rank; the order within equal ranks is by name, then id, so paging is stable. The default sort is Wins (a counting column, so a one-match team with 100% never tops the table). The detail-only figures (wickets lost, runs per innings, lowest total, highest chase, largest victories, extras) are on the team page and are not sort columns (open question 2).

### SQL approach

A new `TeamStatsSql` (package-private, in `repository`, beside `PlayerStatsSql`), `TeamStatsAggregateRepository` and `TeamStatsFactsRepository`, native SQL, the second and third permitted readers of the result tables under `backend.md`'s sentence (gated by the same published constant, touched only by the statistics services, added to the existing ArchUnit rule in `LayeringRulesTest`).

1. **Shared counting constants (a refactor with no behaviour change).** The published predicate and the "played" predicate currently live inline in `PlayerStatsSql.APPEARANCES`. They are extracted into one package-private holder (for example `StatsCountingSql`: `PUBLISHED`, `PLAYED_RESULT`) used by both `PlayerStatsSql` and `TeamStatsSql`, so a change to what counts changes players and teams together. Proven by the existing `PlayerStatsSqlLiteralsTest` and the parity tests of `103` and `115`, unchanged and passing.
2. **`TEAM_RESULTS`**, the shared fragment (CTE `tr`): one row per counted team result: `mrid`, `match_id`, `match_date`, `side`, `team_id`, the team's `section_id`, `outcome`, `winning_side`, `decided_by`, `margin_type`, `margin_value`, `bpo`, `scheduled_balls`, `league_id`, `season_id`, `derby`. It is built from `match_result` and `match`, and from two rows per match (`HOME` where `home_team_id` is set, `AWAY` where `away_team_id` is set; a union or a lateral values list), joined to `team` for the club check and the section. Filters: `seasonId`, `leagueId`, `competition` as `103`; **Team** = the row's team; **Section** and section narrowing = `team.section_id in (:sectionTeamIds)` style, using `StatsScopeResolver.sectionTeamParameter` unchanged. It takes the same named parameters as `103` (with `teamId` and the section list), no `playerId`.
3. **Additive counters per team** (never derived figures), in separate CTEs over `tr` joined to `match_innings` so rows are never multiplied: `rec` (played, won, lost, tied, drawn, noResult by `filter` on `outcome` and `winning_side` against `side`; internal; forfeits are inside `tr` and counted in the record by `winning_side` (decision 15, gated on the outcome, never on innings); a sibling CTE over the no-ball-bowled no-results counts them only to disclose them), `own` (innings where `batting_side = tr.side`: usable-innings count, Σ runs, Σ wickets, Σ balls, Σ balls/bpo, and the encoded maxima/minima below), `opp` (innings where `batting_side <> tr.side`: usable-innings count, Σ runs, Σ wickets, Σ balls, Σ balls/bpo, Σ extras with the count that recorded it, and the five components each with their count), `nrr` (over the NRR-usable results only: Σ own runs, Σ opposition runs, Σ own overs faced and Σ opposition overs faced with an all-out innings at `scheduled_balls`, the usable-match count, and `count(distinct league_id)` and `count(distinct season_id)` among them). **Encoded maxima, as `103` does for highest score:** highest total `runs` key (with wickets and recency), lowest total over complete innings, highest chase, largest victory by runs and by wickets (each with `match_date` in the key, so a plain `max` or `min` picks the winner and the tie-break). SQL only selects stored columns and applies the shared predicates; every classification beyond "stored column against stored column" (complete innings, chase qualification, NRR usability) is defined **once**, as a named SQL predicate constant in `TeamStatsSql`, mirrored by the calculator's unit-tested definition, and the parity test pins them together.
4. **`TeamStatsCalculator`** (pure, `service.support`, no Spring, no repository, no entity, no caller or access information) turns one team's counters into every derived figure, the coverage and the `notCaptured` catalogue. Nothing else computes a figure.
5. **`TeamTableRanker`** (pure, `service.support`) ranks the calculator's output for every team: ordering and tie-breaks of the table above, competition ranking, the unranked group, the optional minimum, paging. It follows `LeaderboardTableRanker`'s rules; the plan decided whether the generic parts (competition ranking, paging slice, comparators) are extracted into a shared helper or the new class stands alone; **as built it stands alone** (decision 31).
6. **Facts for one team** (the page only, a few small indexed queries by `team_id` in scope): the last five results with opponent names, and the match references for the highs, lows, chase and victories (the values come from the counters; the facts only say which match). The opponent name is resolved as `115` does.
7. **Parity.** The table and the page read the same counters through the same calculator; a parity test asserts every table cell equals the same team's page figure for the same filters.
8. **Statement count.** Fixed per request and pinned by a query-count test (no N+1): table = scope lookups + aggregate + team names/sections; page = scope lookups + aggregate + facts.

**Indexes and proof.** No new index is expected. The queries start from `ix_match_result_club_status`, the unique `match_result(match_id)`, the match filters (`ix_match_season`, `ix_match_league`, `ix_match_home_team`, `ix_match_away_team`), reach innings through `ux_match_innings_sequence (match_result_id, sequence)` and teams through `ix_team_section` (all listed in `103`'s Rollout Notes). Volume is small: a club has tens of teams and a season is hundreds of results, all-time thousands, each with two to four innings rows. The plan runs `EXPLAIN` on a seeded club (all-time, no filters, the heaviest request; one season; one section with descendants), records the plan and timings in the PR, and proposes a budget of 300 ms p95 for the table and 150 ms for one team page. A new index or migration only if that shows a problem (candidate: `match_innings(match_result_id, kind)`, not verified to be needed).

an `EXPLAIN` of the heaviest request recorded in the PR; the fixed statement count per request.

**Parity test (the key one), `TeamStatsParityIntegrationTest`,** in the style of `LeaderboardParityIntegrationTest`: for every team in the seeded club and a set of filter combinations (none, each of Season, League, Competition, and a combined one), every cell of the table equals the same team's `TeamStatsDto` figure, including a partial-data team, a derby team and a team whose NRR is not shown. A change to a counting rule or to a definition that breaks parity fails this test. A second check sums the table's `played`, `won`, `lost` over the scope and equals the distinct result counts the seed was built with (internal results counting twice).

**Controller (`TeamStatsControllerIntegrationTest`):** `canAccessClub` required (403 otherwise) on every route; another club's team, season, league or section is 404; a bad `competition`, `sort`, `direction`, `minimum`, `page` or `size` is 400; a section-scoped manager is narrowed and gets 403 for a `sectionId` or `teamId` outside their reach; a team with no result returns 200 with zero counters; only a draft existing changes nothing.

**Structural (ArchUnit):** the team stats repositories are touched only by the statistics services (`LayeringRulesTest` updated and proven non-vacuous by a deliberate break once); `TeamStatsCalculator` and `TeamTableRanker` depend on no Spring, repository or entity class and take no caller or access information (added to the existing `103` rule); the new service touches the stats repositories and not `MatchResultRepository`; DTOs only cross the controller.

**Contract:** OpenAPI diff, additions only (three routes and their DTOs).

**Frontend component (Vitest + Testing Library):** the Teams tab (fifth tab, bound to `?tab=teams`; filters sent as parameters; Team select hidden and Competition select shown; rows in the order received; the sort column highlighted; a second tap on a heading reverses; the unranked group renders last with dashes; equal ranks render as equal numbers; the Minimum matches field shows only on a rate sort and refetches; the NRR dash carries its reason; the basis line states the excluded results; Show more appends and never slices; the row link carries Season, League and Competition; the empty, filtered-empty and error states; table roles and `aria-sort`); the team page (tiles, the form strip with text not colour alone, each card, coverage captions, the three NRR states, the Not captured yet panel from `notCaptured`, the back link to the Teams tab with its filters, the empty states); the `TeamDetailPage` Statistics card (a real button to the page); a formatting helper test for the signed NRR and win percentage.

**End-to-end (Playwright, mobile and desktop), one golden path:** a club with published results across two teams and two seasons: open Leaderboards, choose the Teams tab, see the current season's table sorted by Wins, change the sort and see it reverse, change the Season, open a team, see the record, the form strip and the highlights, press Back and land on the same filtered table, then open the same team from its Team detail page's Statistics card. (Playwright has not been run on `master` since `104`; the roadmap notes it. This test must be runnable before merge.)

## Acceptance Criteria

- The Leaderboards page has a fifth tab **Teams** at `?tab=teams`, listing every team of the club with a counted result in scope, one row each, with the fixed columns P, W, L, T, D, NR, Win %, Runs, Avg, RR, High, Wkts, Bowl avg, Econ, NRR, sortable by any of them, a second click reversing, ranked with equal ranks for ties and the teams with no defined figure for a rate sort last and unranked.
- A team page at `/manage/teams/:teamId/stats` shows the record, the last five results, runs, wickets lost, batting average, runs per innings, run rate, highest and lowest total, highest successful chase, largest victories by runs and by wickets, wickets taken, bowling average, economy, extras conceded, average extras and net run rate, each with its coverage, and a **Statistics** card with an outlined button on the Team detail page, and a **Stats** shortcut on each Teams card and row (decision 21), open it.
- Only **published** results of this club count; a draft, an unpublished result, and a no-result or abandoned match with no ball bowled never contribute (integration tested), and the basis line says how many of the last were left out; a forfeit counts in the record (a win for the receiver, a loss for the conceder) and nowhere else, and the basis line says how many.
- A result with a ball bowled that is NO_RESULT or ABANDONED counts as a match played and a no result and feeds the totals, but never a high, a low or net run rate.
- A **derby** gives each of its two teams its own result, one win and one loss (or both a tie), counted once per team.
- **Summary-level results count** for the record and every figure their innings support; extras and their breakdown, nullable there, are computed only over the innings that recorded them and a coverage caption says how many; a missing figure is never treated as 0.
- The **lowest total** is taken only over complete innings (all out or the full quota) and never from a successful chase, a DLS result that was not all out, or a shortened match; the **highest successful chase** counts only regular wins batting second; **largest victory** uses the stored margin and excludes DLS and super-over wins (each a unit test).
- **Net run rate** follows the stated full-quota rule, is computed only over WON and TIE results with usable innings, is shown only for one league in one season to 2 decimals with a short note and "N of M results" (the rule's name is not printed), and shows the reason when it is not shown; it is never computed across competitions.
- **Partnerships (runs by wicket)** are not shown as a number; they are listed under **Not captured yet**, in one panel, driven by one catalogue in the calculator. **Results capture is unchanged.**
- All figures come from `TeamStatsCalculator`; the client only formats. Every definition in "Precise definitions" has a unit test.
- **A table figure always equals the same team's page figure** for the same filters (parity tested). A Section filter chooses which teams are listed and never changes a team's own figures.
- The filters (Season, League, Section with its descendants, Competition) are backend parameters, offer only values with counted data, and combine; a section-scoped manager sees only their sections' teams and gets 403 for a section or team outside them; another club's team, season, league or section is 404.
- Paging is backend-driven (25 by default, Show more fetches the next page, maximum 100, nothing sliced in the browser); the optional **Minimum matches** (default none) applies to rate sorts only.
- The published and played predicates exist **once** (the shared constant), used by `103`, `115` and `116`; `103`'s and `115`'s tests pass unchanged.
- Manager-only: guarded by `canAccessClub`, DTOs only, `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`, no response names a person, and the calculator and ranker carry no access information.
- No migration, no table, no new dependency, no new menu item or icon; the OpenAPI change is additive.
- `backend.md`, `frontend.md`, `docs/roadmap.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and the pointer in `103` are updated in the build PR (see Rollout Notes).
- Partnerships, a player-facing view, league standings and points, cross-club tables, a per-league net run rate rule, home and away splits, head-to-head, export and any change to results capture are **not** present.

## Open questions for the product owner

Each is a proposal with a recommended default; the owner answers after seeing the mockups, and any answer can still change them.

1. **Where it lives.** Recommended: **one primary home and one link.** The primary home is a **Teams tab on the Leaderboards page** (it reuses the filters, the Sort by, the standings table, the persistence, the menu item and the icon, so there is nothing new to learn), with the team page behind a row. The one link is a **Statistics card with an outlined button on each Team detail page**. Alternatives: (a) a new "Team statistics" page with its own menu entry and brand icon (clearer to find, more to build and a second place to maintain filters); (b) only a Statistics card on each Team detail page (no club-wide comparison, which is the question the committee asks); (c) all three (rejected: three entry points drift).
2. **Table columns, and which statistics are headline versus detail.** Recommended table columns: **P, W, L, T, D, NR, Win %** (record), **Runs, Avg, RR, High** (batting), **Wkts, Bowl avg, Econ** (bowling) and **NRR**. Everything else (wickets lost, runs per innings, lowest total, highest successful chase, largest victories, extras and their breakdown, forfeits) is **team-page detail** and not a sort column, because 15 columns is already wide and the detail figures are one-team questions. **Draws** are added to the owner's list because without them the record does not add up to Matches Played; limited-overs draws are rare and the column can be dropped if you prefer. Alternative: a smaller table (P, W, L, T, NR, Win %, RR, NRR) and everything else on the page.
3. **The per-team page versus only a table.** Recommended: **build the page**, with the headline tiles (Played, Won, Lost, Win %), the last-five form strip, and cards for Record, Batting, Results highlights, Bowling, Extras and Net run rate. The highs, lows, chase and biggest wins are one-team stories that do not fit a table. Alternative: a table only (cheaper, loses the highlights and the match links).
4. **How a derby is shown.** Recommended: **each team gets its own result, one win and one loss**, as `100` decided, with the opponent shown as the other club team's name and a muted "internal" marker in the form strip, and the basis line saying how many internal matches are counted once for each team. No club-wide total row is shown (it would double count). Alternative: show derbies separately and leave them out of the record (rejected: contradicts `100`'s per-team rule and leaves a team's fixture list incomplete).
5. **Net Run Rate approach.** Recommended (the simplest defensible): the **full-quota rule** (an all-out innings counts at the full scheduled overs; other innings at the overs used), over WON (regular or super-over) and TIE results with both innings usable; **DLS, draws, no results, forfeits and friendlies excluded**; **shown only for one league in one season, with `matchesUsed` of `matchesCounted`** (answered: decision 13; the rule's name is not printed, decision 22); never across competitions. Alternatives: (a) a **per-league rule setting** (full quota or actual overs, DLS-aware) stored on the league season's playing conditions (a migration and a settings control, and the right answer if your leagues disagree), deferred; (b) **defer NRR entirely** and show only the plain Run Rate; (c) compute it on actual overs only (simpler, but wrong for the competitions that use the full quota). Please tell me which rule your leagues use; it is the one number here that is not a fact. A related sub-question: should the one-competition test be **per team** (recommended: each team's NRR is over its own single league, so the default Season view works for a club where each team plays one league) or **for the whole table** (simpler, but hides NRR for everyone as soon as two leagues are in scope)?
6. **Opposition of another club and free-text opponents.** Recommended: no special treatment. The opposition's figures come from the **opposing innings in the same result**, which exist for every published result whatever the opponent, so a team's bowling, extras and net run rate work against a free-text opponent exactly as against a team of another club. The opponent's name appears only on a match line (form strip, highlights).
7. **Win Percentage and "decisive results".** Recommended: **Wins / (Wins + Losses) x 100**, with ties, draws and no results out of the denominator (the owner's "decisive results"), 1 decimal, a dash when there are no decisive results. Alternatives: count a tie as half a win; or divide by Matches Played (so a tie or a washout lowers it).
8. **What a "No Result" is.** Recommended, consistent with `103`: a NO_RESULT or ABANDONED match counts only if **a ball was bowled**; a washed-out match with no ball bowled counts for nothing and is reported in the basis line ("1 result with no ball bowled is not counted"). This is a real product question: many leagues call a washed-out fixture a no result and award points for it, so your No Results column may then look short against your fixture list. If you want those counted, the alternative is to count every published NO_RESULT or ABANDONED as a no result and a match played, with the totals still using only matches with play, at the cost of differing from `103`'s player Matches count for the same fixture. Say which you prefer. *(Answered: decision 14, not counted.)*
9. **Forfeits.** *(Answered: the alternative, decision 15; the text below is the original proposal.)* Recommended: a forfeit **is not a played match** and is excluded from the record and every figure (no innings exist, and `103` excludes it), but it is **reported**, as forfeits received and conceded, on the team page and in the basis line. Alternative: count a forfeit as a win for the receiving team and a loss for the conceding one in the record (many league tables do), with no figures.
10. **Wins decided by DLS or a super over.** Recommended: both are **wins and losses** in the record and Win Percentage (the result stands), their innings feed the plain totals and averages, and they are **excluded from the comparisons that need a like-for-like margin**: largest victory, highest successful chase, lowest total (unless all out) and net run rate. A tie settled by a super over is a win for the winner (`100`), not a tie. `105` decides whether super-over runs ever count; until then they never feed a figure.
11. **Home and away split.** Recommended: **not in this spec.** `HOME` and `AWAY` on a match are the fixture's listing, not a ground, and are arbitrary for friendlies and derbies. Revisit with a real ground concept.
12. **Minimum matches for a ranking.** Recommended: an **optional "Minimum matches" field, default none**, on the rate sorts only (Win %, Avg, RR, Bowl avg, Econ, NRR), exactly as `103`'s standings, so a team with two matches is shown, not hidden. No Small-sample chip (the P column is in the row). Alternative: a default minimum, or the chip as on the player tables.
13. **Season and competition defaults.** Recommended: Season defaults to the current season when it has counted data, otherwise All seasons (as `103` decision 29); the Competition select defaults to **All competitions** (friendlies included in the record and the totals; the League and Season filters narrow it). Alternative: default to League matches only, with friendlies a deliberate extra, if the committee thinks of "team stats" as league stats.
14. **Section-scoped managers.** Recommended: narrowed to their sections' teams on the table, and a 403 on a team page outside their reach, as `103`. A team's figures are never narrowed (a team's record is the same whoever reads it).
15. **League matches only, or friendlies too.** See 13; the records for a team always include whatever the Competition filter admits. Friendlies never enter net run rate.
16. **Phone layout of a wide table.** Recommended: the table scrolls sideways with the **Team column pinned** and the Sort by select above it, as the standings do, because the point of this table is to compare teams across columns. Alternative: a stacked card per team (Played, W-L-T, Win %, then a few lines), easier to read one team at a time but not comparable across teams.
17. **The Lowest Team Total rule.** Recommended: only **complete innings** (all out, or the full scheduled quota) of results with an outcome count, never a chase that ended early, a DLS result that was not all out, or a rain-shortened match; the page says how many innings were classifiable. Alternative: the plain minimum of every innings (simplest, but it would crown a 40-over team that won in 8 overs as the "lowest total").
18. **Highest Successful Chase and Largest Victory definitions.** Recommended: the chase figure is the **score the chasing team reached** (shown with the target), regular wins batting second only; the largest victory uses the **stored margin** (runs or wickets), ties broken by recency, and by balls remaining for wickets. Alternative: rank chases by the target rather than the score reached (the two differ by a few runs).
19. **Extras Conceded and Average Extras.** Recommended: the opposition's innings **extras** (so byes, leg byes and penalty runs count even though no bowler is charged), average per opposition innings that recorded extras, with a coverage note because summary-level results leave them blank. Alternative: only wides and no-balls (the bowlers' extras), which the breakdown already allows.
20. **Total Wickets Taken.** Recommended: **all dismissals of the opposition's innings, run-outs included** (a team figure), which can exceed the sum of bowlers' wickets in `103`. Alternative: bowler-credited wickets only (matches the Bowling tab, but needs a scorecard and so covers fewer matches).
21. **Page size and totals.** Recommended: page size 25 (a club has tens of teams, so most clubs see one page), and **no totals row** across teams. Alternative: 10 per page as the player tables, or a summary strip above the table (Teams, Results counted).
22. **Teams with no counted result.** Recommended: **not listed** (as `103` lists only players with an appearance), so an inactive or unused team does not clutter the table; they appear again once a result counts. Alternative: list every active team with zeros and dashes.
23. **Player or public view later.** Recommended: nothing now. Team figures name no person, so the later access rule is only which teams a viewer may see, and a public team page could reuse the service. Confirm that is the direction.
24. **The Not captured yet wording for partnerships.** Recommended: "Partnerships (runs by wicket): the scorecard does not record the fall of wickets", in the one panel at the foot of the team page and a quiet line under the Teams tab, as `103` does for its missing figures.
